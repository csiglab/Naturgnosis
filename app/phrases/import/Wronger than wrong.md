# Wronger than wrong

: 18

> Wronger than wrong: Wronger than wrong is a statement that equates two errors when one of the errors is clearly more wrong than the other. “Example: When people thought the Earth was flat, they were wrong. When people thought the Earth was spherical, they were wrong. But if you think that thinking the Earth is spherical is just as wrong as thinking the Earth is flat, then your view is wronger than both of them put together.”
