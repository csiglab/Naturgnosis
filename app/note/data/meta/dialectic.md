# Dialectic

> **Dialectic** — reasoning through opposition: a method that advances inquiry by drawing out the contradictions latent in a position, confronting it with its negation, and resolving the tension at a higher level of determination. It can be reframed, in a less charged and domain-neutral way, as **antagonistic interaction**: X and Y exert opposing influences.

## Formulation

> What is dialectic? Is it ontic, epistemic, or agentic?
> Why propose a neutral term, and how do the two relate?

Dialectic is **agentic and epistemic in its minimal form, and ontic only in its strong form**: agentic as a method a reasoner performs, epistemic in its aim of exposing one-sidedness, and ontic only in the Hegelian reading where the movement of concepts is held to be the movement of being itself. Its defensible core is methodological — a movement through negation (*Aufhebung*) in which an opposition is neither ignored nor left standing, but *sublated* into a more determinate unity.

## Antagonistic interaction

**Antagonistic interaction** names the general pattern: a relation in which two elements, **X** and **Y**, exert *opposing influences* on a shared variable or outcome. For a state variable *s*, X tends to raise it and Y to lower it (or vice versa); the outcome is the resultant, which may be stable, oscillatory, or resolved toward one side.

The term is deliberately **neutral, formal, and domain-general**: it describes the oppositional structure alone, without the teleology, the metaphysical commitment, or the historical charge carried by "dialectic".

## Dialectic as a loaded subset

Dialectic names a **subset** of antagonistic interactions — historically those in which the opposition is held to be *productive*, driving a movement toward a higher unity. Antagonistic interaction keeps the opposition and drops the rest:

| | Antagonistic interaction | Dialectic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opposition | yes | yes |
| Productive / progressive | no commitment | often asserted |
| Movement to a higher unity (*Aufhebung*) | no commitment | central in Hegel |
| Ontological claim (being unfolds dialectically) | none | in strong readings |
| Domain | any | historically philosophical |

See the [Dialectic](/glossarium/?t=dialectic) term for the historical and semantic evolution of the loaded term.

## Why a neutral name

It avoids (a) the Hegelian baggage of necessary synthesis, (b) the Marxist charge of class antagonism, and (c) the conflation of contradiction (a logical relation between propositions) with opposition (a relation between influences). Antagonistic interaction **describes; it does not prescribe**.

## Examples

| Domain | X / Y | Opposed influence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physics | two forces | opposing vectors acting on one body |
| Biology | predator / prey | opposing effects on population size |
| Economics | supply / demand | opposing pressure on price |
| Logic | p / ¬p | mutually exclusive truth values |
| Social | reform / reaction | opposing pressure on a policy outcome |
