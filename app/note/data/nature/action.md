# Action

> An **action in physics** refers to a quantity that describes the motion of a system. It is a fundamental concept in classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, and field theory, particularly within the **principle of least action**.
>

The action, **S**, is typically defined as the integral of the **Lagrangian** (L) of a system over time:

$S = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} L \, dt$

where:

- $L = T - V$, the Lagrangian, is the difference between the kinetic energy $(T)$ and potential energy $(V)$ of the system.
- $t_1$ and  $t_2$  are the initial and final times.

In the principle of least action, the path taken by a system between two states is the one for which the action is minimized (or stationary). This leads to the **Euler-Lagrange equations**, which describe the system's dynamics.
