# Natural Material

> Natural material read as the totality of untransformed matter: every observer-independent
> substance that no purposeful agent has intentionally worked — minerals, rocks, sediments,
> soils, waters, air, and every product of life from metabolite to peat bed. The first cut
> below intention: what remains when all deliberately transformed matter (the technical
> reading) is set aside. Parent instance [Nature](note.html?n=nature/nature.md); children
> [Natural Biogenic](note.html?n=nature/biogenic-material.md) and
> [Natural Abiogenic](note.html?n=nature/earth-material.md). Worked per
> [How to decompose any natural instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).
> Presupposed definitions (`Reality`, segments, levels of organization, the ontology/epistemology
> dual) live in the epistemicarum guide and are restated nowhere here.

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Substance / Matter` at macro level of organization: the most abstract stuff-kind — all
matter individuated by not having been intentionally transformed, recurring across every
crustal, aqueous, atmospheric, and living system. It binds `(root) -> <<Natural Element>>`
as the generic substance all other material notes specialize. Same name at micro scale (a
grain, a molecule) and meso scale (a bed, a seam) denotes distinct nodes — record the level
per instance; full expansion lives in the child notes, not here.

### What is this natural instance?

**Natural material**: any matter as found, grown, secreted, or deposited without purposeful
working — **mineral** kinds, **earth materials** (rock, sediment, soil), **waters**, **air**,
and **biogenic substances** (tissues, secretions, metabolites, organic sediments, biogenic
rocks). The cut includes every untransformed substance at every level of organization; it
excludes all intentionally transformed matter — smelted metal, fired ceramic, sawn lumber,
synthesized polymer — which is the technical reading ([Technical Material](note.html?n=technique/technical-material.md)),
each with its own tree. The biogenic/abiogenic split below is by origin (made by life or
not), never by use or price.

Limitation checklist (ontic properties of the segment, not method failures): Complexity
(mineral-fluid-biota coupling across all matter), Scale (molecule to formation), Partial
Observability (subsurface, deep time), Non-Repeatability (one depositional history each),
Data Availability (sparse deep-time proxies), Many-Body coupling (grains, fluids, biota).
Nonlinearity, Uncertainty, Chaos, Emergence, Irreducibility, Measurement Disturbance, and
Computational Complexity apply only where the concrete body shows them — record per child
instance, do not assume.

## QA

### Why is Natural Material the most abstract natural category?

> It precedes every split: origin-agnostic (biogenic and abiogenic alike), composition-free
> (no chemistry or lattice in the criterion), and level-spanning (molecule to formation).
> Its single individuating mark is negative — *not intentionally transformed* — which is why
> the origin-defined [Natural Biogenic](note.html?n=nature/biogenic-material.md) is the most
> abstract *positive* category beneath it, and why all worked matter falls outside it
> entirely, into the technical reading.
