# pH

> **pH** is a measure of the **acidity** or **alkalinity** of a solution, expressed on a scale from 0 (highly acidic) to 14 (highly alkaline), with 7 being neutral.
>

> The **measurement of pH** works by determining the concentration of **hydrogen ions (H⁺)** in a solution.
>

## Principle of pH Measurement

> pH is defined as the **negative logarithm of the hydrogen ion concentration**:  $pH=−log⁡10[H+]$
>
- A high concentration of H⁺ ions means the solution is **acidic** (pH < 7).
- A low concentration of H⁺ ions means the solution is **alkaline** (pH > 7).
- A neutral solution (pH = 7) has an equal concentration of H⁺ and OH⁻ (hydroxide) ions.
