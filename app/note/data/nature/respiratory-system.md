# Respiratory System

> The **respiratory system** is a network of organs and structures, including the lungs, airways, and diaphragm, that facilitates the exchange of oxygen and carbon dioxide between the body and the environment, supporting cellular respiration and maintaining gas homeostasis.
>

## Processes

> …
>

| **Process** | **Description** | **Function** | **Key Components Involved** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inhalation (Inspiration)** | The process of taking air into the lungs. | Brings oxygen-rich air into the lungs for gas exchange. | Diaphragm contraction, intercostal muscles, expansion of chest cavity |
| **Exhalation (Expiration)** | The process of expelling air from the lungs. | Removes carbon dioxide-rich air from the lungs. | Diaphragm relaxation, intercostal muscles, lung recoil |
| **Gas Exchange** | The exchange of oxygen and carbon dioxide across the alveolar membrane. | Oxygen is absorbed into the blood, and carbon dioxide is expelled. | Alveoli, capillaries, hemoglobin in red blood cells |
| **Pulmonary Ventilation** | The movement of air into and out of the lungs. | Facilitates air exchange between the atmosphere and alveoli. | Diaphragm, intercostal muscles, trachea, bronchi, alveoli |
| **Oxygen Transport** | The process by which oxygen is carried from the lungs to tissues. | Oxygen is transported via hemoglobin in red blood cells. | Red blood cells, hemoglobin, heart, blood vessels |
| **Carbon Dioxide Transport** | The process by which carbon dioxide is carried from tissues to the lungs. | CO₂ is transported in the blood and expelled through exhalation. | Carbonic acid-bicarbonate buffer system, red blood cells, heart |
| **Regulation of Breathing** | The control of the rate and depth of breathing based on the body’s needs. | Maintains homeostasis by adjusting ventilation to CO₂ and O₂ levels. | Medulla oblongata, pons, chemoreceptors, diaphragm |
| **Diffusion in Alveoli** | The movement of gases (oxygen and CO₂) across the alveolar-capillary membrane. | Oxygen diffuses into the blood, while carbon dioxide diffuses out. | Alveolar walls, capillary walls, oxygen and CO₂ gradients |
| **Ventilation-Perfusion Matching** | The balance between airflow (ventilation) and blood flow (perfusion) in the lungs. | Ensures efficient gas exchange by matching ventilation to perfusion. | Alveoli, capillaries, pulmonary circulation |
| **Coughing and Sneezing** | Protective reflexes to clear the respiratory passages. | Clears airways of foreign particles, mucus, or irritants. | Respiratory tract, reflex control centers, diaphragm |
| **Mucociliary Clearance** | The process of removing mucus and trapped particles from the airways. | Protects the respiratory system by removing debris and pathogens. | Cilia, mucus, goblet cells, trachea, bronchi |
| **Alveolar Macrophage Activity** | The immune response in the alveoli where macrophages engulf pathogens. | Protects the lungs by clearing pathogens, dead cells, and debris. | Alveolar macrophages, immune cells |
| **Surfactant Secretion** | The production of surfactant in the lungs. | Reduces surface tension in alveoli, preventing collapse and aiding gas exchange. | Type II alveolar cells, surfactant proteins |
| **Hypoxic Pulmonary Vasoconstriction** | The constriction of pulmonary blood vessels in response to low oxygen levels. | Directs blood flow to areas of the lung with higher oxygen levels. | Pulmonary arterioles, smooth muscle, oxygen sensing receptors |
| **Bohr Effect** | The shift in the oxygen-hemoglobin dissociation curve due to changes in CO₂ and pH. | Enhances oxygen release in tissues where CO₂ levels are high. | Hemoglobin, CO₂, blood pH, tissues |
