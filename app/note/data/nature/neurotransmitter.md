# Neurotransmitter

> A neurotransmitter is a chemical messenger that transmits signals across a synapse from one neuron (nerve cell) to another target cell, which can be another neuron, muscle cell, or gland cell. Neurotransmitters are released from the axon terminal of a neuron in response to an electrical signal and bind to specific receptors on the target cell, triggering a physiological response.
