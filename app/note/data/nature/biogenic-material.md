# Natural Biogenic

> Natural biogenic read as all matter made by or of life forms: constituents, secretions, and
> metabolites of plants and animals, from living tissue through sediment to fossil and
> transformed substances such as petroleum — the observer-independent furniture that remains
> when assays, mines, firms, and prices are set aside. Parent instances
> [Nature](note.html?n=nature/nature.md) and
> [Natural Material](note.html?n=nature/natural-material.md); sibling
> [Natural Abiogenic](note.html?n=nature/earth-material.md); technical counterpart
> [Technical Biogenic Material](note.html?n=technique/technical-biogenic-material.md). Worked per
> [How to decompose any natural instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Substance / Matter` at meso level of organization: a stuff-kind individuated *genetically*
(made by or of life forms) rather than compositionally, recurring across organisms,
sediments, and rocks. Entered via the composition spine (`Natural Object -> Building Block ->
Substance / Matter`): a **shell bed** is the building-block occurrence, the biogenic
substance is the kind. Same name at micro scale (a metabolite molecule) and macro scale (a
reef platform) denotes distinct nodes — record the level per instance.

### What is this natural instance?

**Natural biogenic**: any naturally occurring product of life — **wood**, **grass**,
**bone**, **shell**, **pearl**, **silk**, **peat**, **coal**, **kerogen**,
**petroleum** (transformed biogenic substance), **chalk** and **reef limestone** (biogenic
rocks). The cut includes the substance, its producing organisms, and its depositional and
diagenetic settings from micro to macro scale; it excludes the instruments that identify it
(technical reading), the tenures and markets priced around it (social reading), the worked
objects made from it ([Technical Biogenic Material](note.html?n=technique/technical-biogenic-material.md)),
and the classification standards themselves (epistemic scaffolding) — those are secondary
readings (see below), each with its own tree.

Limitation checklist (ontic properties of the segment, not method failures): Complexity
(organism-fluid-sediment coupling), Nonlinearity (preservation thresholds), Uncertainty
(buried growth and depositional conditions; abiogenic-mimic isoprenoids in Precambrian
samples), Scale (molecule to reef platform), Data Availability (sparse deep-time record),
Partial Observability (in-situ growth and burial environments), Non-Repeatability (one
depositional and diagenetic history), Many-Body coupling (biota, fluids, grains). Chaos,
Emergence, Irreducibility, Measurement Disturbance, and Computational Complexity apply only
where the concrete occurrence shows them — record per instance, do not assume.

Secondary readings (kept as prose, never merged into the tree above): readable as technique —
chromatography and mass-spectrometry identification methods and all workings of biogenic

production role it is a Social node tagged `production-view`); readable as epistemic —
biomarker standards and paleo-ecological calibrations (decompose under the epistemicarum
grammar). The instrument technically, the formation naturally
([ambiguity-resolution](note.html?n=meta/ambiguity-resolution.md)).

## QA

### Why is Natural Biogenic the most abstract origin-defined category?

> No other origin-defined kind abstracts over so much: every producer (microbe to redwood),
> every chemistry (fatty acid to alkaloid to kerogen), every state (living tissue, sediment,
> fossil, migrated fluid), every era (Precambrian biomarkers to contemporary secretions).
> Its membership criterion is historical — *made by or of life forms*
> (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biogenic_substance) — rather than compositional or
> structural, which is why chalk (a rock) and grass (a tissue) belong to one kind while no
> compositional taxonomy could hold them together. Above it stands only
> [Natural Material](note.html?n=nature/natural-material.md), whose criterion is negative
> (not intentionally transformed) and therefore more abstract still, but origin-free.
