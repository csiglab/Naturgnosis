# Hydrosphere

> **Role**: The hydrosphere is crucial for supporting life, regulating climate, and shaping landscapes through erosion and sediment deposition.
>

> **Description**: The hydrosphere includes all the water found on, under, and above Earth’s surface (oceans, rivers, lakes, groundwater, and ice). Water constantly moves through the Earth’s systems via the water cycle.
>
