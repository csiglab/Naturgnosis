## Anatomical Locations

> How does the distribution of adipose tissue vary among different human populations, and what factors influence these differences?
>

| Location | Description | Function/Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subcutaneous Fat** | Located beneath the skin, especially in the abdomen, thighs, buttocks, and arms. | Largest fat depot; provides insulation and energy storage. |
| **Visceral Fat** | Surrounds internal organs in the abdominal cavity (e.g., mesenteric, omental fat). | Linked to metabolic risk factors and diseases. |
| **Intramuscular Fat** | Deposited between muscle fibers. | Influences muscle function and metabolism. |
| **Bone Marrow Fat** | Fat stored within the bone marrow. | Involved in bone metabolism and hematopoiesis. |
| **Retroperitoneal Fat** | Fat behind the peritoneum around kidneys and adrenal glands. | Cushions and protects organs. |
| **Epicardial Fat** | Around the heart, between myocardium and pericardium. | Provides energy to the heart, may influence cardiac function. |
| **Breast Fat** | Adipose tissue in breast tissue. | Provides shape and cushioning. |
