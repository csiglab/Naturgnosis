# Endocrine System

> The **Endocrine System** is one of the major control systems of the body. It works alongside the **nervous system** but communicates more slowly and broadly through **chemical messengers** called **hormones**.
>

> The **endocrine system** is a collection of glands and specialized cells that **produce and release hormones** to regulate various body functions, such as metabolism, growth, reproduction, and mood.
>

> The endocrine system is decentralized, and various organs from other systems contribute to endocrine activity as well.
>

## Index

## Etymology

The word **"Endocrine"** comes from Greek roots:

- **“endo-”** meaning **“within”** or **“inside”**
- **“-crine”** meaning **“to secrete”**

## 🧬 **Main Features**

| **Feature** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Mode of Signaling** | Chemical (via hormones in the bloodstream) |
| **Speed** | Slower than the nervous system (seconds to days) |
| **Effect Duration** | Longer-lasting effects |
| **Target Specificity** | Hormones affect only cells with specific **receptors** for that hormone |
| **Type of Control** | Regulatory (homeostasis, development, energy balance, etc.) |

## 🏗️ **Major Endocrine Glands**

| **Gland** | **Hormones Produced** | **Functions** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hypothalamus** | Releasing and inhibiting hormones | Controls the pituitary; links nervous and endocrine systems |
| **Pituitary** | GH, TSH, ACTH, LH, FSH, Prolactin, etc. | "Master gland"—regulates other endocrine glands |
| **Thyroid** | Thyroxine (T4), Triiodothyronine (T3) | Regulates metabolism, growth, and development |
| **Parathyroids** | Parathyroid hormone (PTH) | Regulates blood calcium levels |
| **Adrenal glands** | Cortisol, adrenaline, aldosterone | Stress response, metabolism, salt-water balance |
| **Pancreas** | Insulin, glucagon | Regulates blood sugar levels |
| **Ovaries** | Estrogen, progesterone | Regulates female reproductive system |
| **Testes** | Testosterone | Regulates male reproductive system |
| **Pineal gland** | Melatonin | Regulates sleep-wake cycles |

## 🔄 **Feedback Loops**

The endocrine system often works through **negative feedback** mechanisms:

- When hormone levels rise above a certain point, production is **inhibited**.
- When levels drop too low, production is **stimulated**.

## 🧩 **Related Systems**

| **Related System** | **Connection** |
| --- | --- |
| **Nervous system** | Hypothalamus is both neural and endocrine; rapid vs slow coordination |
| **Circulatory system** | Blood transports hormones to target organs |
| **Digestive system** | Hormones like insulin and glucagon affect nutrient processing |
| **Reproductive system** | Hormonal control of ovulation, menstruation, sperm production |
