# Chemical Substance

> A **chemical substance** is a specific type of matter that has a definite chemical composition and distinct properties. Chemical substances are composed of **atoms or molecules** that are bonded together through **chemical bonds**.
>

¿Which substances are not Chemicals?

These substances can be pure elements, compounds, or mixtures of different compounds.

Here are some critical characteristics of chemical substances:

1. **Definite Composition:** Chemical substances have a fixed composition, meaning they consist of a specific arrangement of atoms or molecules in a defined ratio. For example, water (H2O) always consists of two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom.
2. **Chemical Properties:** Chemical substances have unique chemical properties that determine how they react with other substances. These properties include reactivity, acidity/basicity, combustibility, and toxicity.
3. **Physical Properties:** Chemical substances also possess physical properties such as color, odor, density, melting point, and boiling point, which can be observed or measured without altering the chemical composition of the substance.
4. **Stability:** Chemical substances can exist in different states (solid, liquid, or gas) depending on factors such as temperature and pressure. They may also undergo physical or chemical changes under certain conditions, but their chemical composition remains unchanged.

Chemical substances play a fundamental role in chemistry and are the building blocks of all matter in the universe. They can be found in nature, such as minerals, rocks, and gases in the atmosphere, as well as in synthetic materials produced by humans, such as plastics, pharmaceuticals, and industrial chemicals.

It's important to note that "chemical substance" is often used interchangeably with "chemical compound," mainly when referring to pure substances with definite compositions. However, chemical substances can also include elements and mixtures of compounds as long as they exhibit distinct chemical and physical properties.
