# Earth Water System

> The **Earth Water System** is a coupled, nonlinear, open geophysical system comprising the continuous exchange, phase transition, and transport of H₂O across atmospheric, cryospheric, oceanic, terrestrial surface, and subsurface reservoirs, driven primarily by solar radiation and gravitational forces, and regulated through complex feedbacks involving energy, mass, and momentum fluxes within and between Earth's spheres.
