# Mass Transport

> …
>

QA:

- What is Mass Transport? — Define the phenomenon, scope, and significance.
- Which are the  underlying mechanism? — Understand the physical processes involved (diffusion, advection, convection, migration, etc.).
- Which are the model used to describe it? — Learn the mathematical frameworks, constitutive laws, and equations (e.g., Fick’s laws, convection-diffusion equations).

### Fundamental Concepts

1. What is mass transport? How does it differ from other transport phenomena like heat and momentum transfer?
2. What are the primary mechanisms of mass transport?
3. What physical principles govern mass transport? (e.g., **conservation of mass**)
4. What is diffusion? How does it arise at the molecular level?

### Mathematical Modeling

1. What are Fick’s First and Second Laws, and how are they derived?
2. How do I apply Fick’s laws to steady-state and transient diffusion problems?
3. How do boundary and initial conditions affect solutions to diffusion equations?
4. What are common analytical solutions to diffusion problems?

### Extensions and Complexities

1. How is mass transport affected by convection/advection?
2. How do I model mass transport in multi-component or multi-phase systems?
3. What role do chemical reactions play in coupled mass transport?
4. How do non-idealities (e.g., non-constant diffusivity, turbulence) affect mass transport models?

### Experimental and Practical Aspects

1. How are diffusion coefficients measured experimentally?
2. What are typical values and scales of diffusivity in gases, liquids, and solids?
3. What industrial or biological systems rely on mass transport, and how is it controlled or optimized?

### Advanced Topics

1. How does mass transport behave in porous media or complex geometries?
2. What are modern computational methods for simulating mass transport (e.g., numerical PDE solvers, CFD)?
3. How does nanoscale or molecular-scale mass transport differ from classical diffusion?
4. What are the challenges in multi-scale modeling of mass transport phenomena?
5. How can machine learning or data-driven methods be integrated with classical mass transport models?
