# Rock

> In **geology**, a **rock** is a **naturally occurring solid aggregate of one or more minerals, mineraloids, or organic materials**. Rocks form the solid outer layer of the Earth (the **lithosphere**) and are classified based on their origin, texture, and mineral composition.
>

### 🔍 **Key Features of Rocks**

- **Aggregate**: A rock is not a pure substance; it's a mixture of minerals.
- **Solid and Coherent**: It holds together and maintains a shape.
- **Naturally Occurring**: Not synthetic or man-made.
- **May include**:
    - **Minerals** (e.g., quartz, feldspar, mica)
    - **Mineraloids** (e.g., obsidian, which lacks a crystalline structure)
    - **Organic material** (e.g., coal, formed from plant remains)

### 🪨 **Three Main Types of Rocks**

| **Type** | **Formation Process** | **Examples** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Igneous** | Formed from cooling and solidification of magma or lava | Granite, basalt |
| **Sedimentary** | Formed from accumulation and compaction of sediments | Limestone, sandstone |
| **Metamorphic** | Formed from alteration of existing rocks under heat/pressure | Marble, schist, gneiss |
