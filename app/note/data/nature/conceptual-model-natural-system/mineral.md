# Mineral

> Naturally occurring, inorganic solid with a definite chemical composition and an ordered atomic structure.
>

> **Rock**: A composite of one or more minerals.
>

## Index

## 🔍 **Key Characteristics of a Mineral**

| **Property** | **Explanation** |
| --- | --- |
| **Naturally occurring** | Formed by natural geological processes—not synthetic or man-made |
| **Inorganic** | Not derived from living organisms (exception: some debate around biogenic minerals like calcite) |
| **Solid** | Must be in a solid state under normal Earth conditions |
| **Definite chemical composition** | Each mineral has a specific chemical formula, which can vary within limits (e.g., olivine: (Mg,Fe)₂SiO₄) |
| **Crystalline structure** | Atoms are arranged in a repeating, orderly 3D pattern |

## 🪨 **Examples of Common Minerals**

| **Mineral** | **Chemical Formula** | **Type** | **Found in** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quartz | SiO₂ | Silicate | Granite, sandstone |
| Calcite | CaCO₃ | Carbonate | Limestone, marble |
| Feldspar | KAlSi₃O₈ – NaAlSi₃O₈ | Silicate | Most igneous rocks |
| Pyrite | FeS₂ | Sulfide | Hydrothermal veins |
| Halite | NaCl | Halide | Evaporite deposits |
