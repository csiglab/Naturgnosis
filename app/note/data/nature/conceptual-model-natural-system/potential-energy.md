# Potential Energy

> **Potential energy** is the **energy stored** in an object because of its position or arrangement, such as an object held at a height, a stretched spring, or charged particles in an electric field.
>

- **Gravitational systems** – A rock on a cliff or water behind a dam has gravitational potential energy due to its height.
- **Elastic systems** – A stretched or compressed spring stores elastic potential energy.
- **Electrical systems** – Charges in an electric field have electric potential energy depending on their positions.
- **Chemical systems** – Molecules in fuel or food store chemical potential energy in their bonds.
- **Nuclear systems** – Atomic nuclei store nuclear potential energy, released in fission or fusion.
- **Magnetic systems** – Magnetic objects in a magnetic field can store potential energy depending on their alignment and position.
