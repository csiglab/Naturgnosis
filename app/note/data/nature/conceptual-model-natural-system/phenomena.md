# Phenomena

> Phenomena are observable events or occurrences that can be perceived through the senses or detected with scientific instruments. They constitute the empirical basis for knowledge and investigation in science, encompassing both natural and artificial processes or behaviors.
>

> Can any stream of events be consider a phenomena? Or  for there be a phenomena there must be a pattern underlying the events?
