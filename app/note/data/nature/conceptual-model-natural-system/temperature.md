# Temperature

> It **reflects** the average [kinetic energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_energy) of the vibrating and colliding [atoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom) making up a substance.
>

> It indicates how hot or cold an object is.
>

> **Kinetic Energy**: Energy of motion.
>
