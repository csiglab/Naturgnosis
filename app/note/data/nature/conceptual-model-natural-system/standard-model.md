# Standard Model

> The **Standard Model** is a **fundamental theory** in particle physics that describes the **electromagnetic**, **weak**, and **strong forces** governing the behavior of subatomic particles, providing a framework for understanding the interactions between **quarks**, **leptons**, and **gauge bosons**.
>
