# Kinetic Energy

> **Kinetic energy** is the energy an object possesses due to its motion, equal to half its mass multiplied by the square of its velocity ($KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$).
>

> **Kinetic energy** was historically referred to as **"vis viva"** (Latin for *living force*) in the 17th and 18th centuries. **Vis viva** was part of early debates on conservation laws, eventually evolving into our modern concept of **kinetic energy**.
>

## What is the difference between kinetic energy and potential energy?

- **Kinetic energy** is the energy of **motion**—it depends on an object's mass and velocity.
- **Potential energy** is **stored energy** due to an object's position, condition, or configuration—for example, a rock at the top of a hill or a compressed spring.
