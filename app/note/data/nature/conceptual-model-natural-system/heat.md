# Heat

> Heat is another name for **thermal energy**, the **energy** contained in the **vibrations of atoms** and **molecules**.
>

> It's important to note that heat is distinct from **temperature**. Temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy of particles in a substance, while heat is the transfer of energy between substances or systems with different temperatures.
>

> Heat is a form of energy that is transferred between systems or objects due to a temperature difference. It is a measure of the **internal energy** contained within a substance, arising from the random motion of its molecules or atoms. Heat transfer occurs through three main mechanisms: conduction, convection, and radiation.
>

> As we see the normal used of the word “heat” which is used in similar tones to “temperature” is not really a well thought one.
>

> Heat is an emergent property;  and some molecular movement does not produce any heat at all; so it’s a property of some specific ‘configurations’ of those movements. Look more in this line.
>

![image.png](/note/data/nature/conceptual-model-natural-system/heat/image.png)

Actions:

- Heat transfer can raise or lower the **temperature** of a material.
- Heat transfer can change the state of a material without altering its temperature,
- Heat transfer causes most materials to expand when heated and contract when cooled, affecting their size and shape.
- Heat transfer results in the transfer of **kinetic energy** from **higher-energy** particles (atoms or molecules) to **lower-energy** ones, causing an increase in their vibrational, rotational, or translational motion; during phase changes, heat energy breaks intermolecular forces without altering temperature, while in thermal expansion, increased atomic vibrations push particles further apart.

## Heat Transfer

> **Heat transfer** is the **process of thermal energy exchange** between physical systems due to a **temperature gradient**. It governs how heat energy moves from regions of higher temperature to regions of lower temperature until thermal equilibrium is reached.
>

Types:

- **Conduction** Transfer of heat through a **solid or stationary fluid** by **microscopic collisions and vibrations of molecules and free electrons**.
- **Convection** Heat transfer via **bulk fluid motion**, combining conduction and fluid flow.
- **Radiation** Transfer of energy through **electromagnetic waves** without the need for a medium.
