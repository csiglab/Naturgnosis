# Microorganism

> In this note will explore an element of reality — the Microorganism.
>

## **Type of Microorganism**

| **Type of Microorganism** | **Description** | **Instance(s)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bacteria** | Single-celled organisms that can be found in various environments; can be beneficial or pathogenic. | *Escherichia coli* (E. coli), *Streptococcus* species |
| **Viruses** | Acellular entities that require a host cell to replicate and reproduce; can cause various diseases. | HIV, Influenza virus, SARS-CoV-2 (COVID-19) |
| **Fungi** | Eukaryotic organisms that can be single-celled or multicellular; can decompose organic material or cause infections. | *Candida* (yeast infection), *Aspergillus* (fungus) |
| **Protozoa** | Single-celled eukaryotic organisms, often motile and can cause diseases in humans and other animals. | *Plasmodium* (malaria), *Giardia* (giardiasis) |
| **Algae** | Photosynthetic organisms, primarily aquatic, that can be single-celled or multicellular. | *Chlorella*, *Spirogyra* |
| **Archaea** | Single-celled microorganisms that are genetically distinct from bacteria and often live in extreme environments. | *Methanogens*, *Halophiles* |
| **Prions** | Infectious proteins that cause neurodegenerative diseases by inducing misfolding of normal proteins. | *Prion diseases* (e.g., Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease) |
| **Helminths** | Parasitic worms that can infect humans, often affecting internal organs and tissues. | *Ascaris* (roundworm), *Taenia* (tapeworm) |
