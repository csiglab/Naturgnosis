# Transformation of Matter

> **The transformation of matter** is a unifying abstraction that models all changes in matter — whether physical or chemical — as transitions between distinct **states** of a material system, governed by the interactions and rearrangements of its constituent particles under physical laws.
>

## Summary

| **Component** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **State** | A well-defined configuration of matter described by structural, energetic, and compositional properties (e.g. phase, molecular structure, thermodynamic parameters). |
| **Transformation** | A transition from one state to another, triggered by internal dynamics or external influences (e.g. heat, pressure, radiation, or interaction with other substances). |
| **Types of Transformations** | **Physical**: Change of form, state, or energy (e.g. melting, expansion, magnetization) without altering molecular composition. |
|  | **Chemical**: Rearrangement of atoms leading to new molecular species (e.g. combustion, acid-base reaction). |
| **Driving Forces** | Changes in energy, entropy, or external fields (temperature, pressure, electromagnetic fields, etc.). |
| **Modeling Frameworks** | Thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, molecular dynamics, quantum chemistry. |
