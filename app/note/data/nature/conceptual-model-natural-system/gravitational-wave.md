# Gravitational Wave

> A **gravitational wave** is a ripple in spacetime caused by the acceleration of massive objects, such as merging black holes or neutron stars, which propagates outward at the speed of light.
>

## Gravitational Wave Detection & Measurement

> **Gravitational waves** are detected and measured using **laser interferometers**, which track minuscule **distortions in spacetime** caused by **passing waves** through changes in the interference pattern of **laser beams**.
>
