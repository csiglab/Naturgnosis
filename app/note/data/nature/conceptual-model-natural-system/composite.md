# Composite

> A **composite** is a material made by combining two or more distinct constituent materials—typically a matrix and a reinforcement—such that the resulting material exhibits improved properties (e.g., strength, stiffness, or durability) not achievable by the individual components alone.
>

> **Alloys** are **uniform metallic mixtures** with improved bulk properties (e.g., steel).
>

> **Composites** are **multiphase engineered materials** where the combination achieves performance impossible for the individual components (e.g., carbon fiber composites).
>

## Additional Notes (Notion duplicate)

> A **"composite material"** is a material made from two or more constituent materials with significantly different physical or chemical properties, combined to create a new material with enhanced performance characteristics.
