# Photon

> A **photon** is a type of **boson**, specifically a **gauge boson**, that acts as the quantum of **electromagnetic radiation** and the force carrier of the **electromagnetic force**.
>

> **Massless**: It has zero rest mass, allowing it to travel at the speed of light in a vacuum ($c$).
>

> **Charge-neutral**: It has no electric charge.
>

> Photons are the **force carriers** of the **electromagnetic force**, mediating interactions between charged particles (e.g., electrons and protons).
>

> Despite being electrically neutral, photons interact with charged particles through the **electromagnetic field**.
