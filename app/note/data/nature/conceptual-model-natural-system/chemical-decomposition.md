# Chemical Decomposition

> **Chemical decomposition** is a reaction in which a single compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances, often triggered by heat, light, or chemical processes.
>

> **Thermal decomposition** is a chemical process in which a compound breaks down into simpler substances when heated, often producing gases, liquids, or solids as byproducts.
>
