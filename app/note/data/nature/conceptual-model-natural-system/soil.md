# Soil

> The upper layer of loose, unconsolidated material that supports plant life; rich in organic matter.
>

> Soil is a subset of the sedimentary cover.

## Index

## Soil Space

> Which are the different type of materials on the  Earth Crust can be considered soil?
>

| **Type** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Loess** | Wind-deposited silt and fine sand, often fertile and forming thick layers |
| **Alluvial Soil** | Sediments deposited by rivers and streams, rich in minerals and organic matter |
| **Regolith / Weathered Bedrock** | Broken down and chemically altered rock forming the base for soil development |
| **Volcanic Ash / Tephra** | Fine volcanic materials that create highly fertile soils after weathering |
| **Peat / Organic Soils** | Accumulated partially decomposed plant material, rich in organic matter |
| **Colluvial Soil** | Soil and rock fragments accumulated at the base of slopes via gravity |
| **Glacial Till / Moraines** | Mixed soil and rock debris deposited by glaciers |
| **Desert / Aeolian Soils** | Sand and silt transported and deposited by wind in arid regions |

## Space Property

> Which are the properties that can be predicated on the Soil?
>

| **Category** | **Property** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Physical** | Texture | Relative proportions of sand, silt, and clay in the soil |
|  | Structure | Arrangement of soil particles into aggregates (granular, blocky, platy, etc.) |
|  | Porosity | Fraction of soil volume occupied by pores, affecting air and water movement |
|  | Bulk Density | Mass of soil per unit volume; indicates compaction |
|  | Color | Visual indicator of organic matter content, mineral composition, and drainage |
|  | Water Retention / Field Capacity | Ability of soil to hold water available to plants |
| **Chemical** | pH | Acidity or alkalinity of soil |
|  | Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC) | Soil’s ability to hold and exchange positively charged ions (nutrients) |
|  | Nutrient Content | Levels of essential elements like N, P, K, Ca, Mg |
|  | Salinity | Concentration of soluble salts in the soil |
|  | Organic Matter Content | Percentage of decomposed plant and animal material |
| **Biological** | Microbial Biomass | Quantity of living microorganisms in the soil |
|  | Fauna Presence | Presence of soil organisms (earthworms, insects, nematodes) |
|  | Enzyme Activity | Biochemical activity indicative of nutrient cycling |
|  | Root Penetration Capacity | Ability of plant roots to grow and access nutrients |
| **Mechanical** | Bearing Capacity | Soil’s ability to support loads without failure |
|  | Compressibility | Degree to which soil volume decreases under pressure |
| **Thermal** | Heat Capacity | Ability of soil to store heat |
|  | Thermal Conductivity | Rate at which soil conducts heat |

## Process Space

> What physical, chemical, and biological processes occur in the soil?
>

| **Category** | **Process** | **Description** | **Material Substrate** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Physical** | Water Infiltration | Movement of water through soil pores | Soil pores, aggregates |
|  | Erosion | Removal and transport of soil particles by wind or water | Soil surface, loose sediments |
|  | Compaction | Decrease in soil volume due to pressure | Soil particles, organic matter |
|  | Freezing / Thawing Cycles | Expansion and contraction of water in soil causing structural changes | Soil water, soil minerals |
|  | Crusting | Formation of a hard surface layer after rain or irrigation | Fine soil particles |
| **Chemical** | Mineral Weathering | Breakdown of primary minerals into secondary minerals | Parent rock, mineral grains |
|  | Nutrient Cycling | Transformation and movement of nutrients (N, P, K, etc.) | Organic matter, soil minerals |
|  | Acid-Base Reactions | Changes in soil pH due to chemical reactions | Soil solution, minerals |
|  | Redox Reactions | Oxidation and reduction reactions affecting nutrient availability | Soil minerals, organic matter |
|  | Salinization / Desalination | Accumulation or removal of salts in soil | Soil solution, salts |
| **Biological** | Decomposition | Breakdown of organic matter by microorganisms | Organic residues, humus |
|  | Nitrogen Fixation | Conversion of atmospheric N₂ into biologically available forms | Root nodules, free-living microbes |
|  | Microbial Respiration | Release of CO₂ from microorganisms as they metabolize organic matter | Soil organic matter |
|  | Bioturbation | Mixing of soil by organisms such as earthworms or insects | Soil particles, organic matter |
|  | Mycorrhizal Symbiosis | Mutualistic association between fungi and plant roots | Roots, fungal hyphae |

## Interaction Space

> How the soil interact with living things?
>

| **Interaction** | **Participant(s)** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nutrient Supply** | Plants, Microorganisms | Soil provides essential nutrients (N, P, K, micronutrients) for growth |
| **Water Supply** | Plants, Animals | Soil stores and delivers water through its pore spaces |
| **Habitat / Shelter** | Microorganisms, Soil Fauna | Soil serves as habitat for bacteria, fungi, earthworms, insects, and burrowing animals |
| **Decomposition & Recycling** | Microorganisms, Detritivores | Soil organisms break down organic matter, recycling nutrients back into the soil |
| **Symbiosis** | Plants, Mycorrhizal Fungi | Mutualistic relationships enhance nutrient uptake and soil health |
| **Filtration & Purification** | Water, Microbes | Soil filters pollutants and organic matter, maintaining ecosystem quality |
| **Carbon Sequestration** | Plants, Microorganisms | Soil stores organic carbon, reducing atmospheric CO₂ levels |
| **Root Anchorage & Support** | Plants | Soil physically supports plant roots, enabling growth and stability |
| **Influence on Biodiversity** | Plants, Animals, Microbes | Soil properties and structure affect species composition and ecosystem dynamics |

|  | Mulching | Covering soil with organic or inorganic material to retain moisture and prevent erosion |

## Research Technique Space

> How do we investigate the earth?
>

| **Category** | **Technique** | **Description** | **Technical Object** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Field Observation** | Soil Sampling | Collecting soil cores or surface samples to analyze physical, chemical, and biological properties | Soil auger, corer, trowel, sample bags, GPS |
|  | Soil Profiling | Examining soil horizons in a soil pit or trench to study vertical structure | Shovel, spade, soil auger, profile description sheets, measuring tape |
|  | Transect Surveys | Systematic observation along lines to assess spatial variation in soil properties | Measuring tape, flag markers, GPS, field notebook |
|  | In-situ Measurements | Measuring soil moisture, temperature, pH, or other properties directly in the field | Soil moisture probe, thermometers, pH meter, EC meter, tensiometer |
| **Laboratory Analysis** | Physical Analysis | Determining texture, bulk density, porosity, and water retention | Sieves, hydrometer, core samplers, pressure plates, balances |
|  | Chemical Analysis | Measuring nutrient content, pH, salinity, cation exchange capacity, organic matter | Spectrophotometer, pH meter, extraction kits, digestion apparatus, titration setup |
|  | Biological Analysis | Identifying microbial biomass, enzyme activity, fauna, and root interactions | Incubators, microscopes, DNA sequencer, enzyme assay kits, soil fauna extraction equipment |
|  | Mineralogical / Spectroscopic Analysis | Identifying soil mineral composition using X-ray diffraction, spectroscopy, or microscopy | X-ray diffractometer, SEM/TEM microscope, spectrometer, polarizing microscope |
| **Remote Sensing & GIS** | Satellite / Aerial Imagery | Mapping soil types, moisture, vegetation cover, and erosion patterns | Satellites, drones, multispectral/hyperspectral cameras, remote sensing software |
|  | Geographic Information Systems (GIS) | Integrating soil data spatially for analysis and modeling | GIS software (ArcGIS, QGIS), GPS, soil databases, spatial analysis tools |
| **Experimental Manipulation** | Pot / Microcosm Experiments | Controlled studies of soil processes, nutrient cycling, or microbial activity | Pots, growth chambers, controlled-environment rooms, irrigation system |
|  | Tracer Studies | Using isotopes or dyes to track water movement, nutrient flow, or pollutant transport | Isotopic tracers, dyes, spectrophotometer, liquid scintillation counter, sampling equipment |
| **Modeling & Simulation** | Soil Process Modeling | Computational models of erosion, water flow, carbon cycling, or nutrient dynamics | Computer, modeling software (e.g., SWAT, HYDRUS, CENTURY) |
|  | Predictive Mapping | Using statistical or machine learning models to predict soil properties across landscapes | Computer, statistical/ML software (R, Python, ArcGIS), spatial datasets |

## Formal Representation

> Which types  of formal Representation are made about the soil?
>

| **Representation** | **Description** | **Goal** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Soil Classification Systems** | Organized schemes to categorize soils based on properties, horizons, and genesis (e.g., USDA Soil Taxonomy, FAO Soil Classification) | Standardize soil description and enable comparison across regions |
| **Soil Maps** | Spatial representation of soil types and properties over a landscape | Guide land use planning, agriculture, and environmental management |
| **Soil Profiles / Pedons** | Vertical cross-sections showing horizons, texture, structure, and composition | Understand soil formation, stratigraphy, and functional characteristics |
| **Mathematical Models** | Equations or simulations representing soil-water-plant interactions, nutrient cycling, or erosion | Predict soil behavior under different environmental or management scenarios |
| **Laboratory Data Tables** | Structured data of soil properties (physical, chemical, biological) | Support analysis, comparison, and decision-making |
| **GIS & Remote Sensing Layers** | Digital representations of soil properties and processes across spatial scales | Integrate soil data with geography, climate, and land-use information |
| **Soil Quality Indices** | Composite metrics combining multiple soil properties (e.g., fertility, organic matter, contamination) | Assess soil health and monitor changes over time |
| **3D / Virtual Soil Models** | Interactive models of soil structure, horizons, and processes in three dimensions | Visualize soil properties, support education, and aid in research |
