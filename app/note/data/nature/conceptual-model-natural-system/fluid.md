# Fluid

> A **fluid** is a substance that continuously deforms (flows) under any amount of shear stress, no matter how small, and includes both **liquids** and **gases**, characterized by the ability to take the shape of their container and lack a fixed shape but not necessarily a fixed volume.
>

> These systems are studied in fluid mechanics, a branch of continuum mechanics.
>

> An abstraction used to reason about **Liquids**, **gases**, **plasmas**, and sometimes **supercritical fluids.**
