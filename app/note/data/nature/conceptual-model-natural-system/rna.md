# RNA

> **RNA** stands for **Ribonucleic Acid**. It is a molecule essential for various biological roles, primarily involved in the process of translating genetic information from DNA into proteins.
>

> Note: Some Biological Systems like Viruses  has RNA and not DNA; In Those Systems RNA is the digital code of Life.
>

> Note: RNA can also be the digital code of life; look at the downsides of this? And Why DNA is useful?
