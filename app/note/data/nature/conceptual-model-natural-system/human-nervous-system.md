# Human Nervous System

> A **hierarchical**, **multiscale biological control** and **communication network** composed of specialized excitable cells (neurons) and support cells (glia), responsible for the reception, processing, integration, and transmission of electrochemical signals throughout an organism, enabling sensory perception, motor coordination, homeostatic regulation, and higher-order cognitive functions.
>

## Index

## Structure

- **Central Nervous System (CNS):** Brain and spinal cord; responsible for integration, computation, and control.
- **Peripheral Nervous System (PNS):** Nerves and ganglia outside the CNS; mediates sensory input and motor output.
- **Autonomic Nervous System (ANS):** Subsystem regulating involuntary physiological functions (sympathetic, parasympathetic, enteric divisions).
- **Somatic Nervous System:** Controls voluntary muscle movements and conveys sensory information to the CNS.

## Layer Architecture

| Level | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **Molecular/Cellular** | Ion channels, neurotransmitters, synaptic transmission |
| **Circuit** | Local processing, reflex arcs, microcircuits |
| **Network/Systemic** | Global communication, coordination of behavior and states |
| **Cognitive** | Learning, memory, decision-making, consciousness |
