# Energy Level

> **An element of the physically** admissible value-set of the energy observable, determined by the system’s dynamical structure; ontologically a modal-structural feature of the system, not a material or event-like entity.
>

> Note: Different areas of physics will have a different formulation of the term.
>

Across physics, energy level means:

> “This is the set of states compatible with the system’s dynamics, symmetries, and constraints.”
>

It tells you:

- What the system *can* do
- What it *cannot* do
- How it can *change*
