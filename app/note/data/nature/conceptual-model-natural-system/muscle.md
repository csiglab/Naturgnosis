## Composition

> **Myocyte ("myo-"** = muscle & **"-cyte"** = cell**)** is a general term for any muscle cell, with specific types like **cardiomyocytes** (heart), **leiomyocytes** (smooth muscle), and **myofibers** (skeletal muscle)….
>

| **Component** | **Description** | **% of Muscle Mass (approx.)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Muscle Cells (Myofibers)** | Long, contractile cells packed with actin & myosin for movement | **~75–90%** |
| – **Myofibrils** | Protein structures inside muscle cells responsible for contraction (actin, myosin, troponin, tropomyosin) | Major part of myofibers |
| – **Sarcoplasm** | Cytoplasm of muscle cells, containing enzymes, glycogen, and mitochondria |  |
| – **Sarcolemma** | The plasma membrane of a muscle cell |  |
| **Connective Tissue** | Collagen-based tissue that surrounds muscle fibers and groups | **~5–15%** |
| – **Endomysium** | Surrounds individual muscle fibers |  |
| – **Perimysium** | Groups fibers into bundles (fascicles) |  |
| – **Epimysium** | Encloses the entire muscle |  |
| **Blood Vessels** | Supply oxygen and nutrients; remove waste | **~1–3%** |
| **Nerves** | Motor neurons and sensory fibers controlling and monitoring contraction | **<1%** |
| **Extracellular Fluid** | Ions and water around cells, supports metabolic exchange | Small amount |
| **Intracellular Water** | Water within cells; part of the total water content | ~70–75% of muscle weight |
| **Stored Nutrients** | Glycogen, lipids, ATP, creatine phosphate for energy supply | Minor, but essential |
