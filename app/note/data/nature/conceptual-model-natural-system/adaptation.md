# Adaptation

> **Adaptation:** A *process or outcome* in which a system (biological, social, or technical) modifies its structure, function, or behavior in response to environmental or internal pressures to maintain or improve performance or fitness.
>

> **Evolution:**  A *population-level, long-term process* by which the **distribution of traits or structures** changes over generations due to mechanisms like **mutation, selection, drift, and gene flow**.
>

> **Adaptation** is **one mechanism** through which evolution operates. In biology, adaptations (like camouflage or echolocation) are the *phenotypic expressions* of evolutionary processes. In other systems (e.g., AI, economics), adaptation can occur without an evolutionary process, through real-time learning or feedback.
>

> Evolution is the process of change; adaptation is what persists through that change.
>

- Genetic drift (random changes)
- Gene flow (migration)
- Mutation (random genetic changes)
- Natural selection (where adaptation comes in)
- Selective Breeding → Man Made
- Cultural Evolution
