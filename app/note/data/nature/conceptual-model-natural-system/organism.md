# Organism

> In biology, an **organism** is any individual living entity that exhibits the characteristics of life. It is a complex structure made up of one or more cells and is capable of carrying out essential life processes, such as growth, reproduction, metabolism, response to stimuli, and homeostasis.
>

## Molecular Composition

> Here’s a table that categorizes different types of molecules found in biological organisms, along with their characteristics and roles:
>

| **Type of Molecule** | **Subcategory** | **Examples** | **Function/Role** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Biomolecules** | **Proteins** | Enzymes, antibodies, collagen, hemoglobin | Catalyze biochemical reactions, provide structure, immune defense, transport |
|  | **Nucleic Acids** | DNA, RNA | Store and transmit genetic information, protein synthesis |
|  | **Carbohydrates** | Glucose, starch, glycogen, cellulose | Energy source, energy storage, structural components |
|  | **Lipids** | Fats, oils, phospholipids, cholesterol | Energy storage, cell membrane structure, signaling |
|  | **Vitamins & Coenzymes** | Vitamin C, Vitamin A, NADH, FADH | Facilitate biochemical reactions, support enzyme function |
| **Inorganic Molecules** | **Ions & Minerals** | Sodium (Na⁺), Potassium (K⁺), Calcium (Ca²⁺), Iron (Fe²⁺) | Maintain osmotic balance, electrical gradients, enzyme co-factors |
|  | **Gases** | Oxygen (O₂), Carbon dioxide (CO₂), Nitrogen (N₂) | Respiration, metabolism, nitrogen fixation |
| **Simple Organic Molecules** | **Small Organic Compounds** | Acetyl-CoA, glucose, fatty acids | Metabolism, energy production, biosynthesis |
| **Water** | **Water (H₂O)** | Water molecules | Solvent for biochemical reactions, temperature regulation, hydration |
| **Non-Biomolecular Organic Molecules** | **Hormones** | Insulin, estrogen, testosterone | Regulate physiological processes, growth, and development |
| **Other Non-Biomolecular Molecules** | **Waste Products** | Urea, ammonia, uric acid | Excretion, removal of metabolic waste |
