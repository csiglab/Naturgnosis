# Fundamental Interaction

> The **electroweak force** is a unified description of two of the fundamental forces: the **electromagnetic force** and the **weak nuclear force**.
>

> **Unification**: At high energies (above ~100 GeV), the  electromagnetic and weak forces are unified into a single electroweak  interaction, described by the **Glashow-Weinberg-Salam model**.
>

> **Symmetry Breaking:** At lower energies, the **Higgs mechanism** breaks the
electroweak symmetry, giving mass to the W and Z bosons while leaving
the photon massless. This separates the electromagnetic and weak forces
as we observe them today.
>

> **Mediators**: The electroweak force is mediated by four particles: the **photon** (for electromagnetism) and the **W⁺, W⁻, and Z bosons** (for the weak force).
>
