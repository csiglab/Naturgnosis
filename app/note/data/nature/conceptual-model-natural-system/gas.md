# Gas

> A **gas is a state of matter** consisting of particles that are far apart and move freely, allowing it to expand to fill any container and take its shape.
>

## Index

## 🔋 **By Origin or Composition**

| **Type of Gas** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Natural Gas** | Mainly methane (CH₄); extracted from underground reservoirs; used for heating, electricity, fuel. |
| **Biogas** | Produced from organic matter (e.g., animal waste, plant material) via anaerobic digestion; contains methane and CO₂. |
| **LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas)** | Mixture of propane and butane; stored as a liquid under pressure; used for cooking, heating, vehicles. |
| **Syngas (Synthesis Gas)** | A mix of CO and H₂ produced from gasification of carbon-based fuels; used in chemical synthesis and fuel production. |
| **Producer Gas** | Made from partial combustion of coal or biomass; contains CO, H₂, N₂. |
| **Landfill Gas** | Generated by decomposition of waste in landfills; similar to biogas. |
| **Coal Gas** | Historic gas made from coal; includes hydrogen, methane, carbon monoxide. |
| **Water Gas** | Produced by passing steam over hot coal; mixture of CO and H₂. |

## **Avogadro's Law**

> Equal **volumes of gases**, at the same temperature and pressure, contain equal numbers of molecules.
>

> Volume is **directly proportional** to the number of moles of gas when pressure and temperature are constant.
>

## Modelling

| **Tool / Model** | **Modeling Approach** | **Scale** | **Typical Use Cases** | **Assumptions** / Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ideal Gas Law** | Analytical, thermodynamic | Macroscopic | Basic chemistry, physics, engineering | No intermolecular forces, point particles |
| **Van der Waals Equation** | Analytical, empirical | Macroscopic | Real gas behavior near condensation | Adds volume and attraction corrections |
| **Virial Equation of State** | Empirical, series expansion | Macroscopic | Accurate modeling of real gases at low/mod pressure | Uses virial coefficients (data-dependent) |
| **Redlich-Kwong, Peng-Robinson** | Analytical, cubic EoS | Macroscopic | Chemical engineering, phase equilibria | Good for hydrocarbons and supercritical fluids |
| **Statistical Mechanics (Partition Functions)** | Probabilistic | Microscopic → Macroscopic | Predicts thermodynamic properties from molecular structure | Based on Boltzmann distribution |
| **Kinetic Theory of Gases** | Statistical, dynamic | Mesoscopic | Predicts pressure, temperature, transport properties | Treats gases as many colliding particles |
| **Boltzmann Equation** | Integro-differential equation | Mesoscopic | Rarefied gas dynamics, nonequilibrium systems | Fundamental to transport theory |
| **Direct Simulation Monte Carlo (DSMC)** | Stochastic, particle-based | Mesoscopic | High-altitude flows, vacuum chambers | Simulates gas collisions numerically |
| **Molecular Dynamics (MD)** | Newtonian particle simulation | Microscopic | Dense gases, nanoscale systems, shockwaves | High fidelity, high computational cost |
| **Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM)** | Mesoscopic, lattice-based | Mesoscopic → Continuum | Complex flows, porous media, microfluidics | Efficient for parallel computing |
| **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)** | Continuum, Navier-Stokes | Macroscopic | Engineering: engines, wind tunnels, HVAC | Assumes gas is a continuous fluid |
| **Equation of State Databases (e.g. NIST REFPROP)** | Empirical, tabulated | Macroscopic | Engineering, thermodynamics software | Accurate real gas properties |
