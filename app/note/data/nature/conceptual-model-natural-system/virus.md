# Virus

> **Viruses are genetic** material (DNA or RNA) surrounded by a protein coat; they require a host cell for replication.
>

> A **virus** can be described as a **biological system**, but it does not fit neatly into traditional categories of living or non-living systems. Instead, it represents a unique type of system with characteristics of both.
