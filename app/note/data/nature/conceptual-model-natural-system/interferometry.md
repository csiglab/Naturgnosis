# Interferometry

> A **technique** for measuring changes in physical quantities (e.g., distances, refractive index, or phase differences) by analyzing the interference patterns of waves, often light or radio waves.
>

## Uses

> …
>
- **Scientific Research**: Gravitational wave detection (e.g., LIGO and Virgo), quantum mechanics, and astronomy.
- **Engineering**: Surface analysis, optical testing, and precision alignment.
- **Metrology**: Measuring refractive index changes, distances, or material properties.
