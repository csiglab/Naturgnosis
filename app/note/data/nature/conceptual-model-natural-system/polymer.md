# Polymer

> A **"polymer"** is a large molecule composed of repeating subunits called monomers, bonded together to form a long chain structure.
>

> **Chemists** study **polymers** through various experimental and theoretical approaches, including polymer synthesis, characterization techniques such as **spectroscopy** and **microscopy**, analysis of physical and chemical properties, investigation of **polymerization mechanisms**, and computational modeling to understand their structure, properties, and behavior.
>

> **Research Problems**: Investigating the development of biodegradable polymers derived from renewable resources to address environmental concerns surrounding plastic pollution.
>
