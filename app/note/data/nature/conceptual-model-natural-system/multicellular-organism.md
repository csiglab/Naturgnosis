## Cell-Cell Connection / Interactions

> The Structures and Mechanisms Linking Cells in Multicellular Organisms
>

> In multicellular organisms like humans, cells are connected and organized into tissues and organs through various **physical structures** and **chemical interactions**.
>
- **Extracellular Matrix (ECM):** The ECM is a network of proteins and carbohydrates located outside cells.
- **Cell-Cell Junctions:** These specialized structures directly connect neighboring cells and allow communication or structural support.
- **Cell Adhesion Molecules (CAMs):** Proteins on the cell surface mediate adhesion to other cells or the ECM.
- **Cytoskeletal Connections:** The cytoskeleton of neighboring cells is often linked via junctions (e.g., desmosomes or adherens junctions), providing mechanical stability.
