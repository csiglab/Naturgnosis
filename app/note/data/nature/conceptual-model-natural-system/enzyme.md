# Enzyme

> Enzymes are **biological molecules** that catalyze chemical reactions within cells.
>

> Biochemists study the structure and function of enzymes and their role in cellular processes.
>

| **Type of Enzyme** | **Function** | **Examples** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hydrolases** | Enzymes that catalyze the hydrolysis (breakdown) of bonds using water. | Amylase (breaks down starch), Lipase (breaks down fats) |
| **Ligases** | Enzymes that catalyze the joining of two molecules by forming a covalent bond, typically requiring energy. | DNA ligase (joins DNA strands), Pyruvate ligase |
| **Oxidoreductases** | Enzymes that catalyze oxidation-reduction reactions, involving the transfer of electrons between molecules. | Dehydrogenase (removes hydrogen), Oxidase (adds oxygen) |
| **Transferases** | Enzymes that catalyze the transfer of a functional group (such as a methyl or phosphate group) from one molecule to another. | Aminotransferase (transfers amino groups), Kinase (transfers phosphate groups) |
| **Isomerases** | Enzymes that catalyze the rearrangement of atoms within a molecule, converting it into its isomeric form. | Glucose isomerase (converts glucose to fructose) |
| **Lyases** | Enzymes that catalyze the addition or removal of groups to form double bonds or other structures without hydrolysis or oxidation. | Pyruvate decarboxylase (removes CO2 from pyruvate), Aldolase (splits a molecule into two) |
| **Hydrolases** | Enzymes that catalyze the breakdown of molecules through the addition of water (hydrolysis). | Protease (breaks down proteins), Phosphatase (removes phosphate groups) |
| **Translocases** | Enzymes involved in the movement of ions or molecules across cell membranes or within compartments of cells. | ATPase (pumps ions across membranes), Proton pump |
