# Chemical Formula

> A **chemical formula** is a notation that uses element symbols and numbers to represent the composition of a chemical compound, indicating the types and quantities of atoms present.
>

> The **limitation of chemical formulas** is that they represent only the **types and proportions of atoms** in a compound, **without providing information on the molecule's three-dimensional structure**, bond types, or spatial arrangement of atoms.
>
