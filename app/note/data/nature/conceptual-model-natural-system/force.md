# Force

> **Force** is a physical quantity that causes an object to accelerate or deform, characterized by its magnitude and direction.
>

> **Work transfers energy**: it is the mechanism by which energy moves between systems or transforms within a system.
>

> In physics, a **force** is a vector quantity that describes the interaction between objects, causing a change in their motion or shape, mathematically defined by **Newton's Second Law** as **$\mathbf{F} = \frac{d\mathbf{p}}{dt}$**, where $\mathbf{F}$ is force, $\mathbf{p}$ is momentum, and $t$ is time.
>
