# Isomorphism

> Isomorphism refers to a **structural similarity** or **equivalence between two systems**, where the elements and relationships within one system can be mapped onto those of the other system while preserving their **essential properties**.
>

> **Duality** is a concept in physics where two **seemingly distinct theories** or descriptions are **actually equivalent** under **certain transformations or conditions**.
>
