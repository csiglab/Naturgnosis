# Extracellular Matrix

> The **extracellular matrix (ECM)** is a complex network of proteins, glycoproteins, and polysaccharides that provides structural support and biochemical signaling to surrounding cells, regulating processes like growth, differentiation, and tissue repair in multicellular organisms.
