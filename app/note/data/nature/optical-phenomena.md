# Optical Phenomena

> **Optical Phenomena** are interactions between electromagnetic waves in the optical spectrum (approximately 200 nm to 3 mm) and matter, governed by wave optics, ray optics, and quantum electrodynamics, encompassing refraction, diffraction, interference, polarization, scattering, and nonlinear optical effects.
>

> **Optics** is the branch of physics that studies the generation, propagation, manipulation, and detection of electromagnetic radiation in the optical domain, utilizing principles from classical wave theory, geometrical optics, and quantum mechanics to describe phenomena such as reflection, refraction, dispersion, and wave-particle duality.
>
