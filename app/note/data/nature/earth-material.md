# Natural Abiogenic

> Natural abiogenic read as all physical matter not made by life: minerals, rocks, sediments,
> soils, waters, and air — individuated by composition, structure, and phase behavior rather
> than by use or price. (File name `earth-material.md` is kept stable so existing
> `?n=nature/earth-material.md` links and node `noteRef`s keep resolving; the canonical human
> term lives in this heading.) Parent instances [Nature](note.html?n=nature/nature.md),
> [Earth](note.html?n=nature/earth.md), and
> [Natural Material](note.html?n=nature/natural-material.md); sibling
> [Natural Biogenic](note.html?n=nature/biogenic-material.md); composed of
> [Mineral](note.html?n=nature/mineral.md) kinds; exemplified by the worked
> [Quarry](note.html?n=nature/quarry.md). Worked per
> [How to decompose any natural instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Substance / Matter` at meso level of organization: a stuff-kind individuated by mineral
composition, texture, and phase behavior, recurring across crustal systems (strata, massifs,
basins, soils) — here archetyped by limestone at meso scale. Entered via the composition
spine (`Natural Object -> Building Block -> Substance / Matter`): a **limestone bed** is the
building-block occurrence, the earth material is the substance-kind it instantiates. Same
name at micro scale (a thin section, a [Mineral](note.html?n=nature/mineral.md) grain) and
macro scale (a formation) denotes distinct nodes — record the level per instance; minerals
decompose as the child kind in `mineral.md`.

### What is this natural instance?

**Natural abiogenic**: any physical matter not made by life — minerals, rocks, sediments,
soils, natural waters, and air — e.g. **calcite**, **limestone**,
**basalt**, **granite**, **sandstone**, **shale**, **soil**. The cut includes the material
body, its mineral constituents, fabric, pore space, and resident pore life at meso scale;
it excludes extraction machinery and products (technical/production readings), tenure and
price (social reading), and classification standards themselves (epistemic scaffolding) —
those are secondary readings (see below), each with its own tree.

Limitation checklist (ontic properties of the segment, not method failures): Complexity
(mineral-fluid-biota coupling), Nonlinearity (failure and dissolution thresholds),
Uncertainty (hidden joints, buried contacts), Scale (grain to formation), Partial
Observability (subsurface), Non-Repeatability (one burial and exhumation history),
Many-Body coupling (grains, fluids, roots). Chaos, Emergence, Irreducibility, Measurement
Disturbance, Data Availability, and Computational Complexity apply only where the concrete
body shows them — record per instance, do not assume.

Secondary readings (kept as prose, never merged into the tree above): readable as technique —
drill-blast-crush-screen, sawing, dewatering, and soil-working methods and their products
([Technical Abiogenic Material](note.html?n=technique/technical-abiogenic-material.md))
(decompose under the technicarum grammar); readable as production resource — the material as aggregate product
with grades and firm (decompose under the socialium grammar; if it plays a production role
it is a Social node tagged `production-view`); readable as epistemic — petrographic
standards and stratigraphic scales (decompose under the epistemicarum grammar). The
instrument technically, the formation naturally
([ambiguity-resolution](note.html?n=meta/ambiguity-resolution.md)). Biogenic coatings and
admixtures belong to [Natural Biogenic](note.html?n=nature/biogenic-material.md).
