# Mineral

> Mineral read naturally: the naturally occurring crystalline solid kinds — individuated by
> composition and structure — that compose [Natural Abiogenic](note.html?n=nature/earth-material.md)
> aggregates within [Earth](note.html?n=nature/earth.md) and [Nature](note.html?n=nature/nature.md).
> What remains when assays, mines, firms, and prices are set aside. Worked per
> [How to decompose any natural instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Substance / Matter` at micro level of organization: a stuff-kind individuated by chemistry
and crystal structure with characteristic phase behavior, recurring across earth materials
(calcite in limestone, quartz in sandstone and granite, olivine in basalt). Entered via the
composition spine (`Natural Object -> Building Block -> Substance / Matter`): a **calcite
grain** is the building-block occurrence, the mineral is the substance-kind it instantiates.
Same name at meso scale (a vein) and macro scale (a carbonate platform) denotes distinct
nodes — record the level per instance.

### What is this natural instance?

**Mineral**: a naturally occurring, typically crystalline solid with a characteristic chemical
composition and ordered atomic arrangement — e.g. **quartz**, **calcite**, **feldspar**,
**olivine**, **pyrite**. The cut includes the crystal body, its composition, lattice,
defects, inclusions, and growth zoning at micro scale; it excludes the aggregates it composes
(→ `earth-material.md`), the instruments that identify it (technical reading), the tenures
and markets priced around it (social reading), and the classification standards themselves
(epistemic scaffolding) — those are secondary readings (see below), each with its own tree.

Limitation checklist (ontic properties of the segment, not method failures): Complexity
(lattice-fluid-defect coupling), Nonlinearity (nucleation thresholds), Uncertainty (buried
growth conditions), Scale (unit cell to vein system), Partial Observability (in-situ growth
environments), Non-Repeatability (one crystallization history), Many-Body coupling (ions,
fluids, surfaces). Chaos, Emergence, Irreducibility, Measurement Disturbance, Data
Availability, and Computational Complexity apply only where the concrete occurrence shows
them — record per instance, do not assume.

Secondary readings (kept as prose, never merged into the tree above): readable as technique —
crushing, assaying, and synthesizing methods and the instruments that identify lattice and
composition (decompose under the technicarum grammar); readable as commodity — the mineral
as ore or gem with grade, firm, and price (decompose under the socialium grammar; if it
plays a production role it is a Social node tagged `production-view`); readable as epistemic —
mineralogical standards and structure refinements (decompose under the epistemicarum
grammar). The instrument technically, the formation naturally
([ambiguity-resolution](note.html?n=meta/ambiguity-resolution.md)).
