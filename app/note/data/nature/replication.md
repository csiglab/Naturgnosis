# Replication

> A fundamental building block of diverse biological life is high-fidelity replication. The basic reproduction unit is the DNA molecule, which provides a blueprint for the offspring to be built from physical building blocks. There are a variety of replication methods, but most can be lumped into sexual and asexual.
