# Nature

> Nature read as the whole of observer-independent reality: everything that exists whether or
> not it is inquired into — physical fields and matter, chemical substances, living
> organization, earth systems, processes and phenomena, quantities and constraints. The
> epistemic scaffolding used to describe it (operators, standards, frameworks, artifacts,
> methods) belongs to Epistemic Space; transformation methods belong to technique; actors and
> institutions belong to the social reading. This note holds the natural reading only. Worked per
> [How to decompose any natural instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).
> Presupposed definitions (`Reality`, segments, levels of organization, the ontology/epistemology
> dual) live in the epistemicarum guide and are restated nowhere here.

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Complex System` at macro level of organization: a bounded-by-inquiry segment of all
observer-independent furniture with interacting components (matter, life, earth bodies,
fields), inputs/outputs (solar influx, radiogenic heat, meteoritic input, radiative loss),
and feedback (climate — weathering — biosphere; mantle — crust — surface) whose collective
behavior resists component-level reduction. It binds `(root) -> <<Natural Element>>` as the
generic system all other natural notes specialize.

### What is this natural instance?

**Nature**: the constraint space that resists, confirms, and outruns every representation —
the totality of natural systems, constituents, living organization, manifestations,
quantities, and constraints studied across the physical, chemical, biological, and earth
domains. The cut includes every piece of reality at every level of organization (micro
interactions, meso subsystems, macro emergent patterns); it excludes the templates used to
parse it (segment boundaries, variables, scales, representation forms — those are epistemic
scaffolding), the methods that transform it (technical reading), and the actors that govern
or price it (social reading). Each child note below decomposes one region of this root;
full expansion lives there, not here.

Limitation checklist (ontic properties of the segment, not method failures): Complexity
(coupled physical-chemical-biotic systems at every scale), Nonlinearity (thresholds and
tipping behavior), Uncertainty (unresolved initial and boundary conditions), Chaos (weather,
orbital details), Emergence (life, mind, climate regimes), Scale (micro interactions to
macro patterns), Data Availability (sparse deep-time and deep-space records), Computational
Complexity (many-body fluids, climate ensembles), Irreducibility (turbulence, ecosystems),
Partial Observability (interiors, deep time), Non-Repeatability (one realised history),
Measurement Disturbance (probe-coupled micro-states), Many-Body coupling (fields, fluids,
biota). Record per child instance which ones it exhibits — do not assume all apply
everywhere.

Secondary readings (kept as prose, never merged into the tree above): readable as technique —
every method that transforms or measures nature (sensors, drills, reactors, breeding,
geoengineering) decomposes under the technicarum grammar; readable as social — every actor,
institution, tenure, and market drawn around natural bodies (firms, parks, quotas, prices)
decomposes under the socialium grammar; readable as epistemic — every model, standard, and
warrant about nature decomposes under the epistemicarum grammar. The instrument technically,
the formation naturally (`guideline/ambiguity_resolution.md`).
