# Self-Preservation

> Without a strong self-preservation instinct in an organism’s DNA, it would tend to disappear over time, thus eliminating that DNA. While cooperation is another important model, the self-preservation instinct is vital in all organisms and can cause violent, erratic, and destructive behavior for those around them.
