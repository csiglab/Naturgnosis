# Atmosphere

> **Role**: It regulates temperature, weather, and climate, and plays a critical role in respiration, photosynthesis, and the water cycle.
>

> **Description**: The atmosphere is a thin layer of gases surrounding the Earth, primarily composed of nitrogen (78%), oxygen (21%), and trace gases such as carbon dioxide and argon. It is an open system because it exchanges energy and matter with space and other Earth systems.
>
