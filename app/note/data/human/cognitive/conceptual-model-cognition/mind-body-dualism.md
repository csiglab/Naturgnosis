# Mind-body Dualism

> **Mind-body dualism** is the philosophical belief that the **mind** and **body** are fundamentally distinct kinds of substances or realities, often attributing the mind to a non-physical realm and the body to the **physical world**.
>

> **Mind-body dualism** is a **metaphysical model** concerning the nature of the relationship between the mind and the **physical body**, proposing that they are two fundamentally different **kinds of entities**.
