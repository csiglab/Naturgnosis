# Object

> Object:  An **entity** or thing that can be represented, perceived, or reasoned about. Objects can be concrete (physical things) or abstract (mathematical structures, ideas). Objects are the primary targets for mental or formal representation.
>

> Abstract Object: A **non-physical object** that exists conceptually rather than materially. It can be a mathematical entity, a theoretical construct, or a generalized idea (e.g., “number 7,” “justice,” “triangle”).
>

> Concept: A **mental representation** or cognitive construct that captures the essential features or structure of one or more objects. Concepts allow humans to think, reason, and generalize about objects.
>

> Category: A **class or grouping** of objects that share common properties or features. Categories organize knowledge by grouping similar objects under a common label.
>

> Property: A **feature, attribute, or characteristic** that an object or category can possess. Properties describe *what an object is like* and are often used to define categories.

## Property

> **Simple properties** (mass, shape, temperature) are **intrinsic and physical**.
>

> **Complex, non-physical properties** are **emergent, relational, conceptual, or functional**, existing primarily in **cognition, social systems, or abstract reasoning**.
