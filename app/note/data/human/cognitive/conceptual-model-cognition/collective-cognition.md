# Collective Cognition

> Collective cognition is the emergent process by which a group of interacting agents—such as individuals, organizations, or computational entities—jointly perceive, process, and integrate distributed information to generate shared knowledge, make decisions, or solve problems that exceed individual capabilities.
>

## List of Methods

| **Method** | **Description** | **Typical Use Cases** | **Strengths** | **Challenges / Limitations** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Prediction Markets** | Markets where participants trade contracts tied to event outcomes to aggregate beliefs. | Forecasting elections, product success, events | Incentive-aligned, aggregates diverse information | Requires liquidity, susceptible to manipulation |
| **Wisdom of Crowds** | Aggregating independent judgments from a diverse group to improve accuracy. | Estimating quantities, forecasting simple events | Simple, effective with independent diverse inputs | Vulnerable to correlated errors, groupthink |
| **Delphi Method** | Iterative surveys with anonymous experts providing forecasts and feedback to reach consensus. | Expert forecasting, policy analysis | Structured expert input, reduces bias | Time-consuming, depends on expert selection |
| **Crowdsourcing** | Collecting inputs, solutions, or data from a large group, often via online platforms. | Problem solving, innovation, data labeling | Access to large, diverse knowledge pool | Quality control, coordination complexity |
| **Collective Intelligence Platforms** | Tools that integrate multiple user contributions (e.g., wikis, forums, Q&A sites). | Knowledge sharing, collaborative problem solving | Collaborative, scalable, continuous improvement | Coordination issues, vandalism, unequal contributions |
| **Social Sensing** | Using social media and sensor data to infer collective states or trends. | Sentiment analysis, disaster detection | Real-time data, broad reach | Noise, misinformation, privacy concerns |
| **Consensus Decision Making** | Group process designed to reach agreement among members through discussion and negotiation. | Organizational decisions, community planning | Inclusive, fosters commitment | Time-intensive, may suppress minority views |
| **Swarm Intelligence** | Algorithms or systems inspired by social insects for distributed problem-solving. | Optimization, robotics, network routing | Robust, scalable, adaptive | Translating natural heuristics to human contexts |
| **Collaborative Filtering** | Recommender systems aggregating preferences to infer collective tastes. | E-commerce, media recommendations | Personalized predictions, scalable | Cold start problem, popularity bias |
