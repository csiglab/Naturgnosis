# Visual System

> This includes the **neural pathways** and **brain regions** responsible for **processing visual** information from the eyes.
>

## ¿Does Blind People Have Mental Imaginary?

> **Blind people** can have mental imagery, but the nature of that imagery depends on whether they were born blind (congenitally blind) or became blind later in life.
>
1. **Congenitally Blind Individuals**: People who are blind from birth do not have visual mental imagery, as they have never experienced visual perception. However, they can form mental images using their other senses, such as touch, sound, smell, and spatial awareness. For example, they can imagine the texture of objects, the layout of a room, or the sound of a familiar environment.
2. **People Who Became Blind Later in Life**: Those who became blind after having sight often retain the ability to visualize objects, scenes, or people in their mind's eye. Their mental imagery may still include visual elements, though it may fade over time depending on the duration of blindness.
