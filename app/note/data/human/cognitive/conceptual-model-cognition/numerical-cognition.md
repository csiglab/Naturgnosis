# Numerical Cognition

> **Numerical cognition** – the broad field that studies how humans (and sometimes animals) perceive, process, and understand numbers.
>

> **Approximate Number System (ANS)** – an innate, non-verbal system that allows estimation of quantity (e.g., knowing that a group of 8 dots is more than a group of 5 without counting).
>

> **Exact Number System** – develops later with language and education; enables counting, arithmetic, and symbolic number manipulation.
>

> **Subitizing** – the ability to instantly recognize small quantities (typically 1–4 items) without counting.
>

## Human Basic Capabilities

> Examples of human intuitions are the number sense, the length sense, the size sense, etc.
>

| Intuitive Mathematical Ability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Number Sense | The ability to recognize quantities and make estimations about numbers. |
| Spatial Reasoning | The capability to understand and manipulate shapes and objects in space. |
| Magnitude Estimation | The ability to judge the size, length, or amount of something without precise measurement. |
| Pattern Recognition | The ability to identify and predict sequences or regularities in sets of numbers or objects. |
| Proportional Thinking | It is understanding the relationships between quantities and how they change about one another. |
| Geometry Sense | An inherent understanding of shapes, their properties, and their relationships to each other. |
| Approximation | The ability to make close estimates of numbers or quantities quickly and efficiently. |
