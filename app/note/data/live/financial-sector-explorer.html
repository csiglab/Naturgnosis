How to read this sunburst

Rings follow depth — sectors around the center, categories on the middle ring, subsectors on the outer ring — and arc width follows subsector count. Color is the top-level sector. The center disc names the current focus; clicking it zooms one level out. Hover any arc for its full path and counts.

When is this explorer stale?

Data is a snapshot embedded in this page — re-extract the TAXO block if the sector taxonomy changes (new charters, instruments, or sectors).
