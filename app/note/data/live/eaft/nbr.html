Formulation

What epistemic element type does this instance belong to?

Concrete Epistemic Artifact — one realized number encoding affirmations about quantity, magnitude, order, or relation — produced by applying an Epistemic Operator (measurement and calculation), and usable as an Epistemic Standard (constraint satisfaction). Schema: Philosophia Artium Epistemicarum et Operis.

What is this epistemic instance?

A Number: a formal symbolic system with defined syntax, operations, and ordering relations, mapped to quantities, positions, or relations in conceptual or physical reality — for encoding measurement, representing order, expressing relations, and enabling calculation.

What grounds this reading?

It resolves the epistemic gap of coarse-to-fine transition — transforming qualitative, coarse-grained characterizations of reality into precise, quantitative representations, enabling prediction, comparison, and manipulation.
