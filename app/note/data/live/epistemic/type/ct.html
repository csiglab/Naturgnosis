Formulation

What epistemic element type does this instance belong to?

Concrete Epistemic Artifact — a computed validity verdict about one dataset's center — produced by applying an Epistemic Operator (kernel density estimation, bootstrap resampling, structural alignment), and usable as an Epistemic Standard (center-viability score and diagnostic flags). Schema: Philosophia Artium Epistemicarum et Operis.

What is this epistemic instance?

An interactive engine comparing central-tendency estimators across three views — density and metrics, epistemic validity, counterfactual lab — with an affirmation matrix recording what each metric represents (location, variance, multimodality, tail risk).

What grounds this reading?

It resolves the epistemic gap of estimator choice under structural stress — perturbing distributions and observing metric drift under skew and contamination to test which summaries survive. It preserves each metric's breakdown behavior; it destroys the full distributional detail that point summaries collapse away.

Diagnostic questions

What flags a non-viable center?

Skewness, kurtosis, modality, and tail index feed the center-viability score — the Diagnostic Flags panel names whichever metric breaks the reading.

How fragile is the verdict?

Perturb skew and contamination in the Counterfactual Simulator and watch Metric Drift: the distance each metric moves from baseline before the verdict flips.
