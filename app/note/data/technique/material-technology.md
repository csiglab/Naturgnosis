# Material Technology

> **Material Technology** is the study, design, and processing of metals, ceramics, polymers, and composites into functional objects and products. Source: Produceologia `docs/Production/Technique/Catalog/Material.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

### What is this technical instance?

> Structural manipulation of matter: alloy formulation and polymer chemistry plus composite or alloy components, realized in smelters and extrusion lines, yielding engineered materials and structural components across all consuming domains.
