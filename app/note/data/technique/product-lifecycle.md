# Product Lifecycle

> A **Product Lifecycle** is the temporal trajectory of a product from conception through production, operation, and retirement: the staged path a portfolio offer travels. Source: Produceologia `docs/Firm/Toolkit/Product/` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What technical element type does this technical instance belong to?

**Product Lifecycle belongs to the `Technical Lifecycle` technical element type — the temporal trajectory of a technical object from conception through production, operation, maintenance, modification, and retirement.**

### What is this technical instance?

> The lifecycle stages product work: concept and MVP (prototyping-first strategy testing supply-demand fit), growth and maturity (production at scale), retirement (withdrawal and replacement). Market validation gates each transition.
