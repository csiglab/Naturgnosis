# Technical Abiogenic Material

> **Technical Abiogenic Material** is worked matter from mineral feedstocks: steel, glass,
> cement, concrete, silicon, and ceramics — substances whose origin is non-living and whose
> transformation is furnace-, kiln-, and reactor-intensive. Child of
> [Technical Material](note.html?n=technique/technical-material.md); feedstock reading in
> [Natural Abiogenic](note.html?n=nature/earth-material.md). Worked per
> [How to decompose any technical instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-artium-technicarum-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What technical element type does this technical instance belong to?

**Technical Abiogenic Material belongs to the `Technical Resource` technical element type —
a same-type instance family scoped inside the Technical Material resource (No-repetition
exception): inputs consumed by technical activity, individuated by mineral feedstock.**

### What is this technical instance?

> Mineral feedstocks made technical: ore smelted to steel, sand melted to glass, limestone
> calcined to cement and bound to concrete, quartz reduced and grown to silicon. The cut
> includes the worked substance, its feedstock lineage back to the mineral formation, the
> high-temperature mechanisms that transformed it, and the parameters and constraints
> governing its use; it excludes the mineral formation itself (natural reading) and grades,
> firms, and prices (social reading).

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this technical instance?

> Boundary: the mineral-feedstock family, decomposed at middle depth — root, exemplar
> objects, mechanisms, parameters, and constraints.
