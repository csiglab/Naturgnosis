# Technical Biogenic Material

> **Technical Biogenic Material** is worked matter from living feedstocks: lumber, paper,
> leather, charcoal, natural rubber, and bioplastics — substances whose origin is
> presently or recently living and whose biogenic character survives transformation. Child of
> [Technical Material](note.html?n=technique/technical-material.md); feedstock reading in
> [Natural Biogenic](note.html?n=nature/biogenic-material.md). Petroleum-derived polymers are
> deliberately excluded here — their origin is ambiguous, so they classify at the parent
> (see the parent QA). Worked per
> [How to decompose any technical instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-artium-technicarum-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What technical element type does this technical instance belong to?

**Technical Biogenic Material belongs to the `Technical Resource` technical element type —
a same-type instance family scoped inside the Technical Material resource (No-repetition
exception): inputs consumed by technical activity, individuated by living feedstock.**

### What is this technical instance?

> Biogenic feedstocks made technical: wood sawn to lumber, pulp pressed to paper, hide
> tanned to leather, biomass charred to charcoal and fermented to bioplastic. The cut
> includes the worked substance, its feedstock lineage back to the living formation, the
> mechanisms that transformed it, and the parameters, standards, and constraints governing
> its use; it excludes the living formation itself (natural reading) and grades, firms, and
> prices (social reading).

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this technical instance?

> Boundary: the living-feedstock family, decomposed at middle depth — root, exemplar
> objects, mechanisms, parameters, standards, and constraints.
