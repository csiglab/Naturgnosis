# Rare Earth Elements

> **Rare Earth Elements** are the seventeen chemically similar elements enabling high-performance magnets, phosphors, catalysts, and lasers. Source: Produceologia `docs/Foundation/Rare-Earth.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What technical element type does this technical instance belong to?

**Rare Earth Elements belongs to the `Constitutive Technical Object` technical element type — stuff-kinds constituting high-performance objects.**

### What is this technical instance?

> Nd, Pr, Dy, and Tb in NdFeB magnets for EV motors and wind turbines, SmCo for high-temperature aerospace and military use, Eu, Y, and Tb phosphors for LEDs and displays, Ce and La catalysts, polishes, and batteries, Gd, Lu, Er, and Ho for MRI, PET, and fiber-optic lasers.
