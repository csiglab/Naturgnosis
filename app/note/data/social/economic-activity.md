# Economic Activity

> **Economic Activity** is the recurrent, organized doing through which units provision goods and services — the social element type that scopes producing: farming, mining, manufacturing, transport, retail, care.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Economic Activity belongs to the `Economic Activity` social element type (Economic category)** — recurrent organized doings provisioning goods and services. Its layer is **Ontic**: the doing exists extra-mentally. Its facet is economic.

Readable with its acting side, the `Producer` (unit or role that performs the activity), and with its Noetic classification rows (`Producer Type`, `Industry`, `Sector`, `Producer Taxonomy`) which tag rather than own. `Market` and `Product` are its sibling Economic types: activity produces products, products enter markets.

### What is this social instance?

> One provisioning activity class: the doings that turn inputs into goods and services and carry them to where they are used. The activity is distinct from the actor (the producer), the output (the product), and the arena (the market); a decomposition types each separately and cross-links.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one economic activity, decomposed at middle depth — activity, producer, and classification rows only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names an activity class, a producer, or a classification rank.
>
> Identity: Instance Tree Path is the stable identifier of each row; every path is unique.
