# Note Corpus Map

> The social section of Naturgnosis: the note corpus formalizing the elements of social reality — the units, bonds, and coordinators that let an agent understand and act. Governing grammar: [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md). Element types: Interaction Unit, Social Compound, Social Relation, Social Action / Activity, Social Event, Social Process, Social State, Social Property; strata: Ontic, Synontic, Noetic, Multi.
