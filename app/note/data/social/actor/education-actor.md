# Education Actor

> The **Education Actor** is the knowledge-transmission actor of the social space: a staffed body, warranted by a court, a temple, or a mosque, that carries certified knowledge across generations — teaching beyond the elementary, examining, and supplying trained people to the power that sponsors it. It is one of the oldest recurring actor forms in civilization, appearing independently in Egypt, India, China, Greece, Persia, the Islamic world, Korea, Japan, and Vietnam — long before the autonomous corporation of scholars that is the medieval European university. This note decomposes the actor through its oldest attested instances, worked per [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md), with the Chinese [Taixue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taixue) as the worked exemplar.

> The instances follow the [List of oldest higher-learning institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_oldest_higher-learning_institutions) and honor its framing: these bodies are *not* Western-style universities. Sponsorship — by courts, by mosques and monasteries, by observatories and hospitals — is the comparison axis, and where the record is thin (a temple campus known from decrees, a North African university known from ruins), the note says so rather than presenting an even coverage it does not have.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**The education actor belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound unit with identity, membership, and rules.**

It is a warranted teaching body: a head and masters, a student membership admitted by examination or recommendation, a curriculum, and a site — whether a temple campus, a monastery valley, a mosque complex, or a walled academy quarter. Transmission under warrant is what makes it *this* actor rather than its neighbors: the school run as a firm transmits for fees, the monastery transmits devotion, and only the warranted body converts study into rank recognized by the realm. It is a smaller and more purpose-bound unit than the court that warrants it, which is why the branches below carry teaching, examination, and the graduate relation alongside endowment and discipline.

The `Institution` reading — the examination warrant, the degree or rank it confers, and the scholarly norms it polices — is declared as a secondary reading and decomposed below as `Legitimacy`, `Norm / Regulation`, and `Right / Obligation`.

### What is this social instance?

> An education actor: a warranted body that teaches the advanced curriculum of its civilization and converts students into office-holders, clergy, physicians, or masters — doctors of the Five Classics for Han bureaucracy, gate-tested residents of Nalanda, ijazah-bearing mosque students, examination-ranked Confucian scholars — each under a warrant from the power that feeds it. Below it is decomposed through its oldest attested instances, drawn from the [List of oldest higher-learning institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_oldest_higher-learning_institutions).

**Layer.** **Ontic** — the schools, their masters, their students, their endowments, and their examination power exist extra-mentally. Their curricula and commentaries are Noetic instruments; the expectation that a realm can reproduce its own officials and learned men is the shared anticipation they serve.

**Facet.** **Ideational**, by dominant mechanism, with a normative face: these bodies are reorganized by the production and certification of learned persons, and they are *only* partly market-facing actors. Teaching and examination are the core; research, healing, and stargazing are functions attached to it.

**Boundary.** The warranted body: warrants, masters, students, curricula, examinations, discipline, endowments, relations, and present state (surviving or ruined). The knowledge taught is epistemica; the courts, caliphates, and ministries that warrant them are social; the endowment lands and stipends touch production. Student street politics — the Southern Song university boys toppling prefects — belong here as action, not as an idea about action.

Lineage: temple schools of the Old Kingdom and the monastic valley of Taxila → the Athenian Academy and Lyceum, the Alexandrian Mouseion → the Jixia Academy and the Han **Taixue** of 124 BC → Nalanda and its Pala successors → Gondishapur carrying Athens east after 529 → the Guozijian system and its Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese diffusion → the mosque-universities of Qarawiyyin, Zitouna, and Al-Azhar, several of them still teaching.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: a middle-depth decomposition of one actor — its oldest attested instances grouped by civilizational family, plus the spine that states what the instances share (warrant, activity, expectation, and the continuity rule the whole actor is bounded by). No institution is re-decomposed inside its family: each leaf resolves to the historical body, and repeating a body's internal life inside the actor note is duplication, not structure.
>
> Typing reads from the path: the root binds `Collective / Organization`; each family branch nests a `Collective / Organization` scoping that tradition's bodies under the recursion rule for that composite. Bare type rows group and never terminate a branch; every leaf is a **bold** social instance.
