# Research Actor

> A **Research Actor** is a bounded collective that produces or funds inquiry — the research-functional subtype of [Actor](note.html?n=social/actor/actor.md): academies and national laboratories, government research agencies and funding councils, public research institutes, enterprise research labs, university research units, and the research groups they host. It is the actor counterpart of [Research Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/research.md): the activity says what inquiry does, this element says who carries and pays for it.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Research Actor belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type (Agents category)** — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules, carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices. It is the research-functional subtype of Actor, on the same footing as the [Firm](note.html?n=social/actor/firm.md) (economic) and the [Education Actor](note.html?n=social/actor/education-actor.md) (teaching).

Its layer is **Ontic** where the actor exists extra-mentally (institutes, laboratories, agencies with staff, premises, budgets, and address) and **Synontic** where it is constituted by recognition (a scientific academy's standing, a learned society's mandate, a funding council's authority to allocate). Its facet is **research (scientific)** — the dominant causal mechanism is the production or funding of warranted knowledge, not exchange value and not the transmission of certified knowledge.

It is not an `Agency` (the capacity to form intentions and select action — what the actor acts *with*), not a `Social Role` (the expectation-tag it occupies), and not an `Institution` (the stabilized rules — peer review, grant conditions — it acts *within* and hosts). It is distinct from a [Firm](note.html?n=social/actor/firm.md) (converting capability into exchangeable value), from an [Education Actor](note.html?n=social/actor/education-actor.md) (transmitting certified knowledge across generations), and from the [Research Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/research.md) it performs; it hosts research groups, occupies research roles, and inhabits research institutions, but is the carrier, never the carried.

Readable secondarily as an `Interaction Unit` in the plain sense: a bounded entity capable of interaction, nested in national research systems and funded collaboration networks. That secondary reading is prose, not a second root; the tree below is typed once.

### What is this social instance?

> A research actor fixes *who does and funds inquiry, and how*: the boundary of the body, the governing council that forms intention and selects a research direction, the membership or workforce it carries, the funding and collaboration relations binding it to the state, industry, universities, and other bodies, and the institutions — peer review, evaluation, priority-setting — through which it sustains inquiry. Its boundary is deliberately loose: the same body may be publicly chartered, privately endowed, or firm-embedded.

> The typology below classifies research actors by constitution — public research institute, national laboratory, government research agency, funding council, academy or learned society, enterprise research lab, university research unit, independent foundation, research group — and locates the concrete bodies already worked in this module. The `research/` directory groups those instances by ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code, with a nested parent-body folder where a system has a peak body (`cn/cas`, `es/csic`, `fr/cnrs`, `it/cnr`, `us/nsf`, `sg/astar`, `dk/dnrf`, `fi/rcf`, `nl/nwo`).

**Layer.** **Ontic** — the institutes, laboratories, agencies, staff, instruments, and endowments exist extra-mentally. Their research programmes and classifications are Noetic instruments; the standing of an academy and the authority of a funding council are Synontic.

**Facet.** **Research (scientific)**, with a normative and an economic face: the actor is reorganized by the production and funding of warranted knowledge, is warranted by a charter or mandate, and is resourced from public budgets, endowments, or contract revenue. Economic and political readings are prose here, not separate trees.

**Boundary.** The body itself: its charter, governing council, staff and roles, programmes, funding and collaboration ties, and the institutions it hosts. The knowledge it produces is epistemic (cross-linked, not owned); the market it sells into is social; the instruments it buys are technical.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one research-actor family — its subtype classification, the spine constituents it shares, and the national systems whose concrete bodies are already documented in this module. Subtype detail already worked in a linked entry ([Laboratory](note.html?n=social/actor/laboratory.md), [Research Group](note.html?n=social/actor/research-group.md), [Enterprise Research Lab](note.html?n=social/actor/enterprise-research-lab.md), [Public Research Institutes](note.html?n=social/actor/pri.md)) is not duplicated here; the national-system branch links the instances rather than re-decomposing them.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a subtype, a national system, a concrete institute, an agency, a role, a norm, an obligation, or a practice, or when it links an already-decomposed entry.
