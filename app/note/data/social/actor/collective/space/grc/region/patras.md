# Patras

> **Patras** is the third-largest city of Greece and the principal political, economic, administrative, and cultural centre of western Greece and the Peloponnese. It is a `Region` read as `City`: a spatially and institutionally organized local nexus whose port links the peninsula to Italy.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Patras belongs to the `Region` social element type (Structural category), read as `City`** — a spatially and institutionally organized local nexus, a zone of dense recurrent interaction. Its layer is **Ontic**: the built city, its port, and its population exist extra-mentally. Its facet is social-general, with an economic (port-trade) face.

Readable secondarily as a `Jurisdiction` (the Municipality of Patras) — prose, not a second root.

### What is this social instance?

> The city of Patras: an ancient foundation (the Roman *Colonia Augusta Achaica Patrensis*) on the Gulf of Patras, with a metropolitan population of about 250,000. It couples the port — Greece's main passenger and freight gateway to Italy — the University of Patras, the Rio–Antirrio bridge, and the western Peloponnese hinterland into one local nexus. It was a European Capital of Culture in 2006.
