# Georg Heinrich Zincke

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Georg%20Heinrich%20Zincke.jpg"
     alt="Georg Heinrich Zincke"
     width="200"
     align="left"
     style="margin: 0 25px 15px 0;">

**Georg Heinrich Zincke** (1692–1768) was a German jurist and cameralist — Leipzig lecturer on cameral sciences (1740–1745), then Brunswick minister and professor at Helmstedt and the Collegium Carolinum. He systematized the young discipline (General- vs. Special-Oeconomic) and documented it bibliographically.

His *Cameralisten-Bibliothek* (1751–52) mapped the field, the *Anfangsgründe der Cameralwissenschaft* (1755) grounded it as a science beyond practical instruction, and the *Leipziger Sammlungen* (1742–67) ran it as a periodical — with the *Oeconomisches Lexicon* (1731) as its reference work.

> Zincke gave cameralism its library, its system, and its journal — the infrastructure by which Justi's generation learned the science.

<br clear="left">

*Death year follows ADB and the Helmstedt catalog (1768); English Wikipedia gives 1769.*
