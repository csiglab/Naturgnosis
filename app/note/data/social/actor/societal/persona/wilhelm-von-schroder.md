# Wilhelm von Schröder

**Wilhelm Freiherr von Schröder** (1640–1688) was a Saxon cameralist and mercantilist in Austrian service — member of the Royal Society, director of the Viennese *Manufacturhaus* on the Tabor after Becher (1677–1683), and imperial chamber councillor in Hungary. His work continues Becher's program of manufactures and commerce under an absolutist fiscal state.

His *Fürstliche Schatz- und Rentkammer* (1686, eight editions to 1835) is a classic of the literature: a central treasury relying on public loans and paper money rather than taxation, an attack on the guilds, and the doctrine that the ruler's wealth depends on the wealth and well-being of the subjects.

> Schröder links cameralist state-building to English mercantilism — manufactures protected from foreign competition as the key to national wealth.
