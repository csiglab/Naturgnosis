# KKR & Co. Inc.

> **KKR & Co. Inc.** (Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co.) is an American alternative investment firm that manages assets for institutional investors, wealth investors, family offices, and companies. It is a `Collective / Organization` — a firm bounded by identity, membership, and rules that converts financial capability into exchangeable value.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**KKR & Co. Inc. belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type (Agents category)** — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules, carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices. Its layer is **Ontic**: the firm exists extra-mentally, with staff, offices, funds, and accounts. Its facet is economic — the dominant mechanism is the allocation of capital and the management of claims.

Readable secondarily as an `Interaction Unit` nested in capital markets, and as an asset-holding entity (funds, portfolio companies). Those readings are prose; the tree below is typed once. It is distinct from the `Market` it acts in, from an `Institution` (the rules of finance it acts within), and from an `Activity` (the investing it performs).

### What is this social instance?

> An alternative asset manager: founded in 1976 by Henry Kravis, George Roberts, and Jerome Kohlberg, KKR pioneered the leveraged buyout and grew into one of the world's largest alternative investment firms, with roughly $758 billion in assets under management as of 31 March 2026. It operates across three business areas — asset management (private equity, credit, real assets, infrastructure), insurance (Global Atlantic), and strategic holdings — serving institutional investors, wealth investors, family offices, and companies. It is headquartered in New York and listed on the New York Stock Exchange.
