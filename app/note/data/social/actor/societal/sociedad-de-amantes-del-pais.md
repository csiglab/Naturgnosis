# Sociedad de Amantes del País

> A **Sociedad de Amantes del País** ("Society of Lovers of the Country") is the type of patriotic learned society through which a colonial or early-national elite organized inquiry, print, and reform — a voluntary collective actor combining a reading circle, a periodical, and a reformist program.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Sociedad de Amantes del País belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules, carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices.** It is a voluntary learned society: a membership bound by a shared program of inquiry and reform rather than by market exchange or public authority. Its layer is **Ontic** — the society existed extra-mentally, with members, premises, subscriptions, and a press. Its facet is ideational-epistemic (inquiry and print serving a patriotic program).

### What is this social instance?

> The patriotic-society form that produced the classic instances (the Lima *Sociedad de Amantes del País*, 1790s, and its *Mercurio Peruano*): a self-organized elite convening periodically, publishing a periodical, and pressing projects of economic and civic improvement on the crown or the new republic. It is distinct from a `Firm` (not market), a `Public Organization` (no delegated authority), and a plain `Social Group` (it carries membership and rules).
