# Norwegian Institute for Air Research (NILU)

> **NILU** is an independent Norwegian research foundation founded in 1969 to study air pollution, and now a climate and environmental research institute measuring the atmosphere, the contaminants in it, and the health effects of both. It runs observatories in the Arctic, Antarctica, and Norway, holds the national responsibility for air-pollution research, and is consulted by the World Bank, the WHO, and the WMO.

> It is not a state agency. NILU is a private non-profit foundation that holds a public basic grant and competes for the rest of its income, and that distinction is the reason it sits here rather than beside NIBIO as one more state institute. Framed per [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**NILU belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound unit with identity, membership, and rules.**

It is a foundation: a registered legal person (organization number 941 705 561), a board, a staff, and research groups. The foundation form is not a detail here — it is what makes the institute's independence a *rule* the body is organized to protect, rather than a property of being inside a ministry. The board appoints a chief executive; the staff are answerable to the board; the money comes from grants the institute must win.

The `Institution` reading — the stabilized configuration of roles and rules around a national responsibility, the air-pollution mandate the state leans on — is real and is declared as a secondary reading under the multi-root rule. It is decomposed below as `Legitimacy` and `Activity`; no row carries two types.

### What is this social instance?

> An independent environmental research foundation: atmospheric scientists, chemists, and technicians running measurement stations and accredited laboratories, and delivering monitoring, modelling, and assessment to the state, to industry, and to international bodies — with a third of its income from sources outside Norway and a fifth of its staff born outside it.

**Layer.** **Ontic** — the institute, its staff, its laboratories, its observatories, and its legal personality exist extra-mentally. Its measurement series are Noetic instruments; the expectation that the atmosphere can be held to account across decades is the shared anticipation that organizes the work.

**Facet.** **Ideational**, by dominant causal mechanism: NILU reorganizes itself around producing and circulating measurement knowledge, and its services are the delivery form of that knowledge rather than a market-facing business.

**Boundary.** The institute as a body: its warrants, groups, roles, activities, rules, obligations, relations, resources, run, and present state. Not the atmospheric science itself (epistemica), not the instruments it operates (technique), not the industry that buys its services (social and production).

Lineage: an institute created in 1969 for air-pollution research, when smog and acid rain made measurement a national necessity → expansion into atmospheric composition, climate change, and health effects as those fields became measurable → polar observatories, with a permanent Arctic presence from 1973 and scientific responsibility for the Zeppelin station → a service and consultancy business whose revenue now exceeds its grant, and a research position maintained largely on European and international money.
