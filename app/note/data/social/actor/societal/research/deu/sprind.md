# Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND)

> **SPRIND** — the *Bundesagentur für Sprunginnovationen* (legally SPRIND GmbH) — is Germany's federal agency for breakthrough innovation: a public, arm's-length funder that discovers research ideas with disruptive potential and develops them toward market, on the model of the US DARPA.

### What is this social instance?

> The German federal breakthrough-innovation agency: founded by notarial act in October 2019 after a 2018 government decision and a 2019 founding commission, seated in Leipzig, with the federal government (Bund) as shareholder and a mandate from the education/research and economy ministries. Led by founding director Rafael Laguna de la Vera with a ten-member supervisory board from science, business, ministries, and politics; some fifty staff and roughly €250 million in annual means against a planned €1 billion over ten years. Its instruments are start-up funding, investments and convertible loans, research-and-development funding, staged **Challenges**, and short **Funken** demonstration sprints. The SPRIND Freedom Act (passed November 2023, in force December 2023) entrusted it with the federal breakthrough-innovation task, freed its financing, and let it found subsidiaries and take equity; a 2025 evaluation judged the agency on mission. Worked projects include the GICON height wind tower (via its beventum GmbH subsidiary) and the Sovereign Tech Fund.
