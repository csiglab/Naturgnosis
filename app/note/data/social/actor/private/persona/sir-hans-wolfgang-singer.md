# Sir Hans Wolfgang Singer


> Sir **Hans Wolfgang Singer**, an influential **development economist**, emphasized the importance of international cooperation in addressing global economic disparities, advocated for policies to promote economic growth and social justice in developing countries, and is renowned for his work on the theory of **"unbalanced growth"** and the **"Prebisch-Singer thesis"** regarding terms of trade between developed and developing nations.
