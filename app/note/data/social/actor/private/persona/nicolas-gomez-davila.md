# Nicolás Gómez Dávila


![](https://i0.wp.com/theimaginativeconservative.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Nicolás-Gómez-Dávila-1.jpg?fit=600,400&ssl=1)

**Nicolás Gómez Dávila**, a Colombian writer and thinker, is best known for his unique aphoristic style, blending classical conservatism with sharp, critical insights into modernity. 

His main works include:

1. **"Escolios a un Texto Implícito" (Glosses to an Implicit Text)**: This is his principal work, composed entirely of aphorisms and reflective notes. The text itself is 'implicit' as it never directly appears; instead, Dávila's escolios (marginal notes or glosses) offer commentary on a wide range of topics, including philosophy, culture, politics, and religion. The work was published in several volumes from the 1970s to the early 1990s.
2. **"Textos I"**: A collection of various writings that further explore themes similar to those in "Escolios a un Texto Implícito." This compilation includes essays and aphorisms that delve into Dávila's critique of modern society and his conservative, sometimes reactionary, viewpoints.

Dávila's work is characterized by its brevity, depth, and a tendency to challenge conventional wisdom. His writings are highly regarded in certain intellectual circles for their incisive analysis and the elegance of their expression, although they have not always been widely accessible due to the unique nature of his work and his preference for privacy and seclusion from public life.
