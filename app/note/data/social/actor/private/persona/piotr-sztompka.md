# Piotr Sztompka


> **Piotr Sztompka'**s works focus on the dynamics of social change, the role of trust in social cohesion, cultural trauma, the interplay between **human agency** and structure, and the methodology of social sciences, significantly contributing to sociological theory and understanding of modern and postmodern societies.
> 

Here is a table summarizing some of the main ideas and contributions of Piotr Sztompka, a prominent sociologist:

| Main Idea/Concept | Description | Key Publications/Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Social Change** | Sztompka explores the dynamics, mechanisms, and factors influencing social change. | *The Sociology of Social Change* (1993) |
| **Trust and Social Bonds** | He examines the role of trust in building and maintaining social bonds and institutions. | *Trust: A Sociological Theory* (1999) |
| **Cultural Trauma** | Investigates how societies experience and react to traumatic events that affect their identity and functioning. | *Cultural Trauma and Collective Identity* (2004) |
| **Sociological Theory** | Contributions to the development and refinement of sociological theory, particularly focusing on human agency and structure. | *Sociological Dilemmas: Toward a Dialectic Paradigm* (1979) |
| **Visual Sociology** | Advocates for the use of visual methods and materials in sociological research to better understand social phenomena. | Various articles and conference presentations |
| **Sociology of Everyday Life** | Examines how daily interactions and practices contribute to the broader social structure and change. | *Society in Action: The Theory of Social Becoming* (1991) |
| **Social Capital** | Explores how social networks and relationships contribute to the development and sustainability of social capital. | *Trust: A Sociological Theory* (1999) |
| **Modernity and Postmodernity** | Analyzes the characteristics and consequences of modernity and postmodernity in contemporary societies. | Various articles and book chapters |
| **Methodology of Social Sciences** | Focuses on methodological issues in sociological research, advocating for a combination of qualitative and quantitative approaches. | Various articles and book chapters |

This table highlights the key areas of Piotr Sztompka's work, providing an overview of his contributions to sociology.
