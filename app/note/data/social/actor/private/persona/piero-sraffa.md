# Piero Sraffa


> 

| Main Idea | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Theory of Production | In his work "Production of Commodities by Means of Commodities" (1960), Sraffa develops a theory of production that challenges marginalist theories, focusing on the physical and technical relations of production. |
| Critique of Marginalist Theory | Sraffa criticized marginalist theory and the utility theory, arguing that prices and distribution cannot be determined solely by utility and marginal analysis. |
| Prices of Production | Introduced the concept of prices of production, which are the prices at which goods exchange so that capital across different sectors earns a uniform rate of profit. |
| Influence of David Ricardo | Sraffa revived and developed the ideas of David Ricardo, particularly his focus on income distribution and the labor theory of value. |
| Surplus Theory | His surplus theory focuses on the production and distribution of economic surplus in the economy, contrasting with neoclassical theories that emphasize market equilibrium. |
| Critique of Capital Theory | Sraffa criticized the neoclassical theory of capital, especially the use of an aggregate measure of capital and the notion of the marginal productivity of capital. |
| Classical Political Economy | Sraffa played a crucial role in reviving and reinterpreting classical political economy, offering an alternative to the dominant economic theory of his time. |
