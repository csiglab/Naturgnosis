# Joseph von Sonnenfels

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Joseph%20von%20Sonnenfels.jpg"
     alt="Joseph von Sonnenfels"
     width="220"
     align="left"
     style="margin: 0 25px 15px 0;">

**Joseph Freiherr von Sonnenfels** (1733–1817) was the Austrian cameralist of enlightened absolutism — holder of the newly created Vienna chair of police and cameral sciences (1763), rector of the university (1794, 1796), imperial councillor, abolitionist of torture, and systematizer of Josephine reform.

His three-volume *Grundsätze der Polizey, Handlung und Finanz* (1765–76) was the binding university textbook in Austria until 1848: police as the organization of internal security and welfare, commerce (*Handlung*) as the totality of economic relations, finance as taxation and public debt on market principles.

> Sonnenfels continued Justi's systematization under one principle — general welfare (*allgemeine Wohlfahrt*) — and turned it into a century of Austrian administrative doctrine.
