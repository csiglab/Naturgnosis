# Henry Mintzberg


> 

| **Year** | **Title** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1973 | *The Nature of Managerial Work* | Challenges traditional views of managerial roles and analyzes what managers actually do. |
| 1979 | *Structuring of Organizations* | Explores organizational structures and their implications for management. |
| 1983 | *Power In and Around Organizations* | Examines the role of power in organizations and its influence on decision-making and behavior. |
| 1989 | *Mintzberg on Management: Inside our Strange World of Organizations* | A collection of essays offering insights into the complexities of managing organizations. |
| 1994 | *The Rise and Fall of Strategic Planning* | Critiques traditional strategic planning and proposes alternative approaches to strategy development. |
| 1998 | *Strategy Safari: A Guided Tour Through The Wilds of Strategic Management* | Overview of different schools of thought in strategic management (co-authored with Bruce Ahlstrand and Joseph Lampel). |
| 2004 | *Managers Not MBAs: A Hard Look at the Soft Practice of Managing and Management Development* | Critiques MBA education and advocates for practical management development. |
| 2009 | *Managing* | A comprehensive look at what managing truly entails, based on Mintzberg’s research and observations. |
| 2015 | *Rebalancing Society: Radical Renewal Beyond Left, Right, and Center* | Discusses the need for societal balance and proposes ways to achieve it. |
| 2019 | *Bedtime Stories for Managers: Farewell, Lofty Leadership . . . Welcome, Engaging Management* | A collection of short, thought-provoking stories challenging conventional management wisdom. |
