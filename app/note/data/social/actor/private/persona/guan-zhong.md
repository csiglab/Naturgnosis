# Guan Zhong

> Guan Zhong (管仲; c. 720–645 BC), given name Yiwu (夷吾), was a Chinese philosopher and politician who served as chancellor of the State of Qi under Duke Huan during the Spring and Autumn period. His administrative and economic reforms made Qi the strongest of the feudal states and made Duke Huan the first of the Five Hegemons.

| **Contribution** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrative reorganization** | Divided the capital into 21 districts (six of merchants, fifteen of attendants supplying the army) and organized the countryside into graded village, troop, district, and county units with appointed administrators reporting annually. |
| **Fiscal reform** | Abolished the Zhou well-field system; taxed land according to productivity rather than fixed shares. |
| **State monopoly on salt and iron** | Advised obtaining revenue by monopolizing salt, iron, forest products, and ore rather than raising taxes, since taxation would impoverish the people and breed resentment — "managing the mountain and the sea" (官山海). |
| **Price regulation and coinage** | State granaries bought grain in bounty years and released it in lean years; coinage was unified to encourage internal trade. |
| **Merit over aristocracy** | Shifted administrative responsibility from hereditary aristocrats to professional bureaucrats and developed better methods for selecting governors. |
| **Economic warfare** | The Qi–Hengshan war, in which Qi bought up Hengshan's weapons and grain before invading, is cited as an early form of economic warfare. |
| **Diplomacy** | Through reforms and skilful diplomacy Qi became hegemon; Guan Zhong advised Duke Huan to "summon the wavering with courtesy and cherish the remote with virtuous conduct." |

> Guan Zhong is often identified with the origins of the Legalist school, though Legalist philosophy proper developed centuries later. Confucius, while faulting his extravagance and ritual impropriety, credited him with bringing unity and order to the realm. The *Guanzi* compilation, assembled at the Jixia Academy during the Warring States period, makes use of his name.
