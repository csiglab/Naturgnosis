# Louis De Geer

> Louis De Geer (1587–1652) was a Walloon-Swedish entrepreneur, banker, industrialist, and slave trader, considered the father of Swedish industry for introducing Walloon blast furnaces and for pioneering Sweden's involvement in the Atlantic slave trade.
