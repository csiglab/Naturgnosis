# Al-Fārābī

> Abū Naṣr Muḥammad al-Fārābī (c. 872–950) was a philosopher of the Islamic Golden Age. He wrote on logic, political philosophy, and music, and is best known for *al-Madīna al-Fāḍila* (The Virtuous City); his work transmitted and reconciled Greek philosophy, especially Plato and Aristotle, into the Islamic world and later Latin scholasticism.
