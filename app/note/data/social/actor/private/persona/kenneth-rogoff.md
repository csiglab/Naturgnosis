# Kenneth Rogoff


> 

Here’s a table of some of Kenneth Rogoff's most cited papers:

| **Title** | **Publication Year** | **Journal/Publisher** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **"The Curse of Cash"** | 2016 | Princeton University Press | Discusses the economic and policy implications of cash usage and the potential benefits of reducing cash. |
| **"Debt Relief"** | 2008 | CEPR Discussion Paper | Examines the impact of debt relief on economic outcomes and financial stability. |
| **"This Time Is Different: Eight Centuries of Financial Folly"** | 2009 | Princeton University Press | Analyzes historical financial crises and the common patterns and mistakes associated with them. |
| **"Growth in a Time of Debt"** | 2010 | NBER Working Paper | Investigates the relationship between government debt levels and economic growth. |
| **"A Note on the Recent Crisis"** | 2009 | Economic Policy Review | Provides insights into the causes and implications of the global financial crisis. |
| **"Financial and Economic Crises: What Have We Learned?"** | 2011 | IMF Working Paper | Reviews lessons learned from past financial and economic crises and their implications for policy. |
| **"The Anatomy of a Financial Crisis"** | 2013 | Journal of Economic Perspectives | Analyzes the structure and dynamics of financial crises and their impact on economies. |
| **"Globalization and the Economic Crisis: Policy Responses"** | 2009 | Brookings Institution | Discusses policy responses to the global economic crisis and the role of globalization. |
| **"The International Role of the Dollar"** | 2007 | NBER Working Paper | Examines the role of the U.S. dollar in international finance and the implications for global economics. |
