# Antonio Serra


> The First Economy Development Strategy.
> 

> In the book ‘Breve Trattato,’ Serra analyzes the economies of Naples and Venice to understand the prosperity of Venice and the economic challenges faced by Naples, which is rich in natural resources. His conclusions indicate that Venice has a **diversified economy characterized by activities that benefit from increasing returns to scale**, whereas Naples lacks this diversity; and **decreasing returns**.
> 

> Although **Antonio Serra** did not explicitly mention **increasing returns**, he implied this concept through his discussion of **multiplicity in manufacturing**. His analysis focused on the characteristics of **manufacturing activities** and how they contribute to **economic dynamics**.
