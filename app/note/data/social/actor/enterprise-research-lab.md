# Enterprise Research Lab

> An **Enterprise Research Lab** is the corporate R&D collective spanning market-near to distant inquiry with its own lab typology. Source: Produceologia `docs/Breviarium/an-essay-on-enterprise-research-labs.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Enterprise Research Lab belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules.**

It is not an `Institution` (it is a deliberate actor, not a rule system). Its layer is **Ontic**: labs exist extra-mentally with staff, benches, and budgets. Its facet is technical-economic: the dominant mechanism converts inquiry into firm capability.

### What is this social instance?

> Labs arrayed from market-near development through platform research to distant exploration, sponsored by firms that absorb their outputs — distinct from contract centers (external sponsors) and plants (no method mandate), cf. the Product Development Center.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one generic lab, decomposed at shallow depth — root plus lab-form and sponsor constituents only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a lab form or sponsor.
>
> Identity: Instance Tree Path is the stable identifier of each row; every path is unique.
