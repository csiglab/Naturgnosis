# Product Development Center

> A **Product Development Center** is a deliberately structured collective actor that converts a product idea into a realizable product: it holds both halves of product development — realizing the product and developing the technical methods that make and test it — under one management, between a sponsoring firm that states the need and a manufacturing operation that absorbs the outcome.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Product Development Center belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound Interaction Unit carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices.**

It is not an `Institution` (it is a deliberate actor rather than a stabilized system of rules), not a `Social Field` (it is one actor inside a field rather than the field itself), and not a `Practice` (it hosts practices rather than being one). Its layer is **Ontic**: the center exists extra-mentally, with an address, a staff, a charter, and a budget. Its facet is technical-economic — its dominant causal mechanism is the conversion of development capability into a deliverable offer.

Read also, secondarily, as an `Interaction Unit` in the plain sense: a bounded entity capable of interaction, here nested inside a sponsoring firm and connected to suppliers, universities, and certification bodies. That secondary reading is prose, not a second root; the tree below is typed once.

### What is this social instance?

> A Product Development Center is an organization whose mandate is product development — the work of carrying a product from concept to a validated, deliverable offer.

> **Product Development = Product Realization + Technical Method Development**

The two constituents are distinct, jointly necessary, and routinely unbalanced inside a single organization:

- **Product Realization** fixes *what the product is* and builds the physical or digital realization that satisfies it — requirement clarification, concept generation, feasibility study, prototype fabrication, design validation, pilot run. Its output is a product that exists and meets a stated requirement.
- **Technical Method Development** fixes *how the product is made and judged* and develops the processes, design rules, test methods, and measurement methods the realization depends on. Its output is a method that can be repeated by people who did not write it.

A center strong in realization but weak in method development ships products that cannot be produced twice; a center strong in method development but weak in realization holds excellent process knowledge attached to no product. Holding both under one management is precisely what distinguishes the center from a research institute (method without a product mandate) and from a manufacturing plant (realization without method development).
