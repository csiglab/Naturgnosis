# Firm

> A **Firm** is a bounded, goal-oriented socio-technical collective that mobilizes and coordinates resources, capabilities, and knowledge to produce and exchange value within an environment. Source: Produceologia `docs/Firm/README.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Firm belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules, carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices.**

It is not an `Institution` (it is a deliberate actor rather than a stabilized system of rules) and not a `Practice` (it hosts practices rather than being one). Its layer is **Ontic**: a firm exists extra-mentally, with staff, assets, accounts, and address. Its facet is economic — its dominant causal mechanism is the conversion of capability into exchangeable value.

Read also, secondarily, as an `Interaction Unit` in the plain sense: a bounded entity capable of interaction, nested inside markets and business groups. That secondary reading is prose, not a second root; the tree below is typed once.

### What is this social instance?

> A firm acts through intentional agency (vision, goals, strategy), operational capacities (technical, human, financial, organizational), market interfaces (customers, partners, institutions), reflexive representations (brand, identity, reputation), and evolutionary learning (innovation, adaptation).

Produceologia models it across three analytical layers — microeconomic (production, cost, incentives), institutional (governance, contracts, structure), capabilities (learning, innovation, routines) — and six operating layers: operational, financial, intelligence, coordination, strategic, audit.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one generic Firm, decomposed at shallow depth — root plus direct constituents only, no intermediaries.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a direct constituent structure, strategy, portfolio, practice, or context.
>
> Identity: Instance Tree Path is the stable identifier of each row; every path is unique.
