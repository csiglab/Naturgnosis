# Municipal Government

> A **Municipal Government** is the government of a bounded urban locality — the **Local Government** kind that is constituted to exercise public power over a municipality and the local services attached to it, under the legal order of a higher-tier state.

> **Note:** This note documents the generic municipal kind. It is a specialization of [Local Government](note.html?n=social/actor/public/government.md), itself a kind of [Government](note.html?n=social/actor/public/government.md). Named municipal governments (a city, a capital district, a particular council) are instances, not kinds, and are documented as case studies.

## Formulation

### What is this social instance?

> A municipal government fixes *who governs a locality and how*: the local mandate that warrants rule (election, charter, or statutory grant), the office-holders and bodies that compose it (mayor and council, or a manager under a council), the arrangements through which it decides and acts as one, and the jurisdiction — persons, places, and matters — over which it claims authority within a bounded locality. Canonical instances: a city council with an elected mayor, a municipal corporation under a charter, a commune or municipality governed by a deliberative assembly.

> It governs the local commons: roads, water and sanitation, waste, local planning and land use, licensing, civil registration, local policing, and local welfare services. Its competence is granted by, and remains subordinate to, the higher-tier legal order, though its authority is locally constituted and locally accountable.

### What type of social element does this instance belong to?

**Municipal Government belongs to `Social Compound` as its primary kind** — an organized political collective (`Collective (→ Kind) Political Collective`); see [Collective](note.html?n=social/collective/collective.md). It is typed under [Actor](note.html?n=social/actor/actor.md), the root hub of the actor typology, and specialized organizationally under [Organization](note.html?n=social/actor/organization.md); its operating units are [Public Organization](note.html?n=social/actor/public/public-organization.md) instances, notably the `Municipal Organization` kind.

> Its type path is nested: `Government (→ Kind) Local Government (→ Kind) Municipal Government`. **Local Government** is the sub-type of [Government](note.html?n=social/actor/public/government.md); **Municipal Government** is its urban-locality kind, beside the district and metropolitan variants.

Its layer is **Ontic** where the municipal government exists extra-mentally (office-holders, municipal offices, premises, budgets, service fleets) with **Synontic** aspects (local authority, legitimacy, and jurisdiction exist through shared recognition and statutory warrant).

It is not the `Municipio` (the historical and territorial unit — see the study at [Municipio](note.html?n=history/castilla/municipio.md)), not the `Municipality` (the locality and corporate entity it governs), not a [Mancomunidad](note.html?n=social/normative/institution/institution/mancomunidad.md) (an inter-municipal association), not the `Municipal Organization` (the wielding body — decompose the body where authority is wielded), and not the higher-tier [Government](note.html?n=social/actor/public/government.md) (national or regional) from which its competence is delegated.

### Is the type of this `social element instance` unambiguous?

> Yes, as a compound kind: the mandate-bound, locally constituted governing collective is unambiguous, and its nesting under Local Government → Government is established. The state-actor reading (an `Interaction Unit` wielding delegated sovereign power), the `Public Organization` / `Municipal Organization` reading (the municipal body as the wielding organization), and the individual-office reading (mayor, councillor as `Social Role`s) are secondary aspects of the same instance, not rival roots. They are recorded here as `readable as …` prose, not second trees; the tree below is typed once. A second tree is not grown because the state-actor and organization grammars are covered by existing kinds and would otherwise duplicate the same constituents under two roots.

### Which facet does this element belong to, if applicable?

> Political first (authoritative rule-execution over a locality is the dominant causal mechanism); administrative where it operates through municipal departments and the local civil service; legal-constitutional where its mandate, competencies, and limits are defined; fiscal where local revenue, transfers, and budget autonomy are at issue. See [Facet](note.html?n=social/framework/facet.md).

## QA

### Is a municipal government the same as its municipality?

> No. The municipality is the locality and corporate entity governed; the municipal government is the mandate-bound collective that governs it. The locality persists through changes of council and executive; the government's identity is term-bounded. Decompose the governed entity where membership and territory live; decompose the government where mandate and office-holding live.

### Is a municipal government the same as a local government?

> No — it is a kind of it. **Local Government** is the sub-type of [Government](note.html?n=social/actor/public/government.md) covering the subnational level below regional and national tiers; **Municipal Government** is its urban-locality kind, beside district and metropolitan kinds. A rural district council is local government but not municipal; a metropolitan authority is municipal in scale but not bounded to a single city.

### Where do the municipal body, its staff, and its administration decompose?

> The wielding body decomposes as a [Public Organization](note.html?n=social/actor/public/public-organization.md) — the `Municipal Organization` kind. The administrative architecture through which it operates decomposes as [Public Administration](note.html?n=social/actor/public/administration.md). Municipal staff are the administered workforce, not the government; decompose the office where the mandate lives and the person where the capability lives.

### Where do inter-municipal arrangements decompose?

> As their own social elements, by reference — a [Mancomunidad](note.html?n=social/normative/institution/institution/mancomunidad.md) is an institution associating municipalities; a metropolitan authority is a distinct government body. They associate the municipal government; they are not parts of it. Link them from the Association branch rather than nesting them.
