# Abbasid Caliphate

> A caliphal court coordinating a vizier-led administration, patronage-based expertise, and the
> translation movement that turned Baghdad into a knowledge commons.

## Pattern

Patronage-funded knowledge production alongside bureaucratic delegation at imperial scale: the
court paid for the absorption of Persian, Greek, and Indian learning within two generations.

## Sources

Arabic administrative manuals (al-Jahshiyari), the *Fihrist* of al-Nadim, the translation-movement
corpus, and the archaeology of Samarra.
