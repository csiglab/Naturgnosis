# Song dynasty

> Examination meritocracy in full: a large civil bureaucracy recruited by competitive exams, with
> paper money, market coordination, and a central finance apparatus — rule by examined
> administrators at scale.

## Pattern

The examination state as a working system: recruitment by test, a professionalized civil service,
and an administratively managed economy. Included among the polities worth studying for the
organization of collective action.

## Sources

The *Song huiyao* administrative compilations, the *Songshi*, Wang Anshi's reform documents, and
a large surviving bureaucratic record; supplemented by the period's economic and monetary
documentation.
