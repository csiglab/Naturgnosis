# Han dynasty

> A durable imperial bureaucracy (commandery-and-county plus a trained secretariat), state
> monopolies on salt and iron, and periodic censuses — administration at scale, with the first
> examination-inflected recruitment.

## Pattern

The administrative state stabilized: a routinized central bureaucracy, fiscal monopolies, and
population registers. Confucian classics made the curriculum of office, seeding the later
examination system.

## Sources

The *Shiji* and *Hanshu*; the Yinwan and Juyan administrative documents; Han statutes from
Zhangjiashan; and surviving census and household registers.
