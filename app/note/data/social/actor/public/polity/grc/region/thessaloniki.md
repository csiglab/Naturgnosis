# Thessaloniki

> **Thessaloniki** (Salónica) is the second-largest city of Greece and the capital of the periphery of Central Macedonia — a major northern Aegean port at the head of the Thermaic Gulf. It is a `Region` read as `City`: a spatially and institutionally organized local nexus.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Thessaloniki belongs to the `Region` social element type (Structural category), read as `City`** — a spatially and institutionally organized local nexus, a zone of dense recurrent interaction. Its layer is **Ontic**: the built city, its port, and its population exist extra-mentally. Its facet is social-general, with an economic (port-trade) face.

Readable secondarily as a `Jurisdiction` (the Municipality of Thessaloniki) — prose, not a second root.

### What is this social instance?

> The city of Thessaloniki: founded in 315 BC and named for Thessalonike, it served as the second city of the Byzantine Empire and later as an Ottoman port before becoming — with the 1912 Balkan Wars and the 1917 fire — the modern metropolis of northern Greece (the "symprotevousa", or co-capital). Its municipality held 319,045 people in 2022 within a metropolitan area of about 1,006,112. It couples the port, the Aristotle University, and the industrial and trade hinterland of Macedonia into one local nexus.
