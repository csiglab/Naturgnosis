# Athenian Republic

> Sortition over election, ostracism as an elite-circulation valve, deme organization, and
> liturgies (competitive elite funding of triremes and festivals).

## Pattern

Citizen agency at scale through randomization, rotation, and privately financed public goods.
Randomization constrained faction; liturgies converted elite rivalry into public provision.

## Sources

Inscribed decrees (IG), forensic oratory (Demosthenes and others), Aristotle's *Athenaion
Politeia*, Herodotus, Thucydides, and Xenophon, naval and deme records, and the excavated Pnyx
and agora. Sortition, liturgies, and ostracism are directly attested, not inferred.
