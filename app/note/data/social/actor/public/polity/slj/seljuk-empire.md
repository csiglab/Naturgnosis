# Seljuk Empire

> A steppe-origin conquest state governing largely through Persianate ministerial administration
> — the vizierate and the Nizamiyya madrasas — after the model of Nizam al-Mulk's *Siyasatnama*:
> nomadic military power delegating administration to a settled bureaucracy.

## Pattern

Military custodianship fused to a pre-existing administrative tradition. A case of conquerors
adopting the institutions of the conquered rather than replacing them.

## Sources

Nizam al-Mulk's *Siyasatnama*, the chronicles (Ibn al-Athir, Rashid al-Din), the surviving
madrasa network, and archaeology.
