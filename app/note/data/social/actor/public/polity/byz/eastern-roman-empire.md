# Eastern Roman Empire

> The theme system coupled soldier-farming to hereditary military service, later supplemented by
> pronoia grants: funding an army from agrarian surplus without a collapsing money economy.

## Pattern

Fiscal-military self-sufficiency through land-tenure obligations — the state paying for defence in
land and privilege rather than coin.

## Sources

The *Corpus Juris Civilis* and later legal codes, *De administrando imperio* and *De thematibus*,
lead seals (*bullae*), and the Byzantine administrative treatises.
