# Chola Empire

> An imperial canopy over self-governing villages: the Uttaramerur inscription records written
> electoral rules for the village sabha — lot-casting among qualified householders, with
> disqualification clauses — while the Ainnurruvar merchant guilds governed Indian Ocean trade.

## Pattern

Layered collective agency: centralized imperial coordination above, codified local self-rule and
merchant-trade governance below. Shows a state that delegated real decision rights rather than
merely extracting.

## Sources

Tamil epigraphy (Uttaramerur, Thanjavur), merchant-guild grants (Ainnurruvar), Chola-era temple
records, and Chinese and Arab trade notices. Local institutional detail is unusually rich in
inscriptions.
