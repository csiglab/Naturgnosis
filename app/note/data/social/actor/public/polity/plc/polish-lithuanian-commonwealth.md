# Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth

> Liberum veto and confederated obstruction: what veto-point maximalism does to collective
> agency.

## Pattern

Anti-pattern control. Read alongside Venice: both are republics of the nobility, and the
contrast isolates how much a working collective order depends on limiting the veto. The
Commonwealth's deliberative machinery was captured by a single negative vote.

## Sources

Sejm diaries (*diariusz*), *Volumina Legum*, the Nihil novi and Henrician Articles, and nobles'
instructions. Exceptionally documented for a deliberative failure.
