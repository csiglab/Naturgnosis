# Republic of Genoa

> Public debt governed as a shareholder corporation: the Casa di San Giorgio administered
> colonies and customs on behalf of creditor-shareholders — the rival answer to Venice.

## Pattern

Creditor governance of public functions as an agency pattern: bondholders as the governing body,
with revenue streams pledged directly to the corporation.

## Sources

Genoese notarial registers (one of Europe's largest medieval archives), the Casa di San Giorgio
records, and Genoese cartography.
