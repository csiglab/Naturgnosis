# Ottoman Empire

> The stack in one polity: devshirme (elite circulation by levy-and-schooling), timar
> (military-fiscal land coupling), millet (confessional autonomy), and the waqf complex of
> perpetual endowments.

## Pattern

Loyalty to office over blood; decentralized public goods funded outside the treasury. Shows how a
long-lived empire combined central elite formation with deep institutional pluralism.

## Sources

Ottoman *kanunname* and *tahrir* registers, court (*kadi*) records, *waqfiyyas* (endowment
deeds), and European diplomatic reports. Administrative and endowment data are exceptionally
detailed.
