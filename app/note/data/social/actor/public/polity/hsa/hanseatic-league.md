# Hanseatic League

> Two hundred cities coordinating trade, law, and war through diets with no state, no territory,
> and no sovereign.

## Pattern

Credible commitment without a centre — a league form of collective agency in which shared
commercial law, common Kontore, and the credible threat of embargo substituted for government.
Compare with the Old Swiss Confederation and the Iroquois Confederacy.

## Sources

The *Hanserecesse* (diet records), Kontor archives (Bergen, Bruges, Novgorod, London), and the
accumulated Hanseatic privileges.
