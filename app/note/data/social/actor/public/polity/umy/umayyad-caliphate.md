# Umayyad Caliphate

> A conquest state administering a vast territory through pre-existing provincial bureaucracies
> (Byzantine and Sasanian) with an Arab military elite: an early case of thin rulers over heavy
> administration.

## Pattern

Administrative continuity under new sovereigns; *diwan*-based fiscal organization that left
conquered machinery largely intact while changing who commanded it.

## Sources

Arabic papyri, coinage, the caliphal inscriptions (Dome of the Rock), the chronicles, and the
administrative papyri from Aphrodito in Egypt.
