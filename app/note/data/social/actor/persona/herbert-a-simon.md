# Herbert A. Simon


> 

## Work Space

| **Work** | **Period** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Administrative Behavior** | 1947 | Classic work in public administration and organizational theory. Introduces the concept of **bounded rationality** and decision-making in organizations. Emphasizes that humans are limited in their capacity to process information and make fully rational choices. |
| **Models of Man** | 1957 | A collection of essays elaborating on behavioral science, decision-making, and economics. Explores human rationality, problem-solving, and organizational behavior. |
| **The Sciences of the Artificial** | 1969 (1st ed.), revised 1977 | Foundational book in artificial intelligence, cognitive science, and design theory. Discusses **artificial systems**, complex problem-solving, and the design process. Emphasizes modeling, simulation, and the formalization of decision-making. |
| **Human Problem-Solving** (with Allen Newell) | 1972 | Key work in cognitive psychology and AI. Introduces formal models of problem-solving and heuristic search, connecting human cognition to computer simulations. |
| **Models of Bounded Rationality** (volumes 1 & 2) | 1982, 1986 | Extends his earlier ideas on bounded rationality. Presents formal models of economic behavior and decision-making under constraints. Combines mathematical modeling with psychological insights. |
| **Administrative Behavior, 3rd edition** | 1976 | Updated edition of his seminal 1947 book, including new research on decision-making, organizational theory, and the limitations of rational choice. |
| **Research on Decision-Making and Problem-Solving** (various papers) | 1950s–1980s | Numerous influential papers on heuristics, satisfying, organization theory, and computer simulation of cognitive processes. |
