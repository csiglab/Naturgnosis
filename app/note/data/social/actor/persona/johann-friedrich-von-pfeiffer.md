# Johann Friedrich von Pfeiffer

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e9/Johann_Friedrich_von_Pfeiffer_1784_(cropped).jpg"
     alt="Johann Friedrich von Pfeiffer"
     width="250"
     align="left"
     style="margin: 0 25px 15px 0;">

**Johann Friedrich von Pfeiffer** (1718–1787) was a German economist, cameralist, and public official associated with the development of **Cameralism** in the German-speaking states.

He was particularly concerned with the practical organization of the state economy: **agriculture, manufacturing, trade, population, taxation, public administration, and the productive use of resources**. His writings belong to the broader tradition of German *Polizeiwissenschaft* and Cameralist political economy.

Pfeiffer's work is especially interesting because he treated economic development not simply as a matter of exchange, but as a problem of **organizing productive capabilities**. Agriculture, crafts, industry, commerce, infrastructure, and government administration were interconnected components of the productive strength of a territory.

> His writings therefore provide an important historical perspective on how 18th-century German thinkers conceptualized the **development of productive capacity and the role of the state in cultivating it**.
