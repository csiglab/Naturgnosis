# Johann Friedrich von Pfeiffer


> **Johann Friedrich von Pfeiffer**, an influential German economist and public official in the 18th century, is known for his contributions to economic theory and policy, particularly regarding the relationship between agriculture and trade.
> 

Here’s a table summarizing the main ideas of **Johann Friedrich von Pfeiffer**:

| **Main Idea** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Agricultural Importance** | Emphasized the central role of agriculture in a nation's economy, advocating for policies that enhance agricultural productivity as a basis for economic growth. |
| **Mercantilism** | Supported mercantilist principles, arguing that national wealth is best enhanced through a favorable balance of trade by maximizing exports and minimizing imports. |
| **Economic Policy Recommendations** | Suggested protective tariffs and subsidies to nurture domestic industries and agriculture, fostering self-sufficiency and enhancing the nation's economic position. |
| **Critique of Free Trade** | Acknowledged benefits of free trade but critiqued its potential adverse effects on local economies, including undermining domestic production and fostering dependency. |
| **Social Welfare** | Highlighted the social implications of economic policies, arguing that a prosperous agricultural sector leads to improved living conditions and social stability. |
