# Groupon, Inc.

> **Groupon, Inc.** is an American e-commerce marketplace that connects consumers to local services, goods, and travel through discounted, time-limited offers. It is a `Collective / Organization` — the firm that pioneered the daily-deal model of aggregating consumer demand into merchant offers.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Groupon, Inc. belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type (Agents category)** — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules, carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices. Its layer is **Ontic**: the firm exists extra-mentally, with staff, offices, merchants, and accounts. Its facet is economic — the dominant mechanism is the conversion of aggregated consumer demand into exchangeable value.

Readable secondarily as an `Interaction Unit` nested in markets, and as a `Medium` (a platform channel through which offers flow). Those readings are prose; the tree below is typed once. It is distinct from the `Market` it intermediates and from the `Activity` (local commerce) it coordinates.

### What is this social instance?

> A two-sided marketplace firm: founded in Chicago in 2008 by Andrew Mason, Groupon grew out of The Point and built the daily-deal model — a discounted offer activated only when a minimum number of buyers commit, with the platform taking a commission and paying the merchant the rest. It expanded into goods and travel, went public in 2011, and then contracted as the daily-deal model matured while competition (LivingSocial, Amazon Local) and mobile commerce reshaped the field. It is headquartered in Chicago and listed on Nasdaq.
