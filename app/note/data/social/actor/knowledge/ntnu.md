# Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU)

> **NTNU** is Norway's largest university: forty-three thousand students, eight and a half thousand staff, an eleven-billion-kroner budget, and campuses in Trondheim, Gjøvik, and Ålesund. It took its present form in 1996, when the Norwegian Institute of Technology, the university colleges, the natural history museum, the medical faculty, the academy of fine art, and the conservatory were merged into one institution — a merger that makes it the successor of a technical school founded in 1910 and a learned society founded in 1760.

> In the research system it is the sibling that is *not* an institute: NTNU teaches as well as researches, trains the people who staff the institutes, and hosts one of them on its own campus. Framed per [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**NTNU belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound unit with identity, membership, and rules.**

It is a university: a legal person, a senate and rectorate, faculties and departments, an academic staff, and forty-three thousand student members who are also its constituents. It is a larger and more heterogeneous unit than any of the institutes around it, which is why the branches below carry teaching, examination, and the student relation alongside research and third-party activity.

The `Institution` reading — the degree-awarding corporation, its standing in the state, and the academic norms it polices — is declared as a secondary reading and decomposed below as `Legitimacy`, `Norm / Regulation`, and `Right / Obligation`.

### What is this social instance?

> A state university: academic staff, technical and administrative staff, and forty-three thousand students across faculties in Trondheim, Gjøvik, and Ålesund, teaching seven hundred programmes, running research groups of international standing, and educating the engineers, teachers, and researchers who staff the rest of the Norwegian system.

**Layer.** **Ontic** — the university, its staff, its students, its campuses, and its degree power exist extra-mentally. Its curricula and research outputs are Noetic instruments; the expectation that a country can train the professionals it needs is the shared anticipation it serves.

**Facet.** **Ideational**, by dominant mechanism, with a normative face: NTNU is reorganized by the production and accreditation of knowledge, and it is *only* partly a market-facing actor. Its research is a function, not its purpose; the degree and the taught formation are the core.

**Boundary.** The university as a body: warrants, faculties, roles, practices, rules, student rights and academic duties, relations, resources, run, and present state. The science it teaches is epistemica; the firms it works with are social and production.

Lineage: a learned society founded in Trondheim in 1760 and a technical school raised in 1910 → a 1968 university and a 1996 merger of six higher-education and research institutions in the city → incorporation of the regional colleges, the last of them in 2016, which made NTNU Norway's largest single university → a present position where its research groups, its medical and neuroscience work, and its engineering education set the standard the national system measures itself against.
