# Norwegian Institute of Bioeconomy Research (NIBIO)

> **NIBIO** is Norway's national research institute for the bioeconomy: it produces the knowledge, the administrative support, and the sectoral competence that the food, forest, and other biobased value chains run on. It was created on 1 July 2015 by merging three institutes — Bioforsk, the Norwegian Forest and Landscape Institute, and the Norwegian Institute for Agricultural Economics Research — and inherits working institutions with roots back to 1889.

> It is the instrument through which Norwegian agricultural research became one body rather than five. Before the merger, plant genetics, forest, agricultural economics, and applied agricultural research sat in separate institutes with separate mandates and separate relationships to the state. After it, they answer to one board, under one ministry, from one site at Ås. The institute defines the bioeconomy it serves in its own words: an economy that exploits biological resources from land and sea, and waste, as inputs for food, energy, and other goods and services.

> Framed per [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md). The science it produces is decomposed as [Agricultural Science](note.html?n=epistemica/agricultural-science.md) and [Earth Science](note.html?n=epistemica/earth-science.md); the instruments it runs are technique; the farming economy it serves is production.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**NIBIO belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound unit with identity, membership, and rules.**

It is a body: a staff, a board, five divisions, fifteen locations, a mandate, a budget line. What makes it an organization rather than merely an institution is that the organization is the thing that does the work — the divisions are the members, the board is the rule-making body, and the mandate is the warrant they act under.

The `Institution` reading is nonetheless real and is declared rather than hidden: NIBIO is a *forvaltningsorgan med særskilt fullmakt og eget styre* — an administrative agency with special authorization and its own supervisory board — and the stabilized configuration of roles and rules that makes it answerable to a ministry is exactly the Tabular view's `Institution` case, the same case as a central bank. Per the multi-root rule that reading takes its own root in its own decomposition; here the statutory configuration is decomposed *beneath* the organization, as `Legitimacy`, `Norm / Regulation`, and `Right / Obligation`. No row below carries two types.

### What is this social instance?

> A state research institute: researchers, technicians, and advisory staff in five divisions, working on food and plant production, environment, climate, mapping and geodata, land resources, genetic resources, forestry, and the economics of the firms and communities in those sectors — delivering research, administrative support to the ministry, and nationally responsible competence in its fields, for preparedness as much as for policy.

**Layer.** **Ontic** — the institute, its staff, its board, its divisions, its sites, its laboratories, and its legal personality exist extra-mentally and would survive any observer. Its datasets and mapping products are Noetic instruments; the warrant that the food chain's future is knowable in advance is a shared anticipation, and the bioeconomy is the image the institute carries of what it is for.

**Facet.** **Ideational**, assigned by dominant causal mechanism rather than by location: NIBIO is reorganized by the production and circulation of knowledge, and its outputs are warranted by validation rather than by throughput. Its laboratories and field stations are instruments it operates, not the mechanism that reorganizes it.

**Boundary.** This note covers the organization: its mandate and warrants, its divisions, its roles, its practices, its rules, its obligations, its relations, its resources, its run, and its present shape. It does not decompose the science (epistemica), the instruments (technique), or the farmers and firms in the value chains it serves (social and production). A division is decomposed to the level at which it carries its own mandate; a project is an exemplar only where it evidences a rule.

Lineage: scattered agricultural experiment stations and forestry research under a 1889 statute → post-war institutes of applied agricultural research, forest research, agricultural economics, and plant breeding, each with its own directorate → the 2014 decision to merge five bodies, announced in June of that year → establishment on 1 July 2015 with Bioforsk, Skog og landskap, and the agricultural economics institute inside one board → consolidation of five divisions and the build-out of the national data services (Kilden, VIPS, Agromet.no) as the institute's institutional form of long-term monitoring.
