# Mihajlo Pupin Institute (IMP)

> The **Mihajlo Pupin Institute** is Serbia's automation and computing house: founded in Belgrade in 1959 and named for the physicist and inventor Mihajlo Pupin, part of the University of Belgrade, it built the first Serbian digital computers (the CER family from 1960, then the TIM line), wired Yugoslav industry with process control and telecommunications, did early humanoid-robotics work, and reorganized into companies under an institute holding — robotics, AI, and smart grids today.

> In the research system it is the sibling that builds rather than splits atoms: Pupin automates and computes while VINCA handles the nuclear and physical sciences, the University of Belgrade trains the engineers both employ, and the Science and Innovation Funds now finance the transfer of institute work into firms. Framed per [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md).

### What is this social instance?

> A state research institute: scientists and engineers in Belgrade working automation, computer science, telecommunications, and robotics — from the CER computers of the 1960s through process control for Yugoslav industry to present robotics, AI, and smart-grid programs — organized as an institute holding with operating companies.

**Layer.** **Ontic** — the institute, its staff, its laboratories, and its deployed systems exist extra-mentally. Its prototypes and control systems are technical instruments; the expectation that domestic engineering can automate domestic industry is the shared anticipation it serves.

**Facet.** **Ideational**, by dominant mechanism, with an economic face: the institute is organized by the production of applicable knowledge, and it is substantially a market-facing actor through its companies. Its research is a function, and its transfer into operation is the purpose.

**Boundary.** The institute as a body: warrants, laboratories, roles, practices, rules, rights and obligations, relations, resources, run, and present state. The physics it applies is epistemica; the firms it equips are social and production.

Lineage: a 1959 founding in Belgrade named for Mihajlo Pupin, as a member institute of the University of Belgrade → the CER computer family from 1960, the TIM line, and the automation of Yugoslav industry → early humanoid-robotics work and the present robotics, AI, and smart-grid programs → a holding of operating companies (IMP-Automatika, IMP-Computer Systems, IMP-Telecommunications, and others) carrying the lineage into industry.
