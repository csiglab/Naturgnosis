# Research Activity

> **Research Activity** is the coarse bundle of granular social actions through which agents produce warranted knowledge — the research-facet subtype of `Activity`: intelligence, inquiry, experimentation, analysis, validation, dissemination, and transfer. It hosts the technical activities that instrument it without being one.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Research Activity belongs to the `Activity` social element type (Action category)** — a coarse bundle of granular research actions performed as one situated doing. It is the inquiry core of the [Science](note.html?n=social/actor/action/activity/research/science/science.md) activity family, which scopes the evaluation, communication, and advice surrounding primary inquiry. Its layer is **Ontic**: the doing exists extra-mentally. Its facet is research (scientific).

Readable secondarily as an epistemic pursuit (its product is warranted belief, judged by validation standards); that reading is prose here, not a second root — the epistemic decomposition lives in Epistemic Space and is cross-linked, per the derived-view rule. It is distinct from the actor (the research unit that performs it), the output (the artifact or finding), and its technical instruments (which belong to the technical decomposition).

### What is this social instance?

> The research doing-family that moves inquiry from a question to a warranted result: intelligence and scanning, framing, inquiry proper (basic, applied, developmental), experimentation and observation, analysis and modeling, validation against evidence, dissemination, and transfer into use — the inquiry core beneath the broader [Science](note.html?n=social/actor/action/activity/research/science/science.md) family. Each is a bundle of granular research actions; recurrence (a program repeated over cycles) is typed as dynamics, not as the activity itself. Its technical instruments — apparatus, protocols, computation — are worked in [Technical Activity](note.html?n=social/actor/action/activity/research/technical/technical-activity.md), which sits inside this family as instrumentation, not as a separate root.
