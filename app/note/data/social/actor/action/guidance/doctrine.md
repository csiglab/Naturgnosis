# Doctrine

> **Doctrine** is the authoritative body of guiding tenets that orients action in a domain — the guidance coordinator that a collective carries as its shared teaching, binding strategy and policy to an identity rather than to a passing decision.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Doctrine belongs to the `Doctrine` social element type (Action category, `Action Guidance` branch, Cognitive layer)** — an ideational body of tenets carried by a collective as its teaching. Unlike its Normative siblings, a doctrine binds through shared conviction rather than codified constraint; it is distinct from `Belief` (an accepted proposition about reality) and from `Ideology` (a system of beliefs and values), which it resembles but exceeds by authoritatively orienting action.

### What is this social instance?

> The teaching a collective treats as authoritative: tenets that tell its members how to read their domain and what to do in it. Doctrines persist across decisions — strategies change, policies lapse, rules amend, but the doctrine remains the identity against which the next selection is judged.
