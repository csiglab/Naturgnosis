# Initiative

> **Initiative** is the bounded undertaking launched to pursue a new objective — the organization form that charters a fresh doing outside routine: a sponsor committing, a team gathering, and milestones judging whether the new objective is met.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Initiative belongs to the `Initiative` social element type (Action category, `Action Organization` branch, Ontic layer)** — a bounded undertaking existing extra-mentally as chartered work with a sponsor and a team. It is distinct from `Project` (a time-bounded effort it may become), from `Policy` (the course it may execute), and from `Program` (the set it may join).

### What is this social instance?

> The launched doing: a sponsor committing standing and means, a charter constituting the undertaking, an objective fixing the new pursuit, a team performing it, and milestones judging it. Initiatives decompose into their kinds and their anatomy; the launch is the instance, the novelty the form.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one organization family, decomposed at middle depth — kinds and anatomy only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names an initiative kind or an anatomy element.
