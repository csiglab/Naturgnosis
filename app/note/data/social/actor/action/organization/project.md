# Project

> **Project** is the time-bounded organized effort toward a defined outcome — the organization form that charters a team, bounds its scope, schedules its work, and dissolves it on delivery.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Project belongs to the `Project` social element type (Action category, `Action Organization` branch, Ontic layer)** — an organized effort existing extra-mentally as chartered work with a team, a budget, and a deadline. It is distinct from `Program` (the set containing it), from `Plan` (the arrangement sequencing it), and from `Activity` (a coarse bundle of actions without its charter and bound).

### What is this social instance?

> The bounded effort: a charter constituting it, a scope bounding it, work broken down and scheduled, a budget funding it, a team performing it, deliverables marking it done, and risks threatening it. Projects decompose into their kinds and their anatomy; the effort is the instance, the bound the form.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one organization family, decomposed at middle depth — kinds and anatomy only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a project kind or an anatomy element.
