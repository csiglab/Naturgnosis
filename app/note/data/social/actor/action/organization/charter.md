# Charter

> **Charter** is the founding instrument constituting an organized effort and its mandate — the organization form that brings a doing into being: purpose declared, scope bounded, authority conferred, membership fixed, deliverables named, and constraints stated.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Charter belongs to the `Charter` social element type (Action category, `Action Organization` branch, Synontic layer)** — a founding instrument existing through the shared recognition of the authority it confers. It is distinct from `Legitimacy` (the warrant it may carry), from `Policy` (the mandate it may execute), and from `Institution` (the stabilized configuration it may found).

### What is this social instance?

> The constituting act: an authority declares the effort, bounds what it may touch, confers the powers it needs, names who belongs, fixes what it must deliver, and states what binds it. Charters decompose into their kinds and their anatomy; the instrument is the instance, the constitution the form.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one organization family, decomposed at middle depth — kinds and anatomy only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a charter kind or an anatomy element.
