# Chief Executive's Policy Unit (CePU)

> The **Chief Executive's Policy Unit (CePU)** — the Chief Executive's Policy Unit of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region — is the institution advising the Chief Executive on strategic and long-term policy, and coordinating the government's policy-research and advisory capacity. It is a Hong Kong SAR government body, not a generic type.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**CePU belongs to the `Public Organization` social element type (Government Organization → Public Agency), an instance of `Collective / Organization`** — an organization wielding delegated public authority under law. Its layer is **Ontic**: it exists extra-mentally with an office, a head, staff, and a budget. Its facet is **political** — its dominant causal mechanism is authoritative agenda-setting and coordination for the executive.

It is not a `Policy` (the course it helps to shape), not an `Institution` in the abstract sense (though it hosts governance arrangements — the Council of Advisers and the Expert Group), and not an `Activity` (it hosts research and coordination activities).

### What is this social instance?

> A Hong Kong SAR executive-branch body, established on 1 July 2022 to strengthen the government's policy research and advisory capability. Headed by the Head of the Chief Executive's Policy Unit, it provides in-depth policy research and analysis, advises the Chief Executive on strategic and long-term issues, and coordinates cross-bureau policy formulation. It oversees the Chief Executive's Council of Advisers, the Chief Executive's Policy Unit Expert Group, the Policy Research Funding Schemes, and project coordination. Its layer is **Ontic**; its facet is political.
