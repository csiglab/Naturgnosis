# China

## Industrial and Technology Policy Thinking

> The People's Republic of China's path from a poor agrarian economy to an industrial
> and technological power was argued out, decade by decade, as a series of concrete
> **development problems** paired with **policy ideas** — each carried by identifiable
> intellectual and policy **promoters**. From the 1950s drive for concentrated
> investment, through self-reliance and the 1956 science plan, the reform-era opening
> (joint ventures, "market for technology", selective import substitution), to the
> 2000s turn toward indigenous innovation, the table below maps problem → idea →
> promoter across industrial and science-technology policy.

> China Ideas Ecology cannot be understand as a master plan;  or something;  or some simple linear lineage;  one can use the principle of marxist production; technology  + latterpractical markets ideas; as the guiding mechanisms that constraint the ideas space.

> There was not a grand idea man; many people contritute - in incredible creative ways (not to be send to 'educate yourself, etc').

| **Development problem** | **Policy idea** | **Principal intellectual / policy promoters** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insufficient industrial capital | **Concentrated state investment** | **Chen Yun, Li Fuchun, Bo Yibo, Li Xiannian** |
| Excessive dependence on foreign technology | **Self-reliance (自力更生)** | **Mao Zedong, Zhou Enlai, Nie Rongzhen** |
| Technological backwardness | **Selective technological catch-up** | **Zhou Enlai, Nie Rongzhen, Qian Xuesen**, 1956 S&T planners |
| Cannot develop every technology | **Strategic priority selection** | **Zhou Enlai, Nie Rongzhen, Qian Xuesen** |
| Science disconnected from economic/industrial needs | **"Tasks lead disciplines" (任务带学科)** | **Zhou Enlai**, 1956 S&T planners |
| Need to build technological capability from imported technology | **Technology assimilation / absorption** | **Zhou Enlai, Chen Yun, Nie Rongzhen**; later **Deng Xiaoping, Fang Yi** |
| Imported technology becomes obsolete | **Study → use → modify → innovate (一学、二用、三改、四创)** | **Zhou Enlai**; later **Deng Xiaoping** |
| Scarce capital + need for industrial deepening | **Selective import substitution** | **Chen Yun, Li Fuchun, Bo Yibo, Xue Muqiao** |
| Need to construct a complete industrial system | **Strategic industrial development / sectoral prioritization** | **Chen Yun, Li Fuchun, Bo Yibo, Xue Muqiao** |
| Rigid enterprise incentives | **Economic accounting (经济核算)** | **Sun Yefang, Xue Muqiao, Gu Zhun** |
| Enterprises lack economic responsibility | **Enterprise autonomy / responsibility** | **Sun Yefang, Xue Muqiao**; later **Zhao Ziyang** |
| Prices do not reflect economic scarcity | **Economic calculation through prices** | **Sun Yefang, Xue Muqiao** |
| Planning produces excessive administrative control | **Combination of planning and market mechanisms** | **Sun Yefang, Xue Muqiao**; later **Chen Yun, Zhao Ziyang** |
| Domestic R&D disconnected from production | **Technology transfer / commercialization of research** | **Fang Yi, Song Jian**, later S&T-system reformers |
| Foreign technology is available but domestic firms lack capability to use it | **Technology digestion and absorption** | **Zhou Enlai, Nie Rongzhen, Deng Xiaoping, Fang Yi** |
| China lacks advanced technology and capital | **Foreign technology import** | **Zhou Enlai, Chen Yun, Deng Xiaoping, Gu Mu** |
| Need foreign capital + technology | **Joint venture (中外合资)** | **Gu Mu, Li Lanqing, Deng Xiaoping** |
| China has a large market but lacks advanced technology | **"Market for technology" (以市场换技术)** | **Deng Xiaoping, Gu Mu, Ye Jianying, Li Lanqing**; later **Zhao Ziyang** |
| Foreign firms may enter China without transferring core technology | **Technology-transfer requirements / conditional market access** | **Gu Mu, Fang Yi**, reform-era S&T/economic policymakers |
| Imported technology does not automatically generate domestic innovation | **Introduce → digest → absorb → re-innovate** | **Zhou Enlai → Deng Xiaoping → Fang Yi → Xu Guanhua** |
| Research institutes have weak links with firms | **Technology market / commercialization of S&T** | **Fang Yi, Song Jian**, later S&T reformers |
| Domestic firms lack high-technology capabilities | **High-technology development programs** | **Wang Daheng, Wang Ganchang, Yang Jiachi**, 863-program scientific community |
| Basic research is underdeveloped | **Strategic basic-research programs** | **Zhu Lilan, Xu Guanhua**, 973-policy community |
| Technology catch-up through imitation reaches limits | **Indigenous innovation (自主创新)** | **Xu Guanhua, Wang Daheng**, later **Liu Yanhua** and the 2000s S&T-policy community |
| Foreign technology must become domestically controllable | **Integrated innovation + re-innovation + absorption** | **Xu Guanhua, Liu Yanhua**, MLP-policy community |
| Private firms underinvest in strategic technology | **State R&D finance** | **Xu Guanhua, Liu Yanhua**, 2000s innovation-policy community |
| Firms lack incentives to develop domestic technology | **Tax incentives for R&D / indigenous innovation** | **Xu Guanhua** and MLP-policy community |
| Domestic technologies lack a guaranteed market | **Government procurement for indigenous innovation** | **Xu Guanhua, Liu Yanhua**, MLP-policy community |
| Domestic firms need technological coordination and market power | **Technical standards as industrial policy** | **Xu Guanhua, Liu Yanhua**, later ICT/standardization-policy community |
| Strategic technologies require coordinated national effort | **Mission-oriented S&T programs / megaprojects** | **Qian Xuesen, Nie Rongzhen**; later **Xu Guanhua, Liu Yanhua** |
| Need to move from technology acquisition to technological sovereignty | **Indigenous innovation + strategic technological capability** | **Xu Guanhua, Wang Daheng, Liu Yanhua**, 2000s S&T-policy community |
