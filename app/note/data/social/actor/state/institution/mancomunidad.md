# Mancomunidad

> A **Mancomunidad** is an association of municipalities under Spanish local-regime law — a voluntary public-law entity with its own legal personality, created by two or more municipalities to provide services or pursue purposes they share. It is an `Institution`: a stabilized configuration of roles and rules.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Mancomunidad belongs to the `Institution` social element type (Normative category)** — a stabilized configuration of roles and rules. Its layer is **Synontic**: the mancomunidad exists through the recognized legal form and the constitutive agreement of its member municipalities, not merely as a body. Its facet is political-legal: the dominant mechanism is the pooling of municipal competence into a common authority.

It is distinct from the `Organization` it constitutes (the mancomunidad as a working body with staff), from a single municipality, and from a `Comarca` (a territorial division that need not be a voluntary association). Readable secondarily as a `Collective / Organization` (the joint entity) — prose, cross-linked, not a second root.

### What is this social instance?

> A Spanish local entity: two or more municipalities may associate voluntarily into a mancomunidad, endowed with legal personality, to jointly provide services or execute works of common interest — water supply, waste treatment, environmental protection, firefighting, social services, or tourism promotion. It may be constituted for an indefinite term or for a fixed time and for one or more concrete activities, and it is governed by its own statutes under local-regime law (Ley 7/1985, *Ley reguladora de las Bases del Régimen Local*). The form is widespread across Spain, where the small size of most municipalities makes cooperation necessary.
