# Ideational

> Shared ideas, worldviews, ideologies, and cognitive schemas that influence perception, meaning-making, and social goals.

> Morality appears here only insofar as shared moral schemas, norms, and traditions are transmitted, recognized, negotiated, and revised. The normative machinery — justification, duty, imputability, judgment, and repair — belongs to [Philosophia Moralis et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-moralis-et-operis.md).

> The shared moral schemas named here are elaborated in [Morality](note.html?n=social/synontic/ideational/morality/morality.md).
