# Problem

> This note documents `Problem` as a social element: the intentional structure through which an agency apprehends a gap between conditions and a norm, formulates it, and takes it as the object of commitment. Problematization forms the problem; intention holds it; a goal specifies what closing it would look like.

> A **problem** is the normative gap between the current state of affairs and a desired
> state, as apprehended by an agent capable of acting on it. This is the general element;
> the policy-actionable specialization — a problem taken up as warranting a collective
> course under authority — lives at [Problem](note.html?n=social/actor/state/problem/problem.md).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Problem belongs to the `Problem / Problematization` social element type (Cognitive category, `Intention` branch of the agentic spine)** — the apprehended gap between conditions and a norm, and the process of forming it. It sits under `Interaction Unit -> Agency -> Intention`, alongside `Deliberation`, `Decision / Choice`, `Goal`, and `Objective / Target`.

It is distinct from `Goal` (the desired state taken as the intention's object, not the gap itself), from `Policy` (the authorized course answering the problem), and from [The Collective Action Problem](note.html?n=general/collective-action-problem.md) (the coordination failure that keeps recognized problems unanswered).

### What is this social instance?

> A problem has three moments, all held inside one intention: conditions are apprehended (problematization), the gap between conditions and a norm is formulated (the problem proper), and the gap is taken as a commitment's object (intention toward a goal). Without an agency that apprehends, there are conditions but no problem; without a norm, there is dissatisfaction but no gap; without a commitment, there is grievance or analysis but no intention.

> Problems are individual or joint: one unit may apprehend a gap alone (`Individual Agency`), or several units may jointly commit to a shared formulation (`Joint Agency`) — a bargaining pair naming the wage gap together, co-signing ministers declaring a deficit. The joint formulation is irreducibly plural: it cannot be recovered from the parties' separate apprehensions.

### When does a problem become policy-actionable?

> Through problematization to agenda to mandate: the gap is formulated and evidenced, the formulation wins collective attention (agenda), an authority adopts it as its course (mandate), and instruments are chosen and tasked. That chain is documented at [Problem](note.html?n=social/actor/state/problem/problem.md); the [Dimension](note.html?n=social/actor/state/problem/dimension/domain.md) index scopes in which domain of social reality it sits.
