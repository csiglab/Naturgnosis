# Stock Market

> The **Stock Market** is the equity-securities market coordinating issuance and exchange. Source: Produceologia `docs/Finance/Capital/Stock.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Stock Market belongs to the `Synontic Element` social element type — a recognition-constituted coordinator persisting through shared interpretation.**

Readable secondarily as a `Social Compound` (market-as-ensemble of listed firms, venues, and rules); that reading is prose here, not a second tree. Its layer is **Synontic**: listings, prices, and clearing exist through recursive shared recognition. Its facet is economic.

### What is this social instance?

> Firms list shares, buyers and sellers meet in trading sessions, and clearing houses settle claims — the coordinator reading of a market whose ensemble reading would add member firms and venues.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one market-as-coordinator, decomposed at shallow depth — root plus listing, session, and settlement constituents only.
