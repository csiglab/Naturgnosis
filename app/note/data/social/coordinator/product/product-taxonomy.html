Naturgnosis Product Taxonomy

The Naturgnosis Product Taxonomy is the custom ranking Product Category → Product Subcategory → Product Class → Product, HS-inspired but not HS-mapped — populated only with the products present in Social Space. List and treemap views; starts collapsed to the first level — click a node to open the next level. Product detail shows the complete HS code quoted from its Social Space node (metadata.hsCode) with a link into the Social explorer. Source: Product note.

Data: taxonomy.json — Naturgnosis Product Taxonomy v1 (17 Social Space products). HS codes are quotations from Social Space nodes, not WCO assignments — not customs or tariff advice.
