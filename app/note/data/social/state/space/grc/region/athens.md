# Athens

> **Athens** is the capital and largest city of Greece and the principal centre of its economic, cultural, and political life. It is a `Region` read as `City`: a spatially and institutionally organized local nexus whose metropolitan area — the Attica basin, with the port of Piraeus — concentrates roughly a third of the country's population.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Athens belongs to the `Region` social element type (Structural category), read as `City`** — a spatially and institutionally organized local nexus, a zone of dense recurrent interaction. Its layer is **Ontic**: the built city, its population, and its institutions exist extra-mentally. Its facet is social-general, with a political face: the dominant mechanism is the dense co-location that makes a local nexus of interaction.

Readable secondarily as a `Jurisdiction` (the Municipality of Athens as a bounded domain of authority) and as a `Social Field` (the Attic urban field). Those readings are prose, not second roots. It is distinct from the surrounding `Region` of Attica and from the nation-state it belongs to.

### What is this social instance?

> The city of Athens: capital of modern Greece since 1834, the country's largest municipality (643,452 in 2021) within a metropolitan area of about 3.6 million, and the seat of the Parliament, the ministries, the universities, and the country's financial and cultural institutions. Its local nexus couples a dense urban core, the port of Piraeus, and the Attica basin into one labour, housing, and transport market. Lineage: the classical polis and its acropolis → the Roman and Byzantine town → the Ottoman provincial city → the capital of the new Greek state → the postwar metropolitan agglomeration.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one city, decomposed at shallow depth — the local nexus, its municipal jurisdiction, its port infrastructure, and its population property. The wider Attica region and the national state are cross-links, not members.
