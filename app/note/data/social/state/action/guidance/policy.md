# Policy

> **Policy** is the deliberate course of action an authority adopts to orient subsequent decisions — the normative guidance coordinator that converts a `Strategy`'s ends-and-means selection into an authorized mandate, within which `Rule`s bind conduct and `Plan`s arrange the doing.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Policy belongs to the `Policy` social element type (Action category, `Action Guidance` branch, Normative layer)** — a deliberate course of action adopted by an authority, guiding subsequent decisions. Guidance orients action; organization arranges it.

It is distinct from `Strategy` (the ends/means selection over a horizon), from `Rule` (a specific codified constraint prescribing or prohibiting conduct), from `Plan` (a concrete arrangement of future actions specifying ends, means, and sequence), and from `Program` (a coordinated set of projects under one objective).

### What is this social instance?

> The authorized course: an authority declares where collective doing shall head, and the declaration binds what follows — instruments are chosen, agencies tasked, rules laid down, resources committed, and results reviewed against the mandate. Domains run from economic to foreign policy; every domain decomposes into its policies, and every policy into mandate, instrument, goal, target, agency, rule, and review.
