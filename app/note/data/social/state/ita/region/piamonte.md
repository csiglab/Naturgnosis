# Piamonte

> **Piamonte** (Piedmont) is one of the twenty regions of Italy, occupying the northwest of the country with its capital at Turin. It is a `Region`: a spatially bounded zone of dense recurrent interaction, delimited by the Alpine arc and one of Italy's largest exporting economies.

## Formulation

**Piamonte belongs to the `Region` social element type (Structural category)** — a spatially bounded zone of dense recurrent interaction. Readable secondarily through its provincial articulation (its provinces and metropolitan city) — prose, not a second root.

### What is this social instance?

> The region of Piedmont: 25,402 km², one of the twenty regions of the Italian Republic, in the northwest. It borders the Aosta Valley and Switzerland to the north, Lombardy to the east, Emilia-Romagna to the southeast, Liguria to the south, and France to the west, and forms part of the Alps–Mediterranean Euroregion. Its capital and largest city is Turin; its industrial base — the Turin automobile complex (Fiat, now Stellantis) — has made it one of Italy's leading exporting regions, with a gross domestic product of about €130 billion.
