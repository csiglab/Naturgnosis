# History of the Chinese Economic Policy Toolbox: Ideas and Techniques

> The **Chinese Economic Policy Toolbox** is the repertoire of ideas and techniques through which Chinese economic policy has been conceived and executed across reform eras: plans, pilots, zones, state-capital instruments, and macro controls. This note will trace its history — which ideas governed which instruments, and which techniques carried them into operation.
