# South Korea Five-Year Plan

> The South Korea Five-Year Plan is the strategic planning instrument by which the Republic of Korea arranged its developmental take-off: the presidency directed, the Economic Planning Board drafted, budgeted, and coordinated, the National Assembly ratified, and ministries and exporting firms executed under export targeting — binding export and investment ends, policy-loan allocation, and monthly review composed into one five-year arrangement, renewed from 1962 until the cycle closed with the seventh round in 1996.

> It is an instrument rather than a document. No single text governed; the arrangement existed only as the composed sequence of directives, targets, credit allocation, execution, and review, each round inheriting the apparatus of the last. What persisted across rounds was the developmental machinery, not the targets.

> This note decomposes the instrument, not the economics, per the guide at [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md). The targets' substance — export ratios, heavy-chemical shares, liberalization measures — belongs to the policy notes; the growth models warranting them belong to the epistemic decomposition.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**The South Korea Five-Year Plan belongs to the `Strategic Planning Instrument` social element type (Action category, `Action Guidance` branch via `Framework → Planning Framework`, Normative layer)** — a state-scale instrument binding commissions, cycles, and targets into one standing arrangement. It is the developmental form of the instrument: targets backed by policy credit and export discipline rather than by command quotas.

```text
Strategic Planning Instrument
└── South Korea Five-Year Plan (developmental arrangement machinery, 1962–1996)
```

The instrument is also readable as a `Policy` — each round authorized courses that oriented every subsequent decision in the economy. That reading is kept as prose: authorization is what the arrangement carried, not what it is. No row here carries two types.

### What is this social instance?

> The South Korea Five-Year Plan is a strategic planning instrument: a five-year composition of binding export and investment ends and indicative growth expectations, drafted and budgeted by the planning board, directed by the presidency, ratified by the legislature, executed through ministries and exporting firms, and judged at monthly review — the machinery by which one polity arranged its rise from aid dependence to industrial power in a single generation.

**Layer.** The apparatus is **Ontic** — boards, banks, targets, and loans exist extra-mentally. The confidence the cycle moved is a **Noetic** instrument. The arrangement is *bound* by a **Synontic** stratum carried by its anticipation: the shared expectation that the next five years are already composed and exportable, which coordinated bureaucrats and firms toward the composed future before any credit decision applied.

**Facet.** Assigned by dominant causal mechanism: the cycle's work is reorganized by the *developmental-administrative* mechanism of composed targets allocated through policy credit and export discipline, which is what makes the arrangement plan-guided rather than market-coordinated or command-allocated.

**Boundary.** This note covers the instrument: mandate, board apparatus, ratification, targets, ministerial and firm execution, review, and the seven composed rounds. It does not decompose the targets' economic substance or the models warranting them; those are referenced, not expanded.

Lineage: the 1961 coup and the Economic Planning Board's founding → the First Plan (1962–1966) → the Heavy-Chemical Industry drive (1973) → the 1979 assassination and stabilization → 1980s liberalization opening → democratization (1987) → the segyehwa globalization bid → the Seventh Plan (1992–1996), past which the 1997 crisis closes the cycle.
