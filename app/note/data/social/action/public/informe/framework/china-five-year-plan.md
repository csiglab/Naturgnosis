# China Five-Year Plan

> The China Five-Year Plan is the standing strategic planning instrument by which the People's Republic of China arranges its economic and social development: the Communist Party Center proposes, the National People's Congress ratifies, the National Development and Reform Commission drafts and tracks, and provinces mirror — binding targets, indicative expectations, and mid-course reviews composed into one five-year arrangement, renewed without interruption since 1953.

> It is an instrument rather than a document. No single text governs; the arrangement exists only as the composed sequence of proposal, ratification, execution, and review, each round inheriting the apparatus of the last. What persists across rounds is the machinery, not the mandates.

> This note decomposes the instrument, not the economics, per the guide at [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md). The targets' substance — growth rates, industrial policies, development goals — belongs to the policy notes; the models warranting them belong to the epistemic decomposition.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**The China Five-Year Plan belongs to the `Strategic Planning Instrument` social element type (Action category, `Action Guidance` branch via `Framework → Planning Framework`, Normative layer)** — a state-scale instrument binding commissions, cycles, and targets into one standing arrangement. It is the five-year form of the instrument: each round is a `Plan` composing ends, means, and sequence, while the instrument itself is the machinery that renews the composition.

```text
Strategic Planning Instrument
└── China Five-Year Plan (development arrangement machinery, 1953–present)
```

The instrument is also readable as a `Policy` — each round authorizes courses that orient subsequent decisions, and the cycle's signaling effect moves expectations before any target binds. That reading is kept as prose: authorization is what the arrangement carries, not what it is. No row here carries two types.

### What is this social instance?

> The China Five-Year Plan is a strategic planning instrument: a five-year composition of binding targets and indicative expectations, drafted by the planning apparatus, proposed by the Party Center, ratified by the legislature, executed through mirrored provincial plans, and judged at mid-term review — the machinery by which one polity has arranged its development without interruption for over seventy years.

**Layer.** The apparatus is **Ontic** — commissions, congresses, documents, and targets exist extra-mentally. The expectations the cycle moves are **Noetic** instruments. The arrangement is *bound* by a **Synontic** stratum carried by its anticipation: the shared expectation that the next five years are already composed, which coordinates ministries, localities, and firms toward the composed future before any sanction applies.

**Facet.** Assigned by dominant causal mechanism: the cycle's work is reorganized by the *planning-administrative* mechanism of composed targets tracked through the bureaucratic hierarchy, which is what makes the arrangement plan-governed rather than market-coordinated.

**Boundary.** This note covers the instrument: mandate, drafting apparatus, ratification, targets, mirrored execution, review, and the fifteen composed rounds. It does not decompose the targets' economic substance or the models warranting them; those are referenced, not expanded.

Lineage: the First Five-Year Plan (1953–1957) under Soviet assistance → the Great Leap rupture and the planning retreat → the reform-era recentering from the Sixth Plan (1981–1985) → the 11th round (2006–2010) renamed from directive *jihua* to guiding *guihua* → the 13th (2016–2020) and 14th (2021–2025) rounds binding environmental and innovation targets → the 15th round (2026–2030) composing high-quality development and technological self-reliance.
