# Framework

> **Framework** is the organizing lens that structures how agents perceive a domain and act within it — the guidance coordinator that fixes *what counts as what* (categories, relations, admissible moves), so that strategies select, policies authorize, and plans arrange inside a shared intelligibility instead of inventing one each time.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Framework belongs to the `Framework` social element type (Action category, `Action Guidance` branch, Normative layer)** — a recognition-constituted lens that orients collective doing. Its facet is normative: it warrants and constrains action without itself being the action. Frameworks structure perception and action; strategies select ends and means; policies authorize courses.

It is distinct from `Principle` (the general standard warranting judgment), from `Doctrine` (the authoritative teaching binding a collective's identity), from `Strategy` (the ends-and-means selection), from `Policy` (the course adopted by an authority), and from `Plan` (the concrete arrangement of future actions). A framework structures; a principle warrants; a strategy selects; a policy authorizes; a plan arranges.

### What is this social instance?

> The shared lens by which an agent reads its domain: categories fixing what counts, relations fixing how things hang together, and admissible moves fixing what can be done. Every framework decomposes into the lens it declares, the carrier that maintains it, and the review that revises it. The agentic practice runs on frameworks rather than on fixed strategies: goals stay in focus while actions are reformulated, reflection revises the lens, and the lens — not a frozen selection — carries the continuity.
