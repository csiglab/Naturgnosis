# Rule

> **Rule** is the explicitly codified constraint that prescribes or prohibits conduct — the guidance coordinator that converts a `Principle` into an enforceable prescription, naming who must do what, under which conditions, with what consequence for breach.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Rule belongs to the `Rule` social element type (Action category, `Action Guidance` branch, Normative layer)** — a recognition-constituted constraint that binds conduct. It is distinct from `Principle` (the general standard warranting it), from `Norm / Regulation` (the shared protocol stabilizing interaction, of which a rule is the codified form), and from `Policy` (the course within which it operates).

### What is this social instance?

> The codified prescription: an authority lays it down, subjects are bound by it, and breach carries a consequence. Rules compose into regimes (electoral, procedural, traffic, disclosure); a regime's anatomy is maker, subjects, conditions, and the adjudication that applies it.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one guidance family, decomposed at middle depth — kinds, instances, and anatomy only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a rule kind, a rule instance, or an anatomy element.
>
