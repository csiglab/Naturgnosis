# Roadmap

> **Roadmap** is the sequenced long-range outlook aligning planned moves with milestones — the organization form that binds a horizon to a path: themes declared, milestones fixed, dependencies mapped, and assumptions stated, so that each move inherits its place in the whole.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Roadmap belongs to the `Roadmap` social element type (Action category, `Action Organization` branch, Synontic layer)** — a sequenced outlook existing through the shared commitment of its actors to the same path. It is distinct from `Strategy` (the ends/means selection it serves), from `Plan` (the arrangement of any one of its moves), and from `Schedule` (the temporal allocation of its near moves).

### What is this social instance?

> The pathed horizon: a horizon declared, themes grouping the moves, milestones fixing progress, dependencies ordering them, and assumptions stating what the path takes for granted. Roadmaps decompose into their kinds and their anatomy; the outlook is the instance, the shared path the form.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one organization family, decomposed at middle depth — kinds and anatomy only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a roadmap kind or an anatomy element.
>
