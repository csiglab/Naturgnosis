# Science

> **Science** is the scientific-facet `Activity` family — the coarse bundle of granular social actions through which agents produce, evaluate, and carry warranted knowledge of reality: observation, inquiry, experimentation, synthesis, assessment, communication, and advice, judged by shared standards. It is the umbrella under which [Research Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/research.md) sits as the inquiry core, and the doing-counterpart of the [Research Actor](note.html?n=social/actor/research.md).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Science belongs to the `Activity` social element type (Action category)** — a coarse bundle of granular scientific actions performed as one situated doing. Its layer is **Ontic**: the doing exists extra-mentally. Its facet is scientific.

Readable secondarily as an epistemic pursuit (its product is warranted belief, judged by validation standards); that reading is prose here, not a second root — the epistemic decomposition lives in Epistemic Space and is cross-linked, per the derived-view rule. It is distinct from the actor (the [Research Actor](note.html?n=social/actor/research.md) that performs it), the output (the warranted knowledge or artifact), and its technical instruments (worked in [Technical Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/technical/technical-activity.md)). It is the parent of [Research Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/research.md): research is the inquiry core; science also names the evaluation, communication, and advice that make a warranted result a public, reproducible, usable one.

### What is this social instance?

> The scientific doing-family that scopes systematic knowledge production and its evaluation. Research is its center of gravity — moving a question to a warranted result — but the family also carries the doings that surround primary inquiry: integrating what is already known, assessing others' work, carrying findings beyond the peer community, and marshalling evidence for decision-makers. Each is a bundle of granular scientific actions; recurrence (a research program repeated over cycles) is typed as dynamics, not as the activity itself. Its instruments — apparatus, protocols, computation — are worked in [Technical Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/technical/technical-activity.md), which sits inside this family as instrumentation, not as a separate root.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one scientific activity family, decomposed at middle depth — the inquiry core plus the surrounding scientific doings and the norm that judges them. The inquiry core's internal subtypes (intelligence, framing, basic/applied/developmental inquiry, experimentation, analysis, modeling, validation, dissemination, transfer) are worked in [Research Activity](note.html?n=social/action/activity/research/research.md) and are not duplicated here.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names a scientific activity subtype, a norm, or a role, or when it links an already-decomposed entry.
>
> Identity: Instance Tree Path is the stable identifier of each row; every path is unique.
