# Licensing Regime

> The **Licensing Regime** stabilizes patent, trademark, franchise, and software licensing segments. Source: Produceologia `docs/Production/Industry/Licensing/README.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Licensing Regime belongs to the `Institution` social element type — a stabilized configuration of roles and rules.**

Its layer is **Synontic**: licenses exist through recognized rights and recorded grants. Its facet is normative-economic: the dominant mechanism is rights allocation enabling exchange.

### What is this social instance?

> Licensors grant, licensees practice, and registries record across patent, trademark, franchise, and software segments.
