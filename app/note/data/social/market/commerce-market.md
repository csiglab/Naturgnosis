# Commerce Market

> A **Commerce Market** is the bounded ensemble matching producers to buyers across retail, wholesale, and electronic commerce: firms carry portfolios in, retail units sell through, contracts and prices coordinate. Source: Produceologia `docs/Production/Industry/Commerce/` (read-only import; the originals are untouched).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Commerce Market belongs to the `Social Compound` social element type — the generic composite scoping a market-as-ensemble reading (units, relations, norms, coordinators).**

Readable secondarily as a `Synontic Element` (market-as-coordinator: prices, contracts, listings) per the multi-root forest rule; that reading is prose here, not a second tree. Its layer is **Multi**: the market inhabits Ontic (venues, goods, units) and Synontic (prices, contracts) strata at once. Its facet is economic.

### What is this social instance?

> The market covers the Commerce industries (ECommerce, Retail, Trading): member firms offer portfolios, the MiniMarket retail unit is the smallest studied point of sale (tasks, roles, inventory, POS), and sales contracts plus spot prices coordinate exchange. Extended: retail-as-accessibility operations (role, production, logistics, omnichannel) and the digital marketplace as intermediary model belong to the same ensemble.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one Commerce Market, decomposed at shallow depth — root plus direct member, unit, and coordinator constituents only.
