# Polity

> A **Polity** is the political collective organized around governing authority over a political community, territory, or jurisdiction — the kind of which federations, confederations, and empires are sub-kinds.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Polity belongs to the `Polity` social element type (Kind of `Political Collective`, Kind of `Collective`, `Social Compound`, Structural category)** — a political collective constituted by binding authority. Its layer is **Ontic** where the polity exists extra-mentally (territories administered, centers ruling) and **Synontic** where it exists through shared recognition (the community's image of itself as one people under one authority). Its facet is political: the dominant causal mechanism is authority exercised over a community within a jurisdiction.

It is distinct from `State` (the sovereign, territorially bounded polity claiming supreme authority within its jurisdiction), from `Nation` (the people as an identity community, with or without a polity of its own), and from `Government` (the ruling apparatus directing a polity at a time — governments change, the polity persists). A polity rules; a state claims supremacy; a nation belongs; a government administers.

Readable secondarily as an `Interaction Unit` (a bounded entity capable of interaction with other polities) and as an `Institution` (where the polity just is its stabilized governing rules). Those readings are prose, not second trees; the tree below is typed once.

### What is this social instance?

> A polity fixes *who rules whom, where*: the governing authority exercised, the political community over which it is exercised, and the jurisdiction bounding both. Every polity below, centralized or composite, is one way binding authority becomes entity: through a center commanding peripheries, through units pooling sovereignty, through agreement among retaining members, or through asymmetric rule over many peoples.
