# Finance Actor

> A **Finance Actor** is a bounded collective that allocates capital and settles claims — the finance-functional subtype of [Actor](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/actor.md): central banks and monetary authorities, development and export-import banks, sovereign and pension funds, mortgage agencies, stock and derivatives exchanges, and the international financial institutions. It is the actor counterpart of the exchange and market coordinators: the market clears, the finance actor carries and settles.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Finance Actor belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type (Agents category)** — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules, carrying its own agencies, roles, and practices. It is the finance-functional subtype of Actor, on the same footing as the [Firm](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/firm.md) (economic) and the [Research Actor](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/research.md) (inquiry).

Its layer is **Ontic** where the actor exists extra-mentally (a central bank, an exchange, a fund, with staff, premises, balance sheet, and address) and **Synontic** where it is constituted by recognition (a monetary authority's mandate, an exchange's listing rules, a currency's legal-tender status). Its facet is **economic-financial** — the dominant causal mechanism is the allocation of capital and the settlement of claims, prior to any political or ideational scoping.

It is not an `Agency` (the capacity to form intentions and select action — what the actor acts *with*), not a `Social Role` (the expectation-tag it occupies), and not an `Institution` (the stabilized rules — clearing, listing, supervision — it acts *within*). It is distinct from a [Firm](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/firm.md) (converting capability into exchangeable value rather than allocating capital), from the [Public Organization](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/public/public-organization.md) generic (a finance actor may be public or private), and from the [Market](note.html?n=social/coordinator/market/market.md) it operates in.

### What is this social instance?

> A finance actor fixes *who allocates capital and settles claims, and how*: the charter or licence, the governing board, the capital and balance sheet, the instruments it issues and holds, and the payment, clearing, and settlement arrangements it operates. Its boundary is deliberately loose: the same body may be publicly chartered (a central bank, a development bank) or privately held (an exchange, a fund).

Because the finance family mixes public and private bodies, its instances are split by public authority — **public finance actors** under `public/finance/` (monetary authorities, development and export-import banks, sovereign funds, the international financial institutions) and **private finance actors** under `private/finance/` (exchanges and private funds). This entry is the hub linking both halves; see the support links on the catalog for the two directories.
