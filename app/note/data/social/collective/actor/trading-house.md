# Trading House

> A **Trading House** is the sōgō-shōsha general trader coordinating supply, finance, and information across industries and borders. Source: Produceologia `docs/Production/Industry/Commerce/Trading.md` (read-only import; the original is untouched).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Trading House belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type — a compound Interaction Unit with identity, membership, and rules.**

Its layer is **Ontic**: houses exist with desks, offices, and books. Its facet is economic: the dominant mechanism intermediates exchange across distance.

### What is this social instance?

> Marubeni, Mitsubishi, Mitsui, and Itochu rows: trading desks booking flows, overseas offices carrying local presence, and keiretsu ties stabilizing repeat dealing — a firm form specializing in intermediation itself.
