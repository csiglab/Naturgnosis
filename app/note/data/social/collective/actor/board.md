# Board

> A **Board** is the collegial body through which an organization's mandate-holders decide its direction and control its execution — the governing agency that holds a conferred mandate, bounds its membership, fixes its decision rules, and answers for the organization's conduct.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Board belongs to the `Agency` social element type (Interaction Unit branch, `Organization (→ Part) Agency (→ Part) Governing Board`)** — a structured capacity for intention, selection, and control carried by a bounded collegial body. Its layer is **Synontic** where its authority depends on recognition of its charter or statute, and **Ontic** where its members, meetings, and records exist extra-mentally. Its facet is social-general: the dominant causal mechanism is coordinated collective decision and oversight, prior to any economic, political, or ideational scoping. It is carried by [Organization](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/organization.md), the actor it governs.

It is not an `Organization` (the actor whose direction it decides — though a statutory board may itself be an organization), not a `Committee` (a delegated subset or a body constituted for a defined matter), not the `Executive / Management` (the capacity that carries decisions into execution), and not an `Advisory Board` (a body that advises without governing authority). It is distinct from `Charter` (the instrument that confers its mandate), from `Social Role` (the expectation-tags its members occupy), and from `Institution` (the stabilized governance arrangement it hosts and reproduces).

Readable secondarily as a `Public Organization` kind when the board itself is a statutory body with legal personality — a statutory board, public board, or board of governors. That reading is carried at `Public Organization (→ Kind) Board` and cross-linked to [Public Organization](note.html?n=social/collective/actor/public/public-organization.md); it is not merged into this tree. The note's primary reading is the governing body as an agency of an organization.

### What is this social instance?

> The governing body: a mandate conferred by charter or law, a bounded membership, a chair who convenes and speaks for it, committees that prepare and oversee its decisions, decision rules fixing quorum and majority, fiduciary duties binding members to the organization's purpose, meetings and minutes that record deliberation, and a delegation relation through which the executive carries its decisions into execution. Boards decompose into their kinds and their anatomy; the body is the instance, the governing capacity the form.

### What is a critical conceptual evaluation of this social element instance?

> **The decisive distinction is authority, not label.** A body is a governing board when it holds decision rights, oversight, and fiduciary duty over an organization; it is an advisory board when it only issues opinions. A body called an "advisory board" that votes binding decisions is a governing board in function, and a body called a "board" that only recommends is not.

> **Spanish labels are legal-form dependent.** `Consejo` also names councils that are not boards; `junta directiva` may be the governing board of an association or an executive committee within a larger board; `patronato` is foundation-specific; `consejo rector` is common in cooperatives and public entities; `consejo de administración` is the company-law term. Translation should follow the body's mandate and legal form, not the word alone.

> **One-tier and two-tier systems differ structurally.** Where a supervisory board and an executive board are separate bodies, collapsing both into "board of directors" hides who appoints whom, who monitors, and who commands. The decomposition keeps `Supervisory Board` and `Executive Board` as distinct kinds.

> **A statutory board is read twice.** The governing body that decides and oversees is an agency of an organization; the statutory body that holds legal personality is itself a public organization. The two readings answer different questions and are cross-linked, never merged into one row.
