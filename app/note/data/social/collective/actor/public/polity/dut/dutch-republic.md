# Dutch Republic

> Confederal provinces that out-governed monarchies, the VOC as a joint-stock company with
> sovereign powers, and polder water boards as stakeholder commons.

## Pattern

Federalism plus corporate sovereignty, with local commons governance as an effective form: a
state that delegated war, trade, and water management to separately constituted bodies.

## Sources

States-General resolutions, VOC and WIC archives, Holland's council records, and the water-board
(*waterschap*) records.
