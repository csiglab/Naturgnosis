# Zhou dynasty

> *Fengjian* delegation of territory to kin and allies, held together by ritual (*li*) and
> legitimated by the Mandate of Heaven: a patrimonial order coordinated by ceremony and kinship
> rather than by office.

## Pattern

Coordination through ritual and graded kinship: the earliest Chinese template for binding a wide
realm without a salaried bureaucracy. Its breakdown into the Warring States is the start of the
administrative story the later dynasties complete.

## Sources

Bronze inscriptions, the *Shijing* and *Shujing*, the *Zuo Zhuan*, and the *Shiji*, plus
archaeology. Early layers are partly retrospective, written by later compilers.
