# Ugarit

> A small Levantine port whose palace and private merchant archives expose the seam between royal
> authority and merchant autonomy: taxation, guild organization, and royal-versus-merchant
> disputes.

## Pattern

Early evidence for how a trade coalition negotiates with a court — the boundary between public
authority and private commerce visible in the record rather than inferred.

## Sources

The alphabetic-cuneiform RS archive from Ras Shamra, comprising palace and private economic and
legal texts. Compact, largely published, and directly legible.
