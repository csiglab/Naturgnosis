# Achaemenid Empire

> Satrapy plus the royal inspectors ("eyes and ears of the king") plus the Royal Road relay:
> delegation with verification at continental scale.

## Pattern

Durable imperial administration built on delegated provinces under independent scrutiny — the
earliest large-scale separation of execution from verification.

## Sources

Persian royal inscriptions (Behistun), the Persepolis Fortification Tablets (administrative), the
Aramaic imperial chancery, and Herodotus and Greek observers.
