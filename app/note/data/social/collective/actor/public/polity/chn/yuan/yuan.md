# Yuan dynasty

> Mongol steppe-military organization fused to Chinese bureaucracy: the *jam* postal relay,
> census-based taxation, and a plural administration keeping separate registers for different
> peoples.

## Pattern

Conquest administration layered on an inherited bureaucracy — a mobile military-communications
grid (the *jam*) adapting a sedentary fiscal machine. Compares with the Umayyad and Ottoman cases
of thin rulers over heavy administration.

## Sources

The *Yuan shi*, the *Secret History of the Mongols*, Rashid al-Din, and surviving Mongolian and
Chinese administrative documents.
