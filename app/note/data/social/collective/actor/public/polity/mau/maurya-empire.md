# Maurya Empire

> The Mauryan state paired an Arthashastran bureaucracy and espionage service with Ashoka's
> dhamma — moral infrastructure (hospitals, wells, rest houses for humans and animals) used to
> bind an empire of many peoples.

## Pattern

Capacity and legitimation together: a revenue-centred administration with appointed officials
and provincial governors, reinforced by a public ethical programme that made the empire's order
worth accepting. A reference case for how administrative reach and normative legitimation
compound.

## Sources

The Arthashastra, the Ashokan rock and pillar edicts, Megasthenes' *Indica* (fragmentary),
Puranic king-lists, and archaeology. Administrative data is strong for an ancient empire;
economic detail is partly prescriptive rather than observed.
