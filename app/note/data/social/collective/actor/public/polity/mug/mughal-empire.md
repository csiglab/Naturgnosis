# Mughal Empire

> Mansabdari integrated Persian, Rajput, Maratha, and Deccani elites into one ranked service,
> while *sulh-i-kul* ("universal peace") supplied the legitimation for ruling a majority-alien
> population.

## Pattern

Elite integration through a single graded service (rank held by the individual, not the lineage)
combined with an imperial ideology of tolerance: a template for governing a plural society
without forced assimilation.

## Sources

The *Ain-i-Akbari* (Abul Fazl), Mughal chancery and revenue records, Persian chronicles,
European travellers, and surviving architecture. Rich for personnel and revenue; ideology is read
through court historiography.
