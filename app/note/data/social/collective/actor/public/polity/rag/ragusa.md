# Republic of Ragusa

> Perpetual neutrality and tribute-diplomacy run by a narrow patrician senate, with wealth drawn
> from shipping and insurance rather than territory: a small-state survival pattern built entirely
> on negotiated standing.

## Pattern

Collective agency for small polities: institutionalized neutrality, diplomacy, and commerce
instead of military scale.

## Sources

The Ragusan (Dubrovnik) state archives — among the best-preserved medieval and early-modern
series — plus the Sponza customs records and the civic statutes.
