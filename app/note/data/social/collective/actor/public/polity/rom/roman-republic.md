# Roman Republic

> Cursus honorum (term limits on a ladder), checks among senate, assemblies, and tribunes, and
> the municipia system that turned conquered elites into stakeholders — plus the failure arc of
> Sulla and Caesar.

## Pattern

Institutional checks and elite incorporation, and the canonical case of how such machinery is
captured. Study the working system and its capture together.

## Sources

Livy, Polybius, Cicero, Sallust, and Plutarch; the consular *Fasti*; CIL epigraphy; Pompeian
electoral graffiti for campaign mechanics.
