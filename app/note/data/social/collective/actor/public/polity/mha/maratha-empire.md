# Maratha Empire

> Revenue without administration: chauth (a quarter of assessed revenue) and sardeshmukhi claims
> extracted from neighbours without imposing a standing territorial bureaucracy — a portable,
> low-overhead fiscal pattern with clear limits.

## Pattern

Fiscal extraction as a lightweight agency form: high yield at low institutional cost, but brittle
where sustained administration is required. Read as a companion to the high-capacity Mauryan and
Inca cases.

## Sources

Maratha state papers (peshwa *daf tar* records), Shivaji-era documents, and Mughal, Portuguese,
and British observers. Strong for revenue mechanics; weaker for local administration.
