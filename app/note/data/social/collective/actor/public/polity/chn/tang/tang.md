# Tang dynasty

> The mature Three Departments and Six Ministries, the equal-field and *zu-yong-diao* fiscal
> system, and the *keju* examination opening office to talent: the bureaucratic machine at its
> classical high point.

## Pattern

A codified, departmentally divided central administration with a competitive examination feeding
it. A reference case for rule by trained career officials under a single code of administrative
law.

## Sources

The Tang administrative codes (*Tang liu dian*, *Da Tang liu dian*), the Tang legal code, the
Dunhuang documents, and the Old and New *Book of Tang*.
