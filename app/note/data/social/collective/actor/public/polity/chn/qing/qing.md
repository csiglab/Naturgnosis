# Qing dynasty

> Manchu banners (八旗) as a parallel military-social organization grafted onto the Ming
> bureaucracy, plus a dedicated frontier agency (the Lifan Yuan) for a multi-ethnic empire —
> conquest layered on inherited administration.

## Pattern

Dual administration: a conquest elite organized in hereditary banners alongside a merit-recruited
civil bureaucracy, and a frontier bureaucracy managing a plurality of peoples. Compare with the
Ottoman millet system.

## Sources

The *Qingshi gao*, the *Da Qing Huidian*, Grand Council records, and the Manchu-language archives.
