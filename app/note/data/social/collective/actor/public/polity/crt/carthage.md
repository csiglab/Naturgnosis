# Carthage

> A merchant-oligarchic city-state organizing colonization and long-distance trade through
> daughter cities and treaties rather than territorial rule.

## Pattern

Network-based collective agency: a commercial federation of ports and trading posts without a
territorial empire. Compare with the Hanseatic League and the Venetian colonial network.

## Sources

Caution — internal archives are nearly absent. The merchant-oligarchy and colonization picture
rests on hostile Greek and Roman accounts plus contested archaeology; treat structural claims as
inference, not data.
