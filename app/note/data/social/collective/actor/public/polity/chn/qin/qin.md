# Qin dynasty

> The first centralized empire: commandery-and-county (*junxian*) administration replacing
> hereditary fiefs, Legalist law, and radical standardization — script, weights, axle widths,
> roads — to make a diverse realm governable from one centre.

## Pattern

Centralization as a deliberate administrative technology: uniformity imposed to dissolve
particularism. The template every later dynasty inherited, and a case study in high-capacity
rule built fast.

## Sources

The *Shiji*; the Liye and other Qin bamboo slips (administrative statutes, registers, and
casework); and the archaeology of the terracotta army and Qin infrastructure.
