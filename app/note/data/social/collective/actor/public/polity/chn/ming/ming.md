# Ming dynasty

> Abolition of the chancellorship concentrating authority in the emperor, balanced by the Grand
> Secretariat and a powerful Censorate; *baojia* local control at home and the tributary system
> abroad.

## Pattern

Centralization by removing the prime-ministerial layer, then re-creating coordination through
palace secretariats and an independent censorate. A case of tension between personal rule and
bureaucratic administration.

## Sources

The *Mingshi*, the *Da Ming Hui Dian* (administrative code), the Ming veritable records, and local
gazetteers.
