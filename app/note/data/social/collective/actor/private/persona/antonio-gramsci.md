# Antonio Gramsci


> 

Here's a table summarizing the main ideas of Antonio Gramsci, an influential Italian Marxist philosopher and politician:

| Main Idea/Concept | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Hegemony** | The concept that the ruling class maintains power not just through coercion but by achieving cultural and ideological dominance. |
| **Cultural Hegemony** | The process by which the values and beliefs of the dominant class become the "common sense" values of all, securing consent and stability. |
| **War of Position vs. War of Maneuver** | Distinguishes between strategic, long-term cultural struggle (war of position) and direct, short-term revolutionary action (war of maneuver). |
| **Organic Intellectuals** | Intellectuals that emerge from and articulate the interests of the working class, as opposed to traditional intellectuals aligned with the ruling class. |
| **Historical Bloc** | The alliance of different social classes and groups united by a common ideology or political project. |
| **Passive Revolution** | Social change that occurs without a fundamental transformation of the dominant class's power structures. |
| **Civil Society and State** | Differentiates between civil society (the realm of private organizations and institutions) and the state (political society), emphasizing the role of civil society in maintaining hegemony. |
| **Prison Notebooks** | A collection of essays written by Gramsci during his imprisonment, covering his theories on culture, politics, and society. |

These ideas reflect Gramsci's emphasis on the role of culture, ideology, and intellectuals in the struggle for social and political change.
