# Archimedes of Syracuse


> **Archimedes of Syracuse** was an ancient Greek mathematician, physicist, engineer, inventor, and astronomer whose groundbreaking work in geometry, mechanics, and fluid dynamics laid the foundation for modern science and engineering.
> 

## 🧠 **Mathematical & Scientific Works**

| **Title** | **Subject** | **Key Contributions** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *On the Sphere and Cylinder* | Geometry | Volume & surface area of a sphere; famous ratio (sphere: cylinder = 2:3) |
| *Measurement of a Circle* | Geometry | Approximation of π:  $\frac{223}{71} < \pi < \frac{22}{7}$ |
| *On the Equilibrium of Planes* | Mechanics | Laws of the lever; concept of center of gravity |
| *On Spirals* | Geometry | Properties of the Archimedean spiral |
| *On the Quadrature of the Parabola* | Geometry / Early calculus | Area under a parabola segment using method of exhaustion |
| *On Floating Bodies* | Hydrostatics | Archimedes’ Principle; foundational work in fluid mechanics |
| *The Sand Reckoner* | Astronomy / Arithmetic | Estimation of the universe’s size; early large number system |
| *The Method of Mechanical Theorems* | Mathematics / Mechanics | Mechanical reasoning to derive and prove geometrical theorems |
| *Stomachion (Ostomachion)* | Combinatorics / Recreational math | Dissection puzzle; early exploration of permutations and combinatorics |

## ⚙️ **Engineering Works & Inventions**

| **Invention / Device** | **Field** | **Key Contributions / Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Archimedean Screw** | Mechanical Engineering / Hydraulics | Device to raise water; still used in irrigation and sewage systems |
| **Compound Pulley System** | Mechanics | Mechanical advantage to lift heavy loads easily |
| **Catapults and Siege Engines** | Military Engineering | Designed powerful torsion-based projectile machines to defend Syracuse |
| **Heat Ray (disputed)** | Optical Engineering | Allegedly used mirrors to concentrate sunlight and ignite ships (debated by historians) |
| **Defensive Naval Claws** | Military Engineering | Giant grappling devices ("Claw of Archimedes") to overturn attacking ships |
