# Paul Romer


**Paul Romer** is known for his significant contributions to economic growth theory and innovation. Some of his primary papers include:

1. **"Endogenous Technological Change" (1990)**: In this seminal paper published in the Journal of Political Economy, Romer introduced the concept of endogenous growth theory. He argued that technological progress is not exogenous but rather determined by economic factors such as investment in research and development, human capital accumulation, and knowledge spillovers. This paper laid the foundation for understanding how policies and institutions influence long-term economic growth.
2. **"Increasing Returns and Long-Run Growth" (1986)**: This paper, also published in the Journal of Political Economy, explores the role of increasing returns to scale in driving sustained economic growth. Romer demonstrates how positive feedback loops can lead to autonomous growth dynamics, challenging the traditional assumption of diminishing returns in economic models.
3. **"Growth Based on Increasing Returns Due to Specialization" (1987)**: In this paper, Romer further develops the concept of increasing returns to scale by focusing on the role of specialization and knowledge accumulation. He highlights how economies can benefit from specialization and trade, leading to higher productivity and growth.
4. **"The Origins of Endogenous Growth" (1994)**: This paper, co-authored with Diego Puga, provides an overview of endogenous growth theory's historical and theoretical foundations. Romer and Puga discuss key contributions to the field and highlight the implications of endogenous growth for understanding the dynamics of technological change and economic development.
5. **"Economic Growth" (2007)**: Romer's work on economic growth is also encapsulated in his textbook "Economic Growth," which provides a comprehensive overview of theories, empirical evidence, and policy implications related to economic growth. The book synthesizes Romer's research and other economists' contributions, making it a valuable resource for students and researchers in the field.

These papers and contributions have profoundly impacted the study of economic growth and development, influencing academic research and policy debates. Romer's insights have helped shape our understanding of the drivers of long-term prosperity and the role of innovation in fostering economic progress.
