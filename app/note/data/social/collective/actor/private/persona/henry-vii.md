# Henry VII


> Henry VII of England's industrial policy was primarily focused on strengthening the nation's economy through trade, manufacturing, and agricultural advancements.
>

He implemented several measures to achieve this, including:

| **Policy Area** | **Description** | **Impact** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Promotion of Trade and Commerce** | Secured favorable trade agreements and established the Merchant Adventurers to boost trade. | Increased commercial reach and export of English woolen cloth; strengthened economic ties with other nations. |
| **Support for Domestic Manufacturing** | Encouraged the growth of domestic industries, such as wool, cloth, mining, and shipbuilding. | Reduced dependence on foreign goods; diversified the economy and boosted manufacturing sectors. |
| **Agricultural Improvements** | Implemented policies like the enclosure movement and supported new farming techniques and crops. | Increased agricultural productivity and efficiency; supported population growth. |
| **Regulation and Monopolies** | Granted monopolies and patents to encourage investment and innovation; regulated prices and quality standards. | Promoted innovation and high standards in English products; protected consumers and enhanced product competitiveness. |
| **Infrastructure Development** | Invested in infrastructure improvements, including roads, ports, and ships; expanded the navy. | Facilitated trade and commerce, improved maritime protection, and laid the groundwork for future exploration. |
| **Financial Stability** | Focused on increasing royal revenues through efficient tax collection and crown land management, the Court of the Exchequer was established. | Ensured steady income for economic initiatives; supported financial stability and further economic development. |
