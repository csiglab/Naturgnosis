# Ibn Khaldūn

> ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Khaldūn (1332–1406) was a North African historian and social thinker. His *Muqaddimah* founded a naturalistic study of society and history, treating group solidarity (*ʿaṣabiyya*), dynastic cycles, and the rise and decline of states.
