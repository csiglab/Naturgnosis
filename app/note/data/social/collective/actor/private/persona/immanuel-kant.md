# Immanuel Kant

 : Arthur Schopenhauer (Arthur%20Schopenhauer%2010e598edb7908165925bd3e87c053aab.md)

> 

## Work

| Work | Description | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Universal Natural History and Theory of the Heavens** (*Allgemeine Naturgeschichte und Theorie des Himmels*) | Kant applies Newtonian physics to cosmology and speculates on the formation of the solar system. | 1755 |
| **Observations on the Feeling of the Beautiful and Sublime** (*Beobachtungen über das Gefühl des Schönen und Erhabenen*) | Early work on aesthetics, discussing human emotional responses to beauty and the sublime. | 1764 |
| **Critique of Pure Reason** (*Kritik der reinen Vernunft*) | His seminal work in epistemology and metaphysics; introduces transcendental idealism and the limits of human knowledge. | 1781 (first edition), 1787 (second edition) |
| **Prolegomena to Any Future Metaphysics** (*Prolegomena zu einer jeden künftigen Metaphysik, die als Wissenschaft wird auftreten können*) | A concise introduction and clarification of the *Critique of Pure Reason*, intended for wider readership. | 1783 |
| **Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals** (*Grundlegung zur Metaphysik der Sitten*) | Establishes his moral philosophy, including the categorical imperative as the foundation of ethics. | 1785 |
| **Critique of Practical Reason** (*Kritik der praktischen Vernunft*) | Further develops his moral philosophy, focusing on practical reason and the autonomy of the will. | 1788 |
| **Critique of Judgment** (*Kritik der Urteilskraft*) | Bridges his theoretical and practical philosophy; discusses aesthetics and teleology. | 1790 |
| **Religion within the Bounds of Bare Reason** (*Die Religion innerhalb der Grenzen der bloßen Vernunft*) | Explores the relationship between religion and morality, emphasizing rational faith. | 1793 |
| **Metaphysics of Morals** (*Metaphysik der Sitten*) | Comprehensive treatise on ethics and law, including duties, rights, and political philosophy. | 1797 |

## Contribution

| Contribution | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Metaphysics of the Will** | Schopenhauer posited that the underlying reality of the world is **will**, a blind, striving force manifesting in all phenomena, rather than rational or material structures. |
| **Pessimism** | Argued that life is fundamentally characterized by suffering because desire (will) is never fully satisfied; happiness is the temporary cessation of suffering. |
| **Aesthetics and Art** | Proposed that art, especially music, allows temporary escape from the suffering of the will by offering contemplation detached from desire. |
| **Ethics of Compassion** | Morality arises from **recognizing others’ suffering** and acting out of compassion rather than self-interest. |
| **Influence on Psychology** | Anticipated ideas in psychology regarding the unconscious and the irrational forces underlying human behavior. |
| **Influence on Later Philosophy** | Strongly influenced Nietzsche, Freud, Wagner, and later existentialist and pessimistic thought. |
