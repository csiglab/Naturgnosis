# San Isidoro de Sevilla


> **San Isidoro de Sevilla** was a distinguished scholar, bishop, and Doctor of the Church, renowned for his pivotal role in compiling and preserving vast arrays of knowledge during the early Middle Ages, culminating in his masterwork, the "Etymologiae.”
> 

Isidore of Seville, a scholar and theologian of the early Middle Ages, is best known for his extensive encyclopedic work, "Etymologiae" (or "Origines"). This work is considered one of the earliest encyclopedias in Western civilization and was a crucial reference for medieval scholars.

Principal works by Isidore of Seville include:

1. **Etymologiae (The Etymologies)**: Isidore's most famous and influential work, the "Etymologiae" is an encyclopedia that aimed to compile all available knowledge at the time. It covers many subjects, including theology, grammar, geography, medicine, etc. The work is structured around the etymology of words, through which Isidore sought to understand and explain the world.
2. **De Natura Rerum (On the Nature of Things)**: Before the "Etymologiae," Isidore wrote "De Natura Rerum," a book on astronomy and natural history. It was one of the early Christian treatments of science and sought to reconcile classical knowledge with Christian doctrine.
3. **Historia de Regibus Gothorum, Vandalorum et Suevorum (History of the Kings of the Goths, Vandals, and Suevi)**: This history of the Gothic, Vandal, and Suevian kings is a valuable resource for understanding the early history of these peoples in Iberia.
4. **Chronica Maiora (The Great Chronicle)**: This work is a universal history that begins with creation and runs to Isidore's present day. It incorporates much of the content from his "History of the Kings of the Goths, Vandals, and Suevi."
5. **Differentiarum Libri (Books of Differences)**: This includes two works, "Differentiae Verborum," which distinguishes between synonyms, and "Differentiae Spirituales," which deals with ethical and spiritual concepts.
6. **Sententiae (Sentences)**: This theological treatise is divided into three books and deals with the doctrines of the Trinity, ecclesiology, and Christian ethics.

Isidore's works were highly influential throughout the Middle Ages, serving as a cornerstone for medieval scholarship, education, and culture. His efforts to preserve and synthesize knowledge from various sources make him a significant figure in the intellectual history of the West.
