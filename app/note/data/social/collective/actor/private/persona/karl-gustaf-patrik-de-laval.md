# Karl Gustaf Patrik de Laval


> **Gustaf de Laval (1845–1913):** A pioneering engineer and inventor, de Laval is best known for his work on the steam turbine and developing the centrifugal cream separator. His innovations had a profound impact on the fields of power generation and dairy processing.
