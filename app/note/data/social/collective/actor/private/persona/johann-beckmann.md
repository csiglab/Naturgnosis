# Johann Beckmann

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Johann-Beckmann.png"
     alt="Johann Beckmann"
     width="200"
     align="left"
     style="margin: 0 25px 15px 0;">

**Johann Beckmann** (1739–1811) was a Göttingen professor of economy and the founder of scientific technology — he coined *Technologie* (1772) for the science of trades, wrote its first textbook (1777), founded commodity science (*Warenkunde*) and critical history of technology, and taught cameralist agriculture from workshop practice (students included Humboldt).

His work binds the cameralist tradition to technique: agriculture systematized by natural science, crafts and factories described trade by trade, inventions historicized — productive capability made teachable.

> Beckmann is the point where this corpus's two spaces meet: the cameralist organization of production rendered as *Technologie*.
