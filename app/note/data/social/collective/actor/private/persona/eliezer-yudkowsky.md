# Eliezer Yudkowsky


> **Eliezer Yudkowsky** is a researcher and writer focused on artificial intelligence safety, rationality, and decision theory, best known for his work on AI alignment, his role in founding the **Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI)**, and his writings on rationality in ***Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality*** and ***LessWrong***.
