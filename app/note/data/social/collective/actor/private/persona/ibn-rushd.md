# Ibn Rushd

> Abū l-Walīd Muḥammad ibn Rushd (Averroes, 1126–1198) was an Andalusian philosopher, jurist, and physician. The most influential medieval commentator on Aristotle, he defended the concord of philosophy and religion in *Faṣl al-maqāl* and wrote extensively on Maliki jurisprudence and medicine.
