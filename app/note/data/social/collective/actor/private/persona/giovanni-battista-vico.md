# Giovanni Battista Vico


> Giambattista Vico (1668–1744) was an Italian philosopher, philologist, and historian who sought to establish a “new science” of the human world by reconstructing, from language, myth, law, custom, and institutions, the imaginative and historical processes through which human beings collectively make their worlds—and therefore can come to know them.
> 

> Vico was proposing modern ‘Social Sciences’.
> 

## Work

> **Vico's epistemica:** reconstruction of the intelligible structures of the human historical world.
> 

> **Vico's tecnica:** philology, etymology, historical criticism, comparison, imaginative reconstruction, and synthesis used to produce that intelligibility.
>
