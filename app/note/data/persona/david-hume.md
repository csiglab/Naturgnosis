## Research Domains

### 🧠 **Epistemology & Human Understanding**

- **Core Concern**: How humans come to know anything—our perceptions, beliefs, and ideas.
- **Key Works**:
    - *A Treatise of Human Nature* (1739–40), Book I
    - *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding* (1748)
- **Key Contributions**:
    - Distinguished between **impressions** (vivid perceptions) and **ideas** (faint copies).
    - Critiqued the notion of **causality**—argued that cause-effect is inferred through habit, not reason.
    - Formulated the **problem of induction**: no logical proof that the future will resemble the past.

### 🧭 **Moral Philosophy**

- **Core Concern**: The foundations of morality and ethical behavior.
- **Key Works**:
    - *A Treatise of Human Nature*, Book III
    - *An Enquiry Concerning the Principles of Morals* (1751)
- **Key Contributions**:
    - Morality is based on **sentiment**, not reason.
    - Argued for **moral sense theory**—our approval/disapproval arises from feelings, not logic.
    - Introduced the **is-ought problem**: one cannot logically derive what ought to be from what is.

### ⚖️ **Political Economy & Politics**

- **Core Concern**: Nature of government, commerce, liberty, and economics.
- **Key Works**:
    - *Essays, Moral and Political* (1741–42)
    - *Political Discourses* (1752)
- **Key Contributions**:
    - Advocated for **free markets** and limited government intervention.
    - Critiqued mercantilism; emphasized the role of commerce in stabilizing civil society.
    - Precursor to classical economics; influenced Adam Smith.

### 🏛️ **Religion & Theology**

- **Core Concern**: Nature of religious belief, miracles, and arguments for God's existence.
- **Key Works**:
    - *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding*, Section X (Of Miracles)
    - *The Natural History of Religion* (1757)
    - *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion* (1779, posthumous)
- **Key Contributions**:
    - Argued against the **rational basis of religious belief**.
    - Asserted that reports of **miracles** are unreliable and driven by human credulity.
    - In *Dialogues*, questioned the **design argument**, suggesting the world’s imperfections weaken the analogy to a divine creator.

### 📚 **History & Literature**

- **Core Concern**: British history and philosophical aesthetics.
- **Key Works**:
    - *The History of England* (1754–1762)
    - *Of the Standard of Taste* (1757)
- **Key Contributions**:
    - Produced a **popular historical narrative** that shaped British identity for decades.
    - In aesthetics, explored **subjectivity in taste**, while defending the possibility of shared standards through cultivated judgment.

### 🖋️ **Style & Legacy**

- **Writing Style**: Clear, elegant, and often conversational—distinct from the dense *Treatise*.
- **Legacy**:
    - Central figure in **empiricism** and **skepticism**.
    - Major influence on **Kant**, who said Hume “awoke [him] from [his] dogmatic slumber.”
    - Foundational thinker for **modern cognitive science**, **moral psychology**, and **philosophy of science**.
