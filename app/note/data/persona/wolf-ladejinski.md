# Wolf Ladejinski


- Wolf Ladejinski, born in 1899, Ukrainian Jewish family
- flee to America in 1921 as refugee from Soviets
- realize communists won because they promised the peasants land reform, not because anyone actually wanted communism
- somehow end up one of the chief American economists in the postwar era
- influence Japan, Taiwan, and South Korea to implement land reform against massive resistance from landlords & authoritarian governments
- this helps unlock the fastest sustained economic growth in history (Asian miracle) & conclusively undermines communism's legitimacy in the region
- plausibly become the most impactful 20th century anticommunist, not through war but through understanding and treating the root cause of poverty better than the communists themselves
- get blacklisted in the McCarthy era for allegedly being a communist sympathizer
