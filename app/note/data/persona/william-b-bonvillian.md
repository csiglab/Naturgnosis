# William B. Bonvillian


> 

William B. Bonvillian is an influential figure in the fields of science and technology policy, particularly known for his insights on innovation systems and the role of government in fostering technological advancements. Here are some of the main ideas and themes found in his work:

1. **Advanced Manufacturing**: Bonvillian has focused on the importance of advanced manufacturing in the United States, advocating for policies that support the development and adoption of new manufacturing technologies. He argues that revitalizing manufacturing is crucial for economic growth and maintaining competitiveness.
2. **Innovation System Challenges**: He examines the challenges within the U.S. innovation system, particularly the gaps between research breakthroughs and their commercialization. Bonvillian stresses the need for improved mechanisms to bridge this 'valley of death', ensuring that innovations can move from the lab to the marketplace effectively.
3. **Role of Federal R&D**: Bonvillian has explored the role of federal research and development (R&D) programs in driving technological innovation. He argues for strategic adjustments in federal R&D to better support areas critical to national interests and economic leadership, such as energy and biotechnology.
4. **Educational Reforms for Innovation**: He advocates for educational reforms that better align with the needs of an innovation-based economy. This includes enhancing STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) education and integrating skills that promote creativity and innovation in problem-solving.
5. **Policy for Science and Technology**: Bonvillian’s work also delves into specific policy recommendations for supporting science and technology development. He has contributed to discussions on how policies can be structured to support emerging technologies, how to structure government-industry partnerships, and the importance of innovation in addressing societal challenges like healthcare and climate change.

William B. Bonvillian's contributions are particularly valued for their practical implications, offering guidance on how policies can be crafted and implemented to better support technological innovation and economic growth.
