# Earth Science

> **Earth Science** is the inquiry domain that makes the Earth intelligible: its spheres, the processes that move matter and energy through them, the archives in which its history survives, and the models that predict what it will do next. Worked per [How to decomposed any epistemical instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-artium-epistemicarum-et-operis.md).

> It is a family of disciplines, so the decomposition follows the family tree: each sub-field family holds its disciplines, each discipline its sub-fields, each sub-field its own worked scope. The shared scaffolding — artifacts, observation interfaces, methods, standards, constraints, feedback, gaps, infrastructure, agents — is typed once at the root, because the same instruments and the same standards serve every family.

> The instrument is technical, the formation natural: a seismometer is decomposed technically, and the Earth it observes is not decomposed here at all ([Ambiguity Resolution](note.html?n=meta/ambiguity-resolution.md)). The Earth as a piece of observer-independent reality is a natural instance and belongs under [Philosophia Naturalis et Operis](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What epistemic element type does this epistemic instance belong to?

**Earth Science belongs to the `Epistemic Domain` epistemic element type — a segment of reality that an epistemic practice targets and models.**

The cut is made explicit, since a domain without a boundary is not a domain. What is **in**: the whole planetary body — solid, deep, water, air, ice, life, and the magnetic envelope — together with its history, which is preserved in rock, sediment, ice, and tree ring. What is **out**: the instruments that observe it (technique, decomposed under the technicarum grammar), the industries and agencies that decide what to do with it (social and production), the Earth as a natural instance in its own right (nature), and the planetary bodies other than Earth except where they test an Earth theory (Cosmochemistry and Planetary Geology sit inside, on that ground alone).

Typing follows the philosophia. A **family** — Solid Earth Sciences, Geophysical Sciences, and their siblings — is an `Epistemic Framework Domain`: a disciplinary family grouping frameworks by shared structure and reasoning style. A **discipline** and each of its **sub-fields** is an `Epistemic Domain`: a bounded cut over the segment it targets, valid as a decomposition root in its own right. The shared scaffolding takes the type of the element it is — artifact, interface, process, standard, constraint, feedback, gap, infrastructure, agent.

Secondary readings, kept as prose rather than compromise typing: Earth Science read as a `Domain Concrete Epistemic Artifact Set (DCESA)` is the accumulated map, model, and record set itself; the Earth as a `Segment of Reality` is a natural instance and takes its own root under the naturalis grammar. No row below carries two types.

### What is this epistemic instance?

> A field geologist reading an exposure, a geophysicist inverting a seismic network, a glaciologist on an ice core, an oceanographer off a research vessel, a remote sensing analyst over a satellite swath, a modeller coupling the spheres in a coupled run — all holding claims about the same planet, at scales from a mineral grain to the planetary interior, over intervals from an earthquake to four billion years. The Earth is one object of study; the disciplines are the different cuts and instruments that reach it.

Lineage: mineralogy and mining in the eighteenth century, the first discipline with an instrument and a market → stratigraphy and the depth-time ordering, with Darwin's succession making the ordering evolutionary → seismology and the hidden interior becoming an observable → plate tectonics and radiometric dating making the deep history testable → satellite geodesy, Earth observation, and coupled climate modelling making the whole system a state to predict.

The claims the domain supports, each carrying its scope conditions: a subsurface structure *at stated resolution and inversion assumptions*; an age *for a stated sample, method, and calibration*; a hazard recurrence *at stated completeness and interval*; a climate projection *under stated forcing and model*; a resource estimate *at stated cut-off grade and recovery*.
