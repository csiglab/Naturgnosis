# Profile Templates

> The **Profile Templates** render production systems and industries comparable: a production template (formulation, economics, model, organization) and an industry template (role, production, market, technology, structure, dynamics, context). Source: Produceologia `docs/Breviarium/a-guide-to-profile-production.md` and `docs/Breviarium/a-guide-to-profile-an-industry.md` (read-only import; the originals are untouched).

## Formulation

### What epistemic element type does this epistemic instance belong to?

**Profile Templates belongs to the `Epistemic Blueprint` epistemic element type — encoding formats in which inquiry artifacts are expressed.**

### What is this epistemic instance?

> Two companion blueprints: production profiles cut product space, productivity and functions, chain and market structure, process architecture, inputs, and firm organization; industry profiles cut role, production, market, technology, structure, dynamics, value capture, and regulatory, geopolitical, macro, environmental, and technical context with product-space, technical-profile, chain, diversification, consumption, and firm-space tables.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this epistemic instance?

> Boundary: one blueprint pair, decomposed at shallow depth — the pair plus its two templates. Instance Tree Path holds instances only.
