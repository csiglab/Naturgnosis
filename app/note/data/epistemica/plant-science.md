# Plant Science

> **Plant Science** is the inquiry domain that makes the plant intelligible: what a plant is, how it grows and allocates, how it responds to water, nutrient, temperature, and pest pressure, and how those responses may be predicted, tested, and improved. Source: worked per [How to decomposed any epistemical instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-artium-epistemicarum-et-operis.md); no third-party import, the decomposition is authored here.

> It is the complement of an intervention: [Plant Cultivation](note.html?n=technique/systems/multinode/plant-cultivation-technical-domain-set.md) acts on the plant under a regime, while plant science warrants belief about how the plant behaves under that regime. The boundary runs between the claim and the regime — measurement executed in the field is technical; what the measurement warrants believing is epistemic.

## Formulation

### What epistemic element type does this epistemic instance belong to?

**Plant Science belongs to the `Epistemic Domain` epistemic element type — a segment of reality that an epistemic practice targets and models.**

The domain is bounded explicitly, because plant science is readable at several scales and a domain without a cut is not a domain. What is **in** the cut: the individual crop plant and the assemblages it grows in (soil-plant system, crop community, seed and propagation system), the pest and pathogen populations acting on it, and the relations among them. What is **out**: the engineered hardware of cultivation (irrigation assemblies, sprayers, harvesters — technique), the growing enterprise and its firms and markets (social and production), and the plant as living system at organ, tissue, and cellular level (nature).

The collective of artifacts this domain accumulates is its DCESA — the fits, the assemblies, the trial series, the ontic maps, the ontic questionnaires — and the decomposition below reads that set apart from the target, the interface, and the standards.

Secondary readings, kept as prose rather than compromise typing: plant science is readable as a `Domain Concrete Epistemic Artifact Set (DCESA)` in its own right (the accumulated knowledge layer), and the crop as a piece of reality is readable as a `Segment of Reality`. Each takes its own root in its own decomposition; no row below carries two types.

### What is this epistemic instance?

> A botanist, agronomist, breeder, and pathologist working on the plant population under observation: reading leaf gas exchange, canopy temperature, and canopy imagery; growing randomized blocks and multi-location trials; fitting crop models and dissecting quantitative-genetics artifacts; and accepting, rejecting, or refining claims against replication, prediction error, and field anomaly. The regime may be the plot, the glasshouse, the farm, or the literature — the inquiry is the same, and it is judged by warranted transfer, not by yield.

Lineage: the herbarium and the determinate description → Linnaean classification, microscopy, and germ theory → physiology and Mendelian genetics → population genetics and crop simulation → high-throughput phenotyping, genome-enabled prediction, and machine learning over genotype–phenotype data.

The claims the domain supports, each carrying its scope conditions: a genotype ranking *at stated sites and seasons*; a nutrient limitation diagnosed *from a factorial design over the stated range*; a disease attributed to an isolate *under the stated postulates*; a yield predicted *under a stated climate and management*; a practice adopted *at the stated expected marginal return*.
