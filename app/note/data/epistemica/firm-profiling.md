# Firm Profiling

> **Firm Profiling** is the structured method that renders a firm intelligible: cutting it into microeconomic, institutional, and capabilities models, then recording the cut as comparable profiles fed by firm filings. Source: Produceologia `docs/Firm/README.md` and `docs/Breviarium/a-guide-to-profile-firm.md` (read-only import; the originals are untouched).

## Formulation

### What epistemic element type does this epistemic instance belong to?

**Firm Profiling belongs to the `Epistemic Tool` epistemic element type — a modeling procedure used to construct descriptions of a firm.**

### What is this epistemic instance?

> The procedure applies three cuts — microeconomic (production, cost, incentives), institutional (governance, contracts, structure), capabilities (learning, innovation, routines) — and grounds each cut in observable filings, principally the SEC Form 10-K.
