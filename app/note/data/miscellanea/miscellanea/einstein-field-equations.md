## Equation

$R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}$

Structure:

- Left: The **left-hand side** ($R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu}$) describes **how spacetime itself bends** in response.
    - $R_{\mu\nu}$: **Ricci curvature tensor** — how many volumes shrink/expand due to curvature.
    - $-\frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R$: Corrects the Ricci tensor so that energy conservation (local and global) works out.
    - $\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}$: **Cosmological constant** — acts like an energy of empty space (dark energy effect).
- Right:  The **right-hand side** ($T_{\mu\nu}$) describes **what's in spacetime** — matter, energy, momentum, pressure.
    - $T_{\mu\nu}$: **Stress-energy tensor** — matter, energy, pressure, stress inside spacetime.
    - Constants $G$,  $c$ :  **Stress-energy tensor** — matter, energy, pressure, stress inside spacetime.

Goals:

- The EFE are the **core law** of **General Relativity**.
- They describe how **matter and energy** cause **spacetime to curve**.
- That curvature **tells objects how to move**.
