# Slavoj Žižek


> 

- "The Sublime Object of Ideology" (1989): This foundational work explores the relationship between psychoanalysis and ideology, establishing Žižek as a leading thinker in critical theory.
- "Enjoy Your Symptom!" (1992): A deeper dive into Lacanian psychoanalysis as applied to cultural analysis.
- "Looking Awry" (1992): Examines film and literature through the lens of Lacanian psychoanalysis.
- "The Ticklish Subject" (1999): Discusses the relationship between philosophy and modern political ideologies.
- "The Parallax View" (2006): Focuses on the structural gaps in ideology and critique using a wide-ranging philosophical and cultural framework.
- "Less Than Nothing" (2012): A dense philosophical treatise engaging with Hegel, Lacan, and contemporary ideology.
- "The Courage of Hopelessness" (2017): Reflects on political developments and the crises of capitalism.
