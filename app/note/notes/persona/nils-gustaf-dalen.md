# Nils Gustaf Dalén


> **Gustaf Dalén (1869–1937):** An engineer and inventor, Dalén was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics for inventing the automatic sun valve, a device used in lighthouses. His contributions extended to the fields of optics and gas technology.
