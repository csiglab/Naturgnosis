# Georg Friedrich List


> **Georg Friedrich List** was a **19th-Century** **German** economist and politician known for his contributions to the field of economic theory, particularly his advocacy for national economic development and the role of protectionist policies.
> 

Here are some of the main ideas associated with **Georg Friedrich List**:

| **Main Idea** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **National System of Political Economy** | List's influential work where he argued for economic policies that prioritize developing domestic industries and infrastructure to achieve long-term national prosperity. |
| **Infant Industry Argument** | Advocated for protecting emerging domestic industries from foreign competition through tariffs and trade barriers until they become competitive. |
| **Economic Nationalism** | Emphasized the nation's collective interests over individual economic actors, promoting industrialization, infrastructure development, and technological progress for national benefit. |
| **Critique of Free Trade** | Criticized the classical liberal doctrine of free trade, arguing that it favored industrialized nations and perpetuated underdevelopment in developing economies. |
| **Railway Development** | Highlighted the importance of infrastructure, particularly railways, in promoting economic development and national integration, advocating for government investment in these areas. |
| **Pragmatic Approach** | Recognized the need for flexibility in economic policy-making, tailoring policies to each country's specific circumstances and developmental stage rather than adhering to rigid principles. |
