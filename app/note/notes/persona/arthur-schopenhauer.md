## Work

| Work | Description | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On the Fourfold Root of the Principle of Sufficient Reason** (*Über die vierfache Wurzel des Satzes vom zureichenden Grunde*) | Schopenhauer’s doctoral dissertation; establishes the principle of sufficient reason as a foundation for knowledge and introduces his early metaphysical ideas. | 1813 |
| **The World as Will and Representation** (*Die Welt als Wille und Vorstellung*) | His magnum opus; develops his metaphysics of the will, arguing that the will is the underlying reality behind appearances. | 1818 (Vol. 1), 1844 (Vol. 2) |
| **On Vision and Colors** (*Ueber das Sehn und die Farben*) | Treats the perception of color, combining physiological observation with philosophical reflection. | 1816 |
| **On the Will in Nature** (*Über den Willen in der Natur*) | Explores the manifestations of the will in natural phenomena and scientific principles. | 1836 |
| **Parerga and Paralipomena** (*Parerga und Paralipomena*) | A collection of essays and reflections covering ethics, aesthetics, religion, and practical philosophy; made Schopenhauer widely popular. | 1851 |
| **On the Basis of Morality** (*Über die Grundlage der Moral*) | Presents his ethical theory, emphasizing compassion as the basis of morality. | 1840 |
| **Studies in Pessimism** (English compilation) | Collection of essays highlighting Schopenhauer’s pessimistic philosophy and reflections on human suffering. | 1890 (posthumous in English) |
