# Rent Seeking (Rentismo) (Rentier)

: 33
 : 33

Rent-seeking is growing one’s existing wealth without creating new wealth by manipulating the social or political environment. Rent-seeking activities have adverse effects on the rest of society. They result in reduced economic efficiency through misallocating resources, reduced wealth creation, lost government revenue, heightened income inequality, and potential national decline. The word “rent” refers not to lease payment but to Adam Smith’s division of incomes into profit, wage, and rent. The term's origin refers to gaining control of land or other natural resources.
