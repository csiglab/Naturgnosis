> The notion of **dimension** in its deepest philosophical sense captures the very structure of reality, knowledge, and perception. It is not merely a measure of space but an ontological principle that governs how things relate, unfold, and exist.
> 

> How are `points` in a space to specify?  How points in a space are related? 
What is the `address` of this point in space? What are the degrees of freedom of this **point** in space?

Meta dimensions  (😂) :

- Dimension as an Ontological Scaffold. 😎
- **Dimension** is not just a number—it is a measure of the **degrees of freedom** available within a system.
- …

- Euclidean Dimension (Spatial Dimension) – The intuitive notion of dimension as the number of independent directions in a space (e.g., a point is 0D, a line is 1D, a plane is 2D, etc.).
- Vector Space Dimension (Hamel Dimension) – The number of basis elements in a vector space, indicating its degrees of freedom.
- Krull Dimension (in Ring Theory) – The maximal number of strict inclusions in a chain of prime ideals of a commutative ring.
- Metric Dimension (Kolmogorov Dimension, Information Dimension) – Related to complexity and randomness, indicating the minimal information needed to describe a point in a space.
- Lyapunov Dimension – Related to chaos, measuring how volumes in phase space evolve under a system's dynamics.
