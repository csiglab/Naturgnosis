> **Telos** ([/ˈtɛ.lɒs/](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/telos#English); [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): τέλος, [translit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Greek) *télos*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) "end, 'purpose', or 'goal'") is a term used by philosopher [Aristotle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle) to refer to the [final cause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_causes#Final_cause) of a natural organ or entity, or of human art.
