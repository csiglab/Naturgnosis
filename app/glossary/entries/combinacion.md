**Combination**: Means of Combination (Putting Things Together), “Combination” and “composition” refer to ways of building complex objects or systems from simpler parts.

```
"Composition" refers to a design pattern where objects are combined to form more complex objects or systems. In composition, an object is composed of other objects, which are its parts or components. These parts are often encapsulated within the larger object, and they work together to provide the desired behavior. Composition is often used to achieve code reusability, flexibility, and modularity.

"Combination" is a broader term that can refer to any way of combining or putting together multiple elements or objects to create something new. Combination can involve a wide variety of techniques, including composition, inheritance, delegation, and more. Combination can be used to achieve a variety of goals, such as creating new functionality, optimizing performance, or simplifying complex systems.

In general, composition is a specific technique for building complex objects from smaller parts, while combination is a broader term that encompasses many different ways of combining elements or objects. However, composition is often used as a key technique in many different types of combination.
```
