> Masa Crítica (Critical Mass) refers to the minimum quantity of a resource, population, or interacting agents required to sustain a self-perpetuating process, transition, or chain reaction, beyond which the system undergoes a qualitative shift in state or behavior due to **positive feedback mechanisms** and **non-linear dynamics**.
