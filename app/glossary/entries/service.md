# Service

: 7

> A service is an intangible commodity that involves providing labor or expertise in exchange for payment, but does not result in the ownership of a physical product. Services are actions or activities that one person performs for another, and they can range widely in nature. Examples include healthcare, education, banking, consulting, transportation, and entertainment.
> 

> Services are distinct from goods in that they are intangible, perishable, and their production and consumption occur simultaneously. This means they cannot be stored or transported over time or space in the same way physical goods can. The service sector's performance is often a key indicator of an economy's health, reflecting consumer confidence, spending capacity, and employment levels.
