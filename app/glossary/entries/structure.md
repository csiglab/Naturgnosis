> **Structure** is the degree of organization, differentiation, and relational patterning present in a complex situation, whereby entities are arranged into hierarchical or networked configurations that enable multi-level analysis and interpretation.
> 

> **Note:** The function used to assign a **level of structure** to a given situation may be subjective. Suppose we have two functions that **measure structure**, $ef_1$ and $ef_2$. They must preserve the ordering of evaluations; that is, if $ef_1(a) > ef_1(b)$, then $ef_2(a) > ef_2(b)$.
> 

> **Note:** Consequently, the measurement of structure entails a degree of subjectivity, since different cognitive agents may perceive and evaluate structural organization in different ways.
>
