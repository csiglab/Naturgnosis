Here’s a table summarizing the roles in a research group and their responsibilities:

| **Role** | **Responsibilities** |
| --- | --- |
| **Principal Investigator (PI)** | Leads research projects, defines scope and methodology, oversees the team, ensures research standards. |
| **Research Director** | Manages overall research strategy and operations, sets priorities, allocates resources, and supervises projects. |
| **Research Manager** | Coordinates day-to-day research activities, manages timelines and budgets, ensures efficient project execution. |
| **Lead Researcher** | Manages specific projects or sub-projects, develops plans, supervises activities, analyzes data, writes reports. |
| **Research Analysts** | Conducts data analysis, processes data, performs statistical analyses, interprets research findings. |
| **Data Scientist** | Manages and analyzes complex data sets, develops data models, applies machine learning algorithms. |
| **Research Associate** | Supports research activities, conducts literature reviews, assists in data analysis, contributes to reports. |
| **Policy Analyst** | Analyzes policy implications, assesses impacts, provides policy recommendations based on research. |
| **Field Researcher** | Conducts fieldwork, executes surveys and interviews, reports findings from primary data collection. |
| **Research Coordinator** | Manages research logistics, coordinates meetings, schedules, communications, and ensures organized activities. |
| **Administrative Support** | Provides administrative assistance, manages documentation, schedules appointments, handles correspondence. |
| **Technical Specialist** | Maintains research equipment, develops technical tools, manages technical aspects of research. |
| **Communications Specialist** | Manages dissemination of research findings, drafts press releases, manages social media, organizes events. |
| **Ethics Officer** | Ensures ethical compliance, reviews research proposals, manages ethical review processes. |
| **Graduate Students/Interns** | Provides additional support, gains research experience, assists with tasks such as data collection and literature reviews. |
