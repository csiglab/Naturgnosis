> The Council of State in the Netherlands (**Raad van State**) is a key advisory body to the government and also serves as the highest general administrative court.
> 

| **Function** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Advisory Role on Legislation and Governance** | Advises the Dutch government and Parliament on proposed legislation and governance matters. All bills, draft orders, and international treaties must be submitted for advice before passing. |
| **Judicial Function (Administrative Court)** | Serves as the highest administrative court in the Netherlands, handling disputes between citizens or organizations and the government, especially in areas like zoning, environmental law, and immigration. |
| **Guardian of the Constitution** | Ensures that legislation and government actions comply with the Constitution, offering advice on the constitutionality of laws and regulations. |
| **Administrative Supervision** | Supervises the legality and proper functioning of administrative decisions and actions by governmental bodies, particularly in its judicial role. |
| **Advisory Role in International Law** | Provides advice on international treaties and agreements, ensuring alignment with Dutch law and international obligations. |
| **Advice on Administrative Law Reform** | Advises the government on reforms and changes to administrative law, shaping the legal framework for government institutions. |
| **Crisis and Emergency Legislation** | Advises on special legislation and governmental decrees during crises or emergencies, ensuring legal soundness and appropriateness of such measures. |
