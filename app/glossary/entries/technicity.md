# Technicity

> Technical Objects: Simondon explored the relationship between human beings and technical objects. He argued that technical objects, such as tools and machines, are not separate from human existence but are an integral part of it. He saw technology as an extension of human capabilities and a means of individuation.
> 

> Technicity: Simondon discussed the notion of “technicity,” which refers to the intrinsic technical dimension present in all living beings. He saw technology not only as external tools but as an internal aspect of life itself.
>
