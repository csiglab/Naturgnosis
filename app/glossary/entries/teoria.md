# Teoría

english: theory

> A theory is a structured system of concepts, assumptions, and relations that explains and constrains a domain of phenomena.
> 

> A theory is a generative explanatory framework that reduces apparent complexity by positing structured mechanisms or laws.
> 

> La palabra *teoría* proviene del griego **θεωρία (*theōría*)**, que significaba originalmente *contemplación*, *observación* o *mirar atentamente*.
> 

> Note: The Term theory is used in others domains like maths - to mean a coherent set of ideas - and results - no in the same tamdem as natural sciences.
>
