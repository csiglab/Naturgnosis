# Notion

> *An intuitive or general idea about something, often informal, vague, or provisional, which captures a basic understanding but may lack precise structure or formal definition.*
> 

## Etymology

The **etymology of “notion”** traces back to Latin and Old French:

- **Latin:** *notio* – meaning “a becoming known, idea, concept, perception,” from *nosco* (“to know, to learn”).
- **Old French:** *notion* – adopted from Latin, retaining the meaning “idea, conception, understanding.”
- **English:** Entered in Middle English (late 14th–15th century) as *notioun*, meaning “a general idea or understanding of something.”
