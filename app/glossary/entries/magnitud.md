# Magnitud

: 8
 : 8

> Del lat. *magnitūdo* 'grandeza'.
> 

> La cualidad de ser grande, y con el tiempo evolucionó en las lenguas romances a significar tamaño, **cantidad, medida o intensidad** de algo.
> 

> In **physics and mathematics**, magnitude refers to the size or amount of a quantity, regardless of direction (in contrast to vectors).
> 

> **Value**: often used for both, but less formal.
> 

> **Measure**: very close, especially in math and statistics (e.g., measure theory), but less intuitive in everyday usage.
> 

> **"Quantitative characterization"** = describing or measuring something in terms of quantity — i.e., **assigning numbers** or **measurable traits** to it.
>
