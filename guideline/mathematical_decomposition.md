# Mathematical decomposition: content/vehicle sorting

> How to type the constituents of a mathematical instance — numbers, definitions,
> axioms, rules, notations, theories — inside the epistemic grammar
> (`app/note/data/meta/philosophia-artium-epistemicarum-et-operis.md`), so that
> formal disciplines decompose the same way twice. First full application: the
> Arithmetic case study in that note.

## Formulation

### What is the sorting principle?

> **Content stays in Construction; vehicle goes to Constitutive Substrate — except that in formal disciplines the content collapses into the vehicle.** Outside mathematics, what the inquiry is *about*
> types on the `Epistemic Construction` branch while what makes the notation *run*
> types as substrate. Inside mathematics, number-concepts and axiom systems constitute
> the very system they describe (the table's own `Theory` instances are empirical —
> evolutionary, economic), so they sort vehicle-side. The test is always constitution,
> never mere formality.

### How is a candidate constituent sorted?

> 1. **Ask which question it answers.** "What is it?" and "what does it claim?"
>    sort content-side; "what must hold for the signs to work?" sorts vehicle-side.
> 2. **Check the substrate's own instance list first.** The `Constitutive Substrate`
>    row names its members outright — definition, axiom, syntax, grammar, formation
>    rule, convention, semantic rule, transformation rule, interpretation rule,
>    schema. A definitional or rule-like constituent that matches this list goes to
>    substrate even when it also reads as a free-standing claim. Descend into the
   substrate's children (`Convention`, `Formation`,
   `Foundation`, …) wherever one fits; only otherwise type direct to substrate.
> 3. **Split duals across homes, never merge.** A glyph is an element, its content a
>    substrate position: Zero-as-`0` is covered by the digit/character element, while
>    Zero-as-empty-count sorts substrate as constituted content. No row ever carries
>    both types; record the secondary reading as `readable as …` prose (see the ambiguity reading).
> 4. **Theories stay theories — for empirical domains.** An axiomatized account of an
>    empirical target types as `Theory` even though axioms appear inside it. In formal
>    disciplines the exception applies (see principle): the axiom system types as
>    substrate, with exemplar axiom systems realizing it.

### What does the worked mapping look like?

> From the Arithmetic case study (paths abbreviated to the typing tail):

> | Constituent | Types as | Why |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Decimal Convention | `Convention` | "Decimal notation" is verbatim-listed there |
> | Numeral Formation Rule, Successor | `Formation` | formation rules generating well-formed numerals and the number series |
> | Zero | `Constitutive Substrate` (direct) | empty count with no fitting child |
> | Addition Definition, First-Order Arithmetic (+ Peano/Presburger exemplars) | `Foundation` | foundational definition; axiom system with exemplar axiom systems realizing it |
> | Number, Place Value | `Constitutive Substrate` (direct) | constituted content with no fitting child |
> | Decimal Digit | `Representation Element` | combinable character, Zero-as-glyph included |
> | Forty-Two | `Representation Element` | numeral token denoting forty-two |
> | Product Equation | `Representation Element` | equation expression, checkable artifact |
> | Decimal Place Encoding | `Interpretation` | denotation mapping from number to numeral |

### Where do lookalikes go?

> - **Place Value vs Decimal Convention** is the canonical near-collision, resolved substrate-side: positional magnitude-content and notational convention sit as siblings, distinguished as what is carried versus how it is carried. When a candidate echoes an existing row, split within the home rather than duplicating either row.
> - **Discharged expectations stay predictions.** A sum stated before reckoning types as `Prediction` even after it comes out true; truth does not promote it to substrate.
> - **Oral and gestural constituents** (chants, finger counts) sort by role, not medium: drill chants transmit (`Epistemic Transmission`), finger tallies register (`Observable`) or tool (`Epistemic Tool`), never substrate — nothing about them makes signs work.

### What must not be done?

> Do not file empirical content as substrate merely because it is formalized; in formal
> disciplines, number-concepts and axiom systems sort substrate because they constitute
> the system — constitution, not formality, is the test; do not file running rules
> (definitions, formation rules, conventions) as concepts merely because they can be
> contemplated; do not merge a dual (glyph/content, rule/claim) into one row — split it
> and cross-link the readings.
