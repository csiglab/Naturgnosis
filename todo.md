    - [ ]  For **long-term tasks, context is essential**. However, can small errors accumulate over time and eventually lead to failure? Furthermore, even with a very large—or even infinite—context window, what other failure modes can arise?
    
    | Issue | Description | Root Cause | Consequence | Infinite Context Solves It? |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Error Accumulation** | Small reasoning errors propagate through subsequent steps. | Autoregressive dependency on previous outputs. | Drift from the correct solution; eventual collapse. | ❌ No |
    | **Context Contamination** | Context contains outdated, incorrect, tentative, or contradictory information. | No explicit belief revision or truth maintenance. | Confusion about the current state of the world or task. | ❌ No (can make it worse) |
    | **Attention Dilution** | Relevant information becomes difficult to identify within a very large context. | Limited attention allocation over many tokens. | Important facts may be ignored or weakly attended. | ❌ No |
    | **Planning Drift** | Local decisions gradually diverge from the original objective. | Weak maintenance of long-term goals and intentions. | The agent optimizes subgoals instead of the overall task. | ❌ No |
    | **World Model Inconsistency** | Internal beliefs become mutually inconsistent over time. | No principled mechanism for belief revision. | Contradictory reasoning and unstable decisions. | ❌ No |
    | **State Explosion** | The number of entities and relationships grows rapidly. | Increasing combinatorial complexity. | Harder reasoning and planning despite complete memory. | ❌ No |
    | **Limited Computation** | Each inference step has a bounded computational budget. | Fixed inference algorithm and finite compute. | Cannot exhaustively reason about complex tasks. | ❌ No |
    | **Long-Horizon Credit Assignment** | Difficult to determine which earlier decision caused a later failure. | Delayed consequences of actions. | Slow or ineffective correction of mistakes. | ❌ No |
    | **Passive Memory** | Context is stored but not actively organized or managed. | Lack of memory management mechanisms. | Redundant, noisy, and inefficient working memory. | ❌ No |
    | **Fixed Reasoning Capability** | The reasoning algorithm itself does not improve with more context. | Fixed model parameters and architecture during inference. | Better memory but unchanged reasoning quality. | ❌ No |
